Entry points: PF-31: `@Versioned()` and the global `IfMatchVersion` interceptor (`http/if-match.ts`): a top-level `version` in an answer is its ETag; If-Match fills a versioned route's body `version` (stale → the service's 409); If-Match on a route without one, "*", a list or a differing body version → 422. Marked: `PATCH offices/:id`, `PUT firm/profile`, front office `PATCH people/:personId` (now takes an optional `version`, 409 STALE with `current`).
