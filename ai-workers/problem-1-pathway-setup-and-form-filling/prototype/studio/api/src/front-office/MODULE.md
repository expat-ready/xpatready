# Module card: api-front-office (P-615)
The firm's front office: public booking and retainer agreements, with their files. Read this before editing these files; update it in the same PR when an entry point, invariant, table or test below changes (or say `Card unchanged: <reason>` in the PR body).

**Purpose.** `sales/` leads (FO-2): the one arrival path, the public web-form door, the lead inbox and its changes, the open-hours wait (`open-minutes.ts`); `booking/` the booking service, its public door, slots, calendar files (`ics.ts`) and self-service tokens; `retainers/` retainer and consultation agreements from templates (`docx.ts`, `placeholders.ts`); `render/gotenberg.ts` Word to PDF (the real DocumentPdfPort on Gotenberg's LibreOffice route, PF-Fp; not wired yet, FO-5p part 2 wires `pdfFromEnv`); `signing/` (FO-6) the vendor-neutral SigningPort, its fake and its contract (`tests/check_fo6_signing_port.ts`; no route yet: the API is the row's next part, the vendor adapter FO-6v); `files.ts` the real FilesPort with short-lived checked download links; `*-seed.ts` Sample seeds.
**Owner area.** front-office module rows FO-* (Unconfirmed: no lane map in code).
**Entry points.** `/api/public/forms/:token` (open read) and `POST …/submissions` (the public door, FO-2); `/api/front-office`: `GET leads?view=unassigned|mine|all|closed[&source=<stored source>…]` (FO-4sf: the lead's stored source, never the door; repeated, never split; narrowed inside the visibility; a source the person has no visible lead with → 400 naming theirs), `GET leads/sources` (the stored sources of the leads the person may see), `GET leads/assignees`, `GET leads/:id` (key show), `POST leads` (create), `POST leads/:id/take|close|reopen` (edit), `POST leads/:id/assign` (assign); FO-2b (`sales/corporate/`): `GET companies/match?name=`, `POST leads/:id/engage/corporate`, `POST prospects/:id/change-type`, `POST companies/:id/contacts`, `POST company-contacts/:id/role|end` (key edit), `GET pipelines/:type/board`, `GET corporate/prospects/:id`, `GET companies/:id/contacts` (key show); `GET retainer-templates`, `POST retainer-templates/:id/versions`, `POST prospects/:id/retainers`, `POST retainers/:id/send`, `POST retainers/:id/signed-offline`, `POST retainers/:id/download-link`, `GET files/:token`; the booking page's public controller under `/api/public` (FO-3, FO-3pa): `GET firms/:firmKey/book` (the list), `GET firms/:firmKey/book/:slug/slots`, `POST …/bookings`, `GET bookings/:token`, `GET bookings/:token/slots`, `GET bookings/:token/ics`, `POST bookings/:token/reschedule`, `POST bookings/:token/cancel` (optional `reason`); the staff booking routes (`booking/staff.controller.ts`, FO-3sr) `POST bookings`, `POST bookings/:id/reschedule`, `POST bookings/:id/cancel` (keys create, edit), `GET bookings`, `GET bookings/attendance-due` (key list), `GET bookings/:id`, `POST bookings/:id/attendance` (any staff: the host's own, the service checks the rest and answers 404). FO-3sa: create and reschedule take any time; a time with warnings answers 409 `WARNINGS` and saves nothing until the body carries `bookAnyway: true` and `acceptedWarnings` (the codes shown); create takes `hostStaffId` (an active host of the type). `files.ts` `LocalFiles` (FilesPort): `put`, `get`, `exists`, and `delete` (PF-Fd: the caller asked `LegalHoldPort.isHeld` and committed the record first; removes the bytes inside the firm's folder only, keeps a tombstone in the `.meta` file, so the key is retired and a later `put` of it is refused).

**Invariants (set by).**
- Every public read is marked open on the read guard (OpenRead, P-348 (4)); the door's own protections live in the service.
- Every public miss (unknown firm, hidden, retired or unknown type, bad or voided link) is the one identical not-found; public answers carry no ids (FO-3pa, BR-2).
- A type's `allowReschedule` and `allowCancel` are read inside the change's transaction, after the row lock; a booker's cancel reason is kept on the booking (`cancel_reason`, at most 500, only when CANCELLED), never in the audit entry (FO-3pa, Lead B rev 16.30).
- A self-service token is `<selector>.<verifier>`: looked up by id, verifier checked (FO-3, BR-10).
- A web-form post makes nothing on a refusal; its answer never says whether the person is known; a stamp not ours asks for a reload (FO-2, AG-2-49).
- A lead change is seen again under the row's lock; one audit line (entity named literally), the outbox row last (FO-2).
- Leads follow the one own-versus-all rule (`sales/common.ts` `visibilityOf`, as retainers): all = `agent/prospect:view.all` and "everyone sees all" on, else own; no role override. Take on an unseen lead is the GET 404, except a lead taken from Unassigned within 10 minutes (409 naming who, no id) (FO-2v). Take's race answer reads the latest take/assign audit line (AG-2-86 rule 3); no owner-set column. `assign` never widens what a person sees: with the setting off an `assign` holder reassigns only Unassigned leads and their own (AG-2-13c rule 1 keeps the action in every mode; AG-2-64 is the one home of visibility; Lead B rev 16.56).
- A company match is offered, never linked (409 `COMPANY_MATCH` until a pick or `newCompany`); a company keeps one live PRIMARY; a contact change ends the link and starts a new one (history kept); Change type keeps the prospect row and is refused once WON or while a retainer is out (FO-2b, AG-2-76, -77, -79).
- Word to PDF never blocks a send: `toPdf` never throws, a failure is "no PDF" and the `.docx` is kept (`withPdf`); over 10 MB or not a zip is refused before sending; only `/forms/libreoffice/convert` is called, no redirect followed; CI and the demo use `fakePdf` (PF-Fp, gate entry 17).
- Rate limits are rolling windows per key, sized for about 100 firms in one process (P-562).
- Every file belongs to one firm and lives under the firm's own folder (FO-5, contracts §0.3).
- Platform rules apply: `firmScope` on every table, platform guard on every staff route (P-559).
- The database walls these tables (PF-12): a public door finds its firm through its named bypass use (`fo.sales.web-form`, `fo.booking.public-page`, `fo.booking.self-service`), then runs as that firm (`enterFirm`).
- The attendance question (30 min after the end) is shown, not pushed: its job mails no one; the booking is the due item until a person answers (AG-4-39 rule 2, FO-3m). The answer's body is `{ outcome }` only.
- The database's no-overlap rule skips only a booking a staff member confirmed with Book anyway over an overlap (`overlapConfirmedByUserId`, FO-3sa, AG-4-63 rule 4); the public door never sets it, every writer checks overlaps under the host locks, and a move clears it unless the move is itself confirmed.

**Do not.**
- Serve a file without a checked short-lived link, or from another firm's folder.
- Open a staff route without the platform guard.
- Let the signing provider email anyone: SigningPort takes no address and no vendor-email option (FO-6, P-563).
- Store a signing code or link token in clear, or any email address, in the signing tables (codes and tokens as sha256 only, FO-6).
- Store a token's verifier unhashed (it is kept as a SHA-256, `hashVerifier` in tokens.ts).
