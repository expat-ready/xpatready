# Front-office sales (`api/src/front-office/sales/`): entry points and rules

Detail of the module card `../MODULE.md` (kept under its 40 lines, #2114), moved here word for word on 18 Nov (FO-2 part 3); read both before editing these files and update this file in the same PR.

## Entry points (under `/api/front-office`)
- Leads (FO-2, FO-4sf): `GET leads?view=unassigned|mine|all|closed[&source=<stored source>…]` (FO-4sf: the lead's stored source, never the door; repeated, never split; narrowed inside the visibility; a source the person has no visible lead with → 400 naming theirs), `GET leads/sources` (the stored sources of the leads the person may see), `GET leads/assignees`, `GET leads/:id` (key show), `POST leads` (create), `POST leads/:id/take|close|reopen` (edit), `POST leads/:id/assign` (assign).
- Leads, FO-2 part 3: `GET leads` also takes `&owner=<staff id>` and `&from=&to=` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, the arrival day in the firm's zone, both days included); both narrow inside the visibility like `&source=`; another firm's or an unknown staff id → 400 `UNKNOWN_OWNER`.
- FO-2b (`corporate/`): `GET companies/match?name=`, `POST leads/:id/engage/corporate`, `POST prospects/:id/change-type`, `POST companies/:id/contacts`, `POST company-contacts/:id/role|end` (key edit), `GET pipelines/:type/board`, `GET corporate/prospects/:id`, `GET companies/:id/contacts` (key show); the board (`GET pipelines/:type/board`) carries `totals` per currency over the open stages and per stage (`board-totals.ts`, FO-2 part 3, R1.31).
- Prospects, FO-2 part 3 (`prospects.ts`): `POST leads/:id/engage` (Individual; key edit), `GET prospects?type=&stage=&owner=&from=&to=` (default Individual; Lost only with `stage=LOST`, the Lost filter) and `GET prospects/:id` (key show; @OwnOrAll, swept by PF-6b), `POST prospects/:id/move { stageId, cause: PERSON, version }` (key edit), `POST prospects/:id/lost { reasonId, note? }` (key lose), `POST prospects/:id/convert` and `POST prospects/:id/undo-convert { reason }` (key convert).

## Rules
- A lead change is seen again under the row's lock; one audit line (entity named literally), the outbox row last (FO-2).
- Leads follow the one own-versus-all rule (`sales/common.ts` `visibilityOf`, as retainers): all = `agent/prospect:view.all` and "everyone sees all" on, else own; no role override. Take on an unseen lead is the GET 404, except a lead taken from Unassigned within 10 minutes (409 naming who, no id) (FO-2v). Take's race answer reads the latest take/assign audit line (AG-2-86 rule 3); no owner-set column. `assign` never widens what a person sees: with the setting off an `assign` holder reassigns only Unassigned leads and their own (AG-2-13c rule 1 keeps the action in every mode; AG-2-64 is the one home of visibility; Lead B rev 16.56).
- A company match is offered, never linked (409 `COMPANY_MATCH` until a pick or `newCompany`); a company keeps one live PRIMARY; a contact change ends the link and starts a new one (history kept); Change type keeps the prospect row and is refused once WON or while a retainer is out (FO-2b, AG-2-76, -77, -79).
- Prospects (FO-2 part 3): the same own-versus-all rule as FO-2b (all, else own). Engage makes a prospect per request, and a second one says so (`again`). A move is a person's (cause PERSON) between open stages with the version read (its latest stage move's number; stale → 409 `STALE` naming who moved it); never into WON (422 with AG-2-26 rule 3's words), LOST (Mark lost) or NURTURE (Park, AG-2-45, not built), never out of WON or LOST. Mark lost needs a reason from the firm's prospect list (400 otherwise; a merge's reason is never chosen). Won comes only from FO-6's signature or Convert; Undo convert only for a Convert with no signed retainer, back to the stage before WON, the reason kept as an INTERNAL note. Undo checks the retainers only: no case names a prospect or client on `main` yet, and an invoice (`money_invoice`, by person or company) cannot be raised on `main` and has no port the front office may ask; the case and invoice checks join when FO-8 and the client-money port link them (question on the FO-2 part 3 PR). The `prospect.won` payload is contracts §2.6's, with the person's answers and consent ids; `stage_moved`, `lost` and `unconverted` carry the move's `cause`. Board totals are summed per currency, never across.
- The real ArrivalPort (FO-2 part 3b, `arrival-port.ts`, bound in `booking/runtime-ports.ts`; QA-511, R1.28): a public booking runs `arrive()` (door BOOKING, key `booking:<booking id>`, source "Booking page", summary "arrived by a booking") in the booking's transaction; idempotent by key. With the tick empty no consent row is written; ticked, one row through ConsentPort with the firm's wording (EMAIL marketing, source BOOKING_FORM). A booker whose email is one person of the firm with an open (ACTIVE) prospect makes no person or lead (receipt ON_OPEN_PROSPECT); that person's only open prospect moves forward to Consult with the cause BOOKING (audited, `prospect.stage_moved` in the outbox), never backward; several move none. A staff booking (`bookingForPerson`) makes no lead and moves the same way; another firm's person is null. A firm with no sales setup row is seeded once in the booking's transaction (FO-1a's seed, as FO-1p). The contract `ports/contracts/arrival-port.contract.ts` runs on `fakeArrival` and the adapter.
