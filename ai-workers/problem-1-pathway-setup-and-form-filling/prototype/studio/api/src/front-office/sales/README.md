# Front-office sales (`api/src/front-office/sales/`): entry points and rules

Detail of the module card `../MODULE.md` (kept under its 40 lines, #2114), moved here word for word on 18 Nov (FO-2 part 3); read both before editing these files and update this file in the same PR.

## Entry points (under `/api/front-office`)
- Leads (FO-2, FO-4sf): `GET leads?view=unassigned|mine|all|closed[&source=<stored source>…]` (FO-4sf: the lead's stored source, never the door; repeated, never split; narrowed inside the visibility; a source the person has no visible lead with → 400 naming theirs), `GET leads/sources` (the stored sources of the leads the person may see), `GET leads/assignees`, `GET leads/:id` (key show), `POST leads` (create), `POST leads/:id/take|close|reopen` (edit), `POST leads/:id/assign` (assign).
- Leads, FO-2 part 3: `GET leads` also takes `&owner=<staff id>` and `&from=&to=` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, the arrival day in the firm's zone, both days included); both narrow inside the visibility like `&source=`; another firm's or an unknown staff id → 400 `UNKNOWN_OWNER`.
- FO-2b (`corporate/`): `GET companies/match?name=`, `POST leads/:id/engage/corporate`, `POST prospects/:id/change-type`, `POST companies/:id/contacts`, `POST company-contacts/:id/role|end` (key edit), `GET pipelines/:type/board`, `GET corporate/prospects/:id`, `GET companies/:id/contacts` (key show); the board (`GET pipelines/:type/board[?source=]`, R1.30: cards, counts and totals follow the source) carries `totals` per currency over the open stages and per stage (`board-totals.ts`, FO-2 part 3, R1.31).
- Prospects, FO-2 part 3 (`prospects.ts`): `POST leads/:id/engage` (Individual; key edit), `GET prospects?type=&stage=&owner=&source=&from=&to=` (default Individual; `source` R1.30: the lead's, repeatable, checked as on Leads by `source-filter.ts`; Lost only with `stage=LOST`, the Lost filter) and `GET prospects/:id` (key show; @OwnOrAll, swept by PF-6b), `POST prospects/:id/move { stageId, cause: PERSON, version }` (key edit), `POST prospects/:id/lost { reasonId, note? }` (key lose), `POST prospects/:id/convert` and `POST prospects/:id/undo-convert { reason }` (key convert).
- FO-4 part 2a (two reads the screens need): each stage of `GET pipelines/:type/board` carries its `id` (what `move` names), and `GET prospects/lost-reasons` (key show) lists `{ id, key, label }` of the reasons Mark lost accepts.
- Calls, notes and the timeline, FO-2 part 4 (`calls.ts`, `timeline.ts`): `POST leads/:id/calls` and `POST prospects/:id/calls { outcome, direction?, when?, seconds?, notes? }` (key log-call; the seven AG-2-37a outcomes, `when` defaults to now), `POST leads/:id/notes` and `POST prospects/:id/notes { text }` (key edit; INTERNAL only), `GET leads/:id/timeline` and `GET prospects/:id/timeline` (key show; @OwnOrAll, swept by PF-6b) → `{ lines: TimelineLine[], more }`.

- The embed script, FO-4b (`embed.ts`; under `/api/public`): `GET embed/form.js` serves `web/src/modules/front-office/embed/form-embed.js` as it is (nosniff, any site may load it); `OPTIONS forms/:token/submissions` answers an allowed site's pre-flight (else 403 `SITE_NOT_ALLOWED`, an unknown form 404). The form's read and send (`public.ts`) add `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` for the form's allowed sites and our own page only (`corsFor`), never credentials; the send's site check is that same function.

- Web-form settings, FO-4w (`web-form-settings.ts`; key `agent/prospect:configure-form`, Owner and Admin): `GET web-forms` (the firm's forms: state, allowed sites, public token, hosted page, version) and `PUT web-forms/:id/allowed-sites { sites, version }` (replaces the list: each an http or https site with no path, query, fragment or sign-in part, stored as `siteOf` gives it, duplicates merged, at most 20; a refused entry is named `sites.<n>` and nothing is saved; another person's newer save → 409 `STALE`; the row is locked, and the row and one audit line `fo.web_form.sites_changed` (entity `fo_web_form`) are written in one transaction). R1.26 QA-605: `PUT web-forms/:id/enabled { enabled, version }` switches the form on or off (same key, same version check → 409 `STALE`; the same state again changes nothing; one audit line `fo.web_form.enabled_changed` with `before` and `after` in full). QA-617: switching on a form with no consent wording links the firm's current wording (its newest published EMAIL marketing wording, the booking page's) in the same transaction, shown in the line's before/after; a firm with none → 422 `NO_CONSENT_WORDING` on `enabled`, the form stays off.

## Rules
- A lead change is seen again under the row's lock; one audit line (entity named literally), the outbox row last (FO-2).
- Leads follow the one own-versus-all rule (`sales/common.ts` `visibilityOf`, as retainers): all = `agent/prospect:view.all` and "everyone sees all" on, else own; no role override. Take on an unseen lead is the GET 404, except a lead taken from Unassigned within 10 minutes (409 naming who, no id) (FO-2v). Take's race answer reads the latest take/assign audit line (AG-2-86 rule 3); no owner-set column. `assign` never widens what a person sees: with the setting off an `assign` holder reassigns only Unassigned leads and their own (AG-2-13c rule 1 keeps the action in every mode; AG-2-64 is the one home of visibility; Lead B rev 16.56).
- A company match is offered, never linked (409 `COMPANY_MATCH` until a pick or `newCompany`); a company keeps one live PRIMARY; a contact change ends the link and starts a new one (history kept); Change type keeps the prospect row and is refused once WON or while a retainer is out (FO-2b, AG-2-76, -77, -79).
- Prospects (FO-2 part 3): the same own-versus-all rule as FO-2b (all, else own). Engage makes a prospect per request, and a second one says so (`again`). A move is a person's (cause PERSON) between open stages with the version read (its latest stage move's number; stale → 409 `STALE` naming who moved it); never into WON (422 with AG-2-26 rule 3's words), LOST (Mark lost) or NURTURE (Park, AG-2-45, not built), never out of WON or LOST. Mark lost needs a reason from the firm's prospect list (400 otherwise; a merge's reason is never chosen). Won comes only from FO-6's signature or Convert; Undo convert only for a Convert with no signed retainer, back to the stage before WON, the reason kept as an INTERNAL note. Undo checks the retainers only: no case names a prospect or client on `main` yet, and an invoice (`money_invoice`, by person or company) cannot be raised on `main` and has no port the front office may ask; the case and invoice checks join when FO-8 and the client-money port link them (question on the FO-2 part 3 PR). The `prospect.won` payload is contracts §2.6's, with the person's answers and consent ids; `stage_moved`, `lost` and `unconverted` carry the move's `cause`. Board totals are summed per currency, never across.
- The real ArrivalPort (FO-2 part 3b, `arrival-port.ts`, bound in `booking/runtime-ports.ts`; QA-511, R1.28): a public booking runs `arrive()` (door BOOKING, key `booking:<booking id>`, source "Booking page", summary "arrived by a booking") in the booking's transaction; idempotent by key. With the tick empty no consent row is written; ticked, one row through ConsentPort with the firm's wording (EMAIL marketing, source BOOKING_FORM). A booker whose email is one person of the firm with an open (ACTIVE) prospect makes no person or lead (receipt ON_OPEN_PROSPECT); that person's only open prospect moves forward to Consult with the cause BOOKING (audited, `prospect.stage_moved` in the outbox), never backward; several move none. A staff booking (`bookingForPerson`) makes no lead and moves the same way; another firm's person is null. A firm with no sales setup row is seeded once in the booking's transaction (FO-1a's seed, as FO-1p). The contract `ports/contracts/arrival-port.contract.ts` runs on `fakeArrival` and the adapter.
- Calls and notes (FO-2 part 4): the record seen again under its locks (404 as the read): a prospect first, then every lead of the person in id order. A logged call is the first response (time, `CALL`, who) of every OPEN lead of that person that has none and had arrived by the call's time, whichever screen it was logged from (AG-2-37a rule 5, BR-6); an ENGAGED or CLOSED lead keeps what it has; the answer names only the leads the caller may see (`firstResponseFor`) and counts the rest (`firstResponseOthers`); the audit line keeps every id. `when` is ISO 8601 with its offset, never in the future, never before the lead arrived. A call never moves a stage (AG-2-26 rule 7). A note is INTERNAL; its words stay in `fo_note`, the audit line carries ids and the length only.
- The timeline (FO-2 part 4) is one read in the one shape `TimelineLine` (`ports/events.ts`; review D10): audit lines of the record, outbox events that have no audit line of their own name, calls by when they happened, notes; a call's or note's own audit line is not shown twice; oldest first, at most 500 lines (`more` says so).
