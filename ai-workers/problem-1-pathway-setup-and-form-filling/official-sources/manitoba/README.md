## Skilled Worker in Manitoba (SWM) — eligibility
Page: https://immigratemanitoba.com/mpnp/skilled-worker/swm/eligibility/ ("Eligibility – Skilled Worker in Manitoba"), read 17 Nov 2026.

| Rule (in plain words) | Short quote from the page |
|---|---|
| Needs ongoing Manitoba employment and a valid work permit | "you possess a valid work permit" |
| A full-time, long-term job offer after at least 6 months' continuous full-time work with that Manitoba company | "six months or more of continuous full-time employment with that company" |
| Graduates of a post-secondary program in another province: at least 1 year with the company | "at least one year or more of continuous full-time employment" |
| Self-employment, unauthorized work and work while studying full time (e.g. co-op) do not count toward the period | "will not be included when calculating the period" |
| Not eligible: self-employed, business owners, owner-operators, independent contractors to the supporting business, work-permit-exempt workers | "are not eligible to apply under SWM" |
| Employer: established business able to offer full-time, long-term work; incorporated or registered; place of business in Manitoba | "an established production capability, plant or place of business in Manitoba" |
| Applicant holds every qualification, licence or certification the job needs | "including training/education and any required licence or certification" |
| Enough official-language proficiency for the job and to settle | "sufficient official language proficiency" |
| Tie to Manitoba through work stronger than to any other province | "stronger than ties you may have to another province" |
| A Settlement Plan showing intent to live, work and settle in Manitoba | "in a Settlement Plan your intention" |

**Who cannot apply to the MPNP (any stream):** refugee claimants or people in a federal appeal or removal process; live-in caregivers living in Canada; temporary foreign workers working and living in another province; spouses of Canadian citizens or permanent residents; people refused by the MPNP in the last six months who cannot address the reason; people with an active application in another provincial or federal program ("an Express Entry profile is not considered an immigration application"). Only one active MPNP application per applicant and spouse.
