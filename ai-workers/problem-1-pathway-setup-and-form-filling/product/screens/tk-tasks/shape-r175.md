## Journey j_75 (the walk at 1440 px; `tests/journeys/j_75.ts`)
A Case Officer signs in → opens a lead → **Add a task** "Call about the job offer", due today, assigned to themself → **Tasks** in the menu → it is in **My Tasks** under Today → **Home** shows it in "My Tasks · 1 due today or overdue" → **Done** → it leaves Today. A Case Manager adds an unassigned task on a prospect → sees the badge "1 unassigned task" → **Unassigned tasks** → the Case Officer **Takes it** (the Case Manager's second Take it reads "<name> already has this task") → **Unassigned cases** → **Take it** on a case with no officer. The Owner opens a colleague with 3 open tasks → **Deactivate** → "Give their open tasks to" the Case Manager → **Reassign and deactivate** → toast "3 open tasks moved" → the Case Manager's My Tasks shows them. Next capability's first step: R1.76 notes on the same lead (the page is open).
