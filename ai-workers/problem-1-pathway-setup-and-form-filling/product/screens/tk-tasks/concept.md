## 1. Job and journey
**Job:** every staff member sees the work that needs them, in due order, and can add, take, hand on and finish it where they are. Personas: case officer (My Tasks, the record panel), case manager or Owner (Unassigned, bulk, the hand-over). Requirement text (trace tool): **AG-13-5** (My Tasks), **AG-13-62** (Unassigned list, badge), **AG-13-63** (hand-over), **AG-13-67** (visibility, "restricted"), **AG-11-115** (the Home's "My Tasks" section). Journey (map row 75): a task is added on a lead → it shows in its owner's My Tasks and on Home when due → done; a task with no owner → Unassigned → Take it; a colleague leaves → their open tasks move in one step.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 My Tasks = mine, both ways** (AG-13-5): assigned to me (default) or by me (a chip); never filtered by the Individual/Corporate view; each task on a client or case shows its type label, a lead's none (AG17-D6).
* **BR-2 Due order in the task's own zone** (AG-13-5 "derived flag", PF-29c, BR-3): groups Overdue · Today · This week · Later · No due date, worked out by the server in each task's office zone; every due time names its zone ("Toronto time"). "Overdue" in words and red, never colour alone.
* **BR-3 Restricted parents stay nameless** (AG-13-67 (c)): a task on a record I cannot open shows its title and "Restricted", no name, number, status or link; I can still mark it Done. Search, counts and the Home section follow the same rule.
* **BR-4 Unassigned tasks** (AG-13-62): open, no assignee, for holders of `agent/task:view.all` or `agent/task:assign`, each item also passing its host's visibility; **lead tasks are not listed** (they live on Unassigned leads, AG-2-13c; question 2). Take it (myself) and Assign… (`:assign`); a row says why it is unassigned when the API knows (handed over, its rule's assignee inactive).
* **BR-5 First Take it wins** (PF-29b): the second person sees "<name> already has this task" and the row leaves the list; the same for cases: "<name> already has this case" (CS-T1, AG-7-106).
* **BR-6 Bulk, up to 200, all or nothing** (AG-13-6, PF-29c): Assign to, Priority, Due date, Mark done on ticked rows; one id refused refuses all, said in words; holders of `agent/task:bulk`.
* **BR-7 Unassigned cases** (batch 5, CS-T1): the third tab, for `agent/case:edit` or `agent/case:assign`; nearest government deadline first; Take it needs `:edit` (self), Assign… `:assign`.
* **BR-8 The Tasks panel on a record** (row): on the lead, prospect, client and case pages; open first in due order, done and cancelled folded; Add a task (title 1-200, optional due date and time in the record's office zone, assignee from active staff of this firm or Unassigned, priority Low/Medium/High/Urgent default Medium, optional category from the suggestions "Client request", "Call", optional details ≤ 5,000), Done, Reopen, Cancel with a reason (required), Edit. Only holders of `agent/task:create` see Add a task.
* **BR-9 Home: "My Tasks"** (AG-11-115 section 2): mine, Open or In progress, due on or before the end of today in my office zone; after "Needs you"; not drawn when empty; the heading's count links to Tasks › My Tasks; unassigned work is a count on the Tasks menu entry, never a Home section (AG-11-115, AG-13-62 badge).
* **BR-10 Hand-over on deactivation** (AG-13-63, PF-29e): shown only when the person has open tasks: the count and the first 10, one new owner from active staff of this firm whose role holds `agent/task:view.own` (suspended and inactive people never offered), then "Reassign and deactivate" or "Deactivate, leave unassigned"; done and cancelled tasks keep their owner; one transaction (the server's).
* **BR-11 Plain names** (AG17-D61, PRODUCT §4): "Tasks", "My Tasks", "Unassigned tasks", "Unassigned cases"; never "My Work" or "My Day".

## 4. Layout (EXPERIENCE §3: Library list for the Tasks page, minus facets (chips instead); a section on the record and Home pages; Dialog for Add a task; PF-6's dialog for the hand-over)
* **S1 Tasks page** `/tasks` in PlatformFrame; menu entry "Tasks" (key `agent/task:view.own`) with a count of my tasks due today or overdue, plus the unassigned count for those who may reassign (question 3). One row of tabs with counts; a tab the person may not use is not drawn. Table: Task (title, category) · On (host link, or "Restricted"; type label) · Due (window word, date, time, zone) · Priority · By · Done and ⋯ (Edit, Reassign…, Cancel…).
* **S2 Unassigned tasks:** a checkbox column, the bulk bar when rows are ticked, Take it and Assign… per row, "Why unassigned" when known. **S3 Unassigned cases:** Case · Client · Stage · Next deadline · Type · Take it / Assign….
* **S4 Tasks panel** (one component on four pages): heading with Add a task; rows with Done first; ⋯ for Edit, Reopen, Cancel…. Add a task is a Dialog with the fields of BR-8 and "Add task".
* **S5 Home section** "My Tasks" with "3 due today or overdue · see all". **S6 hand-over step** inside PF-6's Deactivate dialog. **S7 phone:** the table becomes cards; tabs scroll in their own row; no sideways page scroll.
* **States (every list):** loading (skeleton rows) · empty ("Nothing due. Tasks you are given or add show here." / "No unassigned tasks." / "No case is waiting for an officer.") · error (Notice + Try again) · lost race (warn Notice naming the person) · bulk refused (Notice naming the reason, nothing changed) · a long title wraps · read-only (no `:create`: no Add a task) · conflict (409 "This task changed. Reload to see it.").

## 4b. Best in class (vendor behaviour as remembered, not re-read for this concept: Unverified)
Asana's My Tasks (due-date groups, "assigned by me"), Todoist (Today and Overdue first, natural due entry), Clio (tasks on a matter, a firm task list, bulk reassign), Zendesk (an unassigned queue with "Take it"). **Copy:** due groups, Take it, bulk bar. **Beat them:** the zone named on every due time, "Restricted" instead of a leak or a silent gap, the lost race named, the hand-over inside deactivation so nothing is orphaned. Effort saved: Add a task pre-fills the record and its zone; Take it is one click.

## 7. Questions for the tech lead (recommendation first)
1. **The Home section's name.** The row says "Due today or overdue"; AG-11-115 names the section "My Tasks" (AG17-D61). **Recommend** heading "My Tasks" with "3 due today or overdue" as its count line.
2. **Lead tasks in Unassigned.** AG-13-62 leaves unassigned lead tasks out (they show on Unassigned leads, AG-2-13c); PF-29c's `…/unassigned` does not say so. **Recommend** a line in PF-29c's brief: `context_type = LEAD` excluded; the screen relies on it.
3. **Counts.** The tab counts, the Home count line and the Tasks menu badge (AG-13-62) need totals; PF-29c's lists are keyset pages with no total. **Recommend** PF-29c returns `total` (capped "200+") for mine, unassigned and home, and CS-T1 for its queue.
