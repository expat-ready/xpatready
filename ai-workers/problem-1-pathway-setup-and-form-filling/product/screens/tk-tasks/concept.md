## 1. Job and journey
**Job:** every staff member sees the work that needs them, in due order, and can add, read, take, hand on and finish it where they are. Personas: case officer (My Tasks, the record panel), case manager or Owner (Unassigned, bulk, the hand-over). Requirement text (trace tool): **AG-13-5** (My Tasks), **AG-13-62** (Unassigned list, badge), **AG-13-63** (hand-over), **AG-13-67** (visibility, "restricted"), **AG-11-115** (the Home's "My Tasks" section). Journey (map row 75): a task is added on a lead → it shows in its owner's My Tasks and on Home when due → done; a task with no owner → Unassigned → Take it; a colleague leaves → their open tasks move in one step.

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 My Tasks = mine, both ways** (AG-13-5): assigned to me (default) or by me; never filtered by the Individual/Corporate view; a client or case task shows its type label, a lead's and a Restricted one's none (AG17-D6, AG-13-67).
* **BR-2 One "today", my zone** (AG-11-115 rule 2; r2, M4): the server decides each task's window (Overdue · Today · This week (to the end of Sunday) · Later · No due date) at the end of today **in my office zone** (AG-13-5 says "firm TZ", PF-29c and this row say "the item's office zone": a conflict, question 3), the same function for My Tasks and Home; the list shows times in my zone with one footer "Times in Toronto time", and names a task's own zone only when it differs ("15:30 Vancouver time"). Bulk "Due date…" keeps each task's zone and sets the date in it. "Overdue" in words and red, never colour alone.
* **BR-3 Restricted parents stay nameless** (AG-13-67 (c)): title and "Restricted" only: no name, number, status, type or link; the assignee can still read the task (its details) and mark it Done. Counts and the Home section follow the same rule.
* **BR-4 Unassigned tasks** (AG-13-62): open, no assignee, for `agent/task:view.all` or `:assign` (AG-13-62's "or"; PF-29c's text says "+": question 3), each passing its host's visibility; **lead tasks are not listed** (Unassigned leads, AG-2-13c). Take it (myself) and Assign… (`:assign`). No "why unassigned" column (r2, M1: no record holds it).
* **BR-5 First Take it wins** (PF-29b): the loser reads "<name> already has this task" and the row leaves the list; cases: "<name> already has this case" (CS-T1, AG-7-106).
* **BR-6 Bulk, up to 200, all or nothing** (AG-13-6, PF-29c): Assign to, Priority, Due date, Mark done on ticked rows, for `agent/task:bulk`; one refused id refuses all, said in words.
* **BR-7 Unassigned cases** (batch 5, CS-T1): the third tab, for `agent/case:edit` or `:assign`; nearest government deadline first; Take it (`:edit`, self), Assign… (`:assign`); its rows show the stage (task rows do not: no host carries it; r2, m5).
* **BR-8 Actions follow keys** (AG-13 §6, §10, AG-13-67 (c); r2, M7): Add a task needs `:create`; Edit, Cancel… and Reopen need `:edit`; Done needs being the assignee or `:edit`; Assign… and Reassign… need `:assign`; **Take it needs `:assign`, or `view.all` with `:edit`** (seeded: Owner, Admin, Case Manager, Case Officer; not Billing / Finance or Read-only, SA-06 §10 rows `agent/task:*`); a person without them sees a muted "You can view these tasks" (Read-only sees the Unassigned tab, through `view.all`, but no Take it). The server enforces each (PF-29b).
* **BR-9 The Tasks panel on a record** (row): lead, prospect, client and case pages; open first in due order, done and cancelled folded; the prospect page lists every task on its `fo_person` host (lead-time ones included, context shown); Add a task (title 1-200, optional due date and time in the **record's** office zone, named, assignee from active staff of this firm or Unassigned, priority Low/Medium/High/Urgent default Medium, optional category with suggestions "Client request", "Call", optional details ≤ 5,000).
* **BR-10 A task can be read** (AG-13-67 (c) "on the task itself"; r2, M6): a title opens the task's side panel: details, On (or "Restricted"), due, priority, assignee, created by and when, and the actions BR-8 allows.
* **BR-11 Home: "My Tasks"** (AG-11-115 section 2): mine, Open or In progress, due by the end of today in my zone (BR-2); after "Needs you"; not drawn when empty; government-deadline items first (that tier stays empty until letter tasks arrive, AG-10-71), then urgent or overdue (oldest due first), then by due time; at most 20 with the total in words (as PF-18h); "see all" opens My Tasks with the filter "Due today or overdue" on, so the numbers match.
* **BR-12 One badge, one meaning** (AG-13-62 "for anyone who can reassign"; r2, M5; r2.1): the Tasks menu entry's count is unassigned work this person can reassign: tasks for `agent/task:assign` holders (Owner, Admin, Case Manager) plus cases for `agent/case:assign` holders, tooltip "2 unassigned tasks · 1 unassigned case"; nobody else sees a count (not `view.all`, which all six starter roles hold, SA-6-24). It is the "home-screen badge" (the menu sits on Home). Question 2.
* **BR-13 Hand-over on deactivation** (AG-13-63, PF-29e; r2, M2; the row's "inside PF-6's dialog" becomes a dialog of its own, and AG-13-63's "Reassign now / Skip" is worded as below: question 3): the Deactivate dialog reads the person's open tasks first; with none it is today's dialog; with some it shows the count, the first 10 and one owner picker (active staff of this firm whose role holds `agent/task:view.own`; never the leaver, suspended or inactive people) and "Reassign and deactivate" or "Deactivate and leave them unassigned"; the choice rides in the deactivate request (and its step-up re-send) and applies in the deactivation transaction, tasks opened meanwhile included; the toast states the number moved, from the server.
* **BR-14 Plain names** (AG17-D61, PRODUCT §4): "Tasks", "My Tasks", "Unassigned tasks", "Unassigned cases"; never "My Work" or "My Day".

## 4b. Best in class (vendor behaviour as remembered, not re-read for this concept: Unverified)
Asana's My Tasks (due groups, "assigned by me", a task pane), Todoist (Today and Overdue first), Clio (tasks on a matter, a firm list, bulk reassign), Zendesk (an unassigned queue with "Take it"). **Copy:** due groups, the task pane, Take it, the bulk bar. **Beat them:** one "today" for list and Home, zones named, "Restricted" instead of a leak, the lost race named, hand-over inside deactivation.
