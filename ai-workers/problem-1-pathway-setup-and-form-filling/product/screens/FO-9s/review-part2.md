## Issues, ranked
1. **Must (Major): focus goes to `<body>` when the Keep separate dialog closes (Cancel, Escape or confirm) and after Undo merge.** `people-to-merge-page.tsx:125-127`: the `Dialog` is opened without a Radix trigger and gets no `returnFocus`. `:104`: the Undo button is replaced by the pill or the reason. Measured by keyboard: after Escape, after Cancel by keyboard, and after Undo by Enter, `document.activeElement` is BODY. A keyboard user has to tab again from the page header. This breaks WCAG 2.4.3 (level A) and EXPERIENCE §2.12 [M]. Fix: pass `returnFocus` to the row's Keep separate button on cancel; after a success, send focus to the next row's Keep separate (or the To review tab when the list is empty); after Undo, focus the row's result. Add a fail test: the check's R4 and R7 call `.focus()` again after Cancel (`check_fo9s_review_browser.ts:155`), which hides this bug.

Fix: ignore clicks while `keep.isPending`, or use a ref guard. The server already makes it safe (409, nothing changed).
3. **Should: at 375 px the table is unreadable and the actions are off screen with no cue that the table scrolls.** Names and lead numbers break inside words ("Kimm/wvs/mnid", "L-0002/8"); Merge… and Keep separate sit past the right edge (committed `screenshots/fo9s2-01-to-review-375.png`). `:34` uses `[overflow-wrap:anywhere]`; `:73` passes no `headClass`.
