## What held
* **Roles (API):** READ_ONLY gets 403 on list, merges and keep-separate. A Case Officer gets 403 on undo (`agent/prospect:merge`). With "each person sees their own", a Case Officer gets 404 on a pair with someone else's side, and so does a second Case Officer on the first one's pair. A bad `status` gets 422; a non-uuid id gets 404.
* **BR-6:** with "each person sees their own", the Case Officer sees only the pair of their own leads (1 of 15), and their Merged tab is empty. The Owner does not see the Case Manager's merge. No Merge… and no Undo merge without the merge key. The READ_ONLY member has no menu item and sees the no-key sentence word for word.
* **BR-7:** the case pair's Merge… is off, with the sentence word for word (the builder's check passes; nothing contradicted it).
* **Races (API):** two concurrent Keep separate requests give 200 and 409 "This pair was already decided." Two concurrent Undos give 200 and 409 "This merge was already undone." Undo racing Keep separate on a merged pair gives 200 and 409, and the pair ends OPEN once.
* **Stale Keep separate:** acting on a pair another session decided shows the server's 409 in a toast, closes the dialog and refreshes the list and the menu count (6→5).
* **401 mid-action:** the session is ended on the server while the dialog is open, then Keep separate is confirmed. The toast reads "This session was ended. Sign in again.", the sign-in card appears in place, and the pair stays OPEN. A network failure gives "Could not keep … separate. Nothing changed.", and the pair stays OPEN.
* **Reload, Back, address:** reload with the dialog open changes nothing; `?tab=merged` survives a reload; an unknown `tab` falls back to To review.
* **Counts:** the menu count equals the To review tab and the API's `openCount` (6, 12, 500). The menu updates after the user's own Keep separate and Undo.
* **Text:** Arabic and CJK names render and wrap; 100-character names wrap with no overflow at 1024 px; the tooltip trigger is reachable by Tab. The Tab order is menu, tabs, then per row Match then Keep separate.
* **Widths:** 1024 px, and 200 % zoom (720 and 512 CSS px at scale factor 2), on all three tabs: no sideways scroll and nothing outside the viewport.
* **Budgets (§9):** list API 40-62 ms at 500 rows. First rows render in 810 ms with 500 rows (under 1 s). Tab switch at 500 rows takes 288-554 ms, which is above the 200 ms budget, but the budget applies to Sample data and scale is the team's. No request per row. No page errors in any run.
* **Not applicable to this screen:** uploads and planted-instruction PDFs, rules/conditions, and create-time duplicates (part 1 / FO-9a).
