## 1. Job and journey
* **Job:** a staff member with the key records IRCC's decision on each submitted filing (and corrects one), withdraws a case before any decision, closes a decided case with a reason, and reopens a closed case with a reason; the case header then says how and when the case ended.
* **Persona:** firm staff signed in through PF-4's local sign-in. Keys from PF-3's seed (`migrations/20261114015002_pf3_platform_tables`, lines 586-598): `agent/case:record-decision` = Owner, Admin, Case manager; `agent/case:reopen` = Owner, Case manager; `agent/case:show` = every staff role. Sample firm: the Representative (Owner + Case manager) holds all three; the Case officer only show.
* **Journey (qa's R1.77 walk, by screens):** Record the submission (C8.2) → **Record a decision** per filing → **Close the case** with a reason → header "Closed" → **Reopen** with a reason → open again at the stage it closed from. Second case: **Withdraw the case** with a date and a reason → header "Withdrawn". Activity and SA-01B ids: not known.

## 3. Business rules shown (the API decides; the screen shows the API's answer)
* **BR-1** (AG-7-33 r1, BR-10): decision, withdraw and close need `agent/case:record-decision`, never `agent/case:edit`.
* **BR-2** (AG-7-33 r2): a decision only on a filing recorded as submitted (C8.2); else 409 `NOT_SUBMITTED`.
* **BR-3** (AG-7-33 r3, r8): outcome, decision date and an optional note (≤2,000); a second decision on a filing is a correction: a new row, the first kept; withdrawal is not an outcome.
* **BR-4** (AG-7-50 r1, r2, r4, r6): withdraw the whole case before any decision, with a date and a reason (≤1,000); after a decision 409 `HAS_DECISION`.
* **BR-5** (AG-7-34 r2, r4): close only when every active filing **of every stage** has a decision (`case-close.ts:60,142`), with a reason; else 409 `NOT_DECIDED`; a closed case takes no decision (409 `CASE_CLOSED`).
* **BR-6** (AG-7-13 r2, r3, r7): reopen needs `agent/case:reopen` and a reason; back at the stage it closed from; closing fields cleared, the audit line keeps them.
* **BR-7** (PF-4, BR-20): another firm's case is 404 with the same words; a missing key is 403.
* **Which buttons show** comes from the read's `can` block (§6), made on the server, plus the person's keys from `GET /platform/me`. The screen holds no copy of a rule.
* **Out of scope, named:** AG-7-33 r5 (filing and case stage moves, D9 Q54), r6 (next options on a refusal), r7 (class-2 client message box); AG-7-34 r1 (auto-close `COMPLETED`, waits on AG-7-49); AG-7-50 r3 (a filing's own withdrawal); AG-7-13 r4-r5 (reopen withdrawn filings, re-close rule); FIRM_SERVICE reopen by `agent/case:edit` (BUILD-26); the other case panels on a closed case (AG-7-34 r4: they still offer their actions; follow-up row); a decision date after today (the API accepts up to 2200: follow-up row, no change to the four actions here).

## 4. Layout (`design/board.html`)
* **Pattern:** a case panel (as Submission to IRCC) with **Dialogs** (EXPERIENCE §3: right first time, one step each). Panel "Decision and closing", directly under Submission to IRCC; a fixed-height skeleton while it loads so nothing below moves (`check_c69_page_browser`'s focus check). One line in the case header.
* **Panel header:** heading, one-line purpose, right-aligned "Signed in as {displayName} (firm staff)" (tooltip: "Decisions use your firm staff sign-in.").
* **Rows, from the new read (never the Submission read, which lists only the current stage):** one per active filing of any stage: person and programme words, stage name when not the current stage, Submitted on {date} or "Not submitted yet", the latest decision "Approved (Sample) · decided 16 Nov 2026 · note" and "1 earlier decision" (opens the history in place: outcome, date, recorded by {staff name} on {date}). Action per row: **Record a decision…** (`can.decide` lists it) or **Correct the decision…** (decided; quiet button).
* **Footer, one filled button at most (BIC-11):** `can.close` → **Close the case…** (filled); `can.withdraw` → **Withdraw the case…** (quiet, never filled); `can.reopen` → **Reopen the case…** (filled). Close not offered while open → "Close the case once every filing has a decision: {n} of {m} decided." and, for a filing that is not submitted, its name.
* **Header line (closed only):** Pill "Closed" or "Withdrawn" replaces the raw `CLOSED`; under the client line: "Closed by staff (Sample) on 18 Nov 2026 · Decision: Approved (Sample) · Reason: …" / "Withdrawn (Sample) on 15 Nov 2026 (recorded 18 Nov) · Reason: …" (the withdrawal date from the newest WITHDRAWAL row). With no staff session the header shows the Pill only. Long reasons wrap.
* **Dialogs (520 px):** Record a decision: filing named in the title, Outcome (radio Approved / Refused, each marked Sample), Decision date (empty: the authority's date, never defaulted), Note (optional, counter /2,000). Correct: the same, with "This records a new decision. The earlier one stays in the history." Withdraw: date (empty) + reason (/1,000). Close: reason. Reopen: reason, and "The case goes back to {stage}." Cancel left, the act's name right; one `idempotencyKey` per decision dialog; focus back to the opener, or to the panel heading when it is gone.
* **States** (board frames 1-7 draw open, decided, closed, withdrawn, the three dialogs, no session, no key, another firm and 375 px; the rest are specified here in words): loading; **no staff session**: "Sign in as firm staff to see and record decisions." and `SignInCard embedded` (its own words, h3, no frame) inline in the panel, replaced by the rows as soon as the token appears (`useStaffToken`); terms not accepted (428): the API's words and a link to My account; signed in without the key (read-only rows + "Your role does not record decisions. An Owner, Admin or Case manager can." / "An Owner or Case manager can reopen this case."); no filing submitted; some decided; all decided; an earlier-stage filing not submitted (named, Close not offered); closed; withdrawn; reopened (toast "Reopened. The case is back at {stage}."); each 409 (§5's words, in the dialog, nothing changed, the panel reads again); 404 ("This case is not in {firm name}, the firm you are signed in to."); 403; field errors on the field; long note and reason; 375 px (rows stack, buttons full width).

## 5. Copy
* Purpose: "Record IRCC's decision on each submitted filing, then close the case. Before any decision, the case can be withdrawn."
* Sample tooltip: "Sample words. The decision words come from the programme's pack; they become final once cited from an official IRCC source." Evidence found: Guide 5256 (visitor visa, saved 19 Oct 2026, https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5256-applying-visitor-visa-temporary-resident-visa.html, saved as `official-sources/ircc/instructions/guide-5256.html`): "If your application is approved, any original documents … will be returned to you with the requested visa." / "If your application is refused, … with an explanation of why your application was refused."
* Close-outcome words Withdrawn, Closed by staff, Completed: marked Sample too: the brief labels them Sample, which answers the challenge's question 2.
* **Refusals, per code (the screen's words; the API's text carries rule numbers, EXPERIENCE §5):** NOT_SUBMITTED "This filing is not recorded as submitted yet. Record the submission first." · HAS_DECISION "A filing has a decision, so the case cannot be withdrawn. Close it with a reason instead." · NOT_DECIDED "Every filing needs a decision before the case is closed. Before any decision, withdraw the case." · CASE_CLOSED "This case is closed. Reopen it first." · CASE_OPEN "This case is open." · CHANGED "This case changed at the same time. Open it again." · an unknown code: the API's detail. Field errors (REASON_REQUIRED, INVALID) use the API's field words as they are ("Give a reason.", "At most 1000 characters.", "A real date as YYYY-MM-DD.").
