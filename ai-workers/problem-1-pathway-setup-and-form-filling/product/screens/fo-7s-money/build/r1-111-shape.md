## The one rule the parts prove
Per party and per currency (never summed across currencies), the statement lists every fact in time order: invoice issued (+ its stored total with tax), payment into operating (−), refund (+), trust applied (−), credit applied (−), credit note (−), write-off (−). Its **closing running balance equals Σ over the party's issued invoices of (total − credited − written off − paid)** from `FACTS_LATERAL`, the same facts the invoice list and status use; its opening balance for a period is the same sum before `from`. The summary's **owed** is Σ `DUE_SQL` (never below zero per invoice) and its **credit** the credit-entry balance; a test proves closing balance = owed − overpaid-and-kept on the same data. Each export file's amount column sums, per currency, to the same figure the statement and summary give for that period (invoices = billed, payments = paid into operating, …; the trust ledger's last running balance = the trust balance).
