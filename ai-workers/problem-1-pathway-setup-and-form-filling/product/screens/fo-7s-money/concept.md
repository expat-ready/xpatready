## 2. Job and journey
**Job:** a firm bills won clients for work done, takes money in the right account, holds advance money in trust only when the Owner says it does, and corrects mistakes with new records — with one clear next step on each screen and no figure that adds two currencies. **Personas:** Owner · Billing person (`BILLING_FINANCE`) · Admin · Case Manager · Case Officer (C5: only cases and people they can already see, fail closed) · the client (pay page, no login). **The whole walk** (P-352): W-G1 → W-I1 → W-P1 → W-T1 → W-I2 → W-T2 → W-D1 → W-D2 → W-D3 → W-P2 (each file's §Walks, numbered steps with the expected screen after each, P-592).

## 3. Rules every money screen follows (each file adds its own BR-…)
* **T — trust words only in trust-on states** (AG-6-59 r4-r6): states U (Unanswered) · Y · N0 (No, nothing held) · N$ (No, money held or To move not empty). Trust screens show in Y and N$; N$ keeps only refund, Pay from trust, To move and Monthly check. The one exception is the Owner's own question (S1-S3). Full table: `concept-trust.md` §7.
* **O — two ways out of trust** (P-603): Pay from trust (to an issued invoice) and Refund trust to client. No screen has Transfer, Withdraw, Move or Adjust; "I moved these" records the firm's bank step and moves nothing (AG-6-73 r3).
* **N — never negative** (P-607 (1)): a shortfall shows as "CAD 1,500.00 must move from operating to trust: reversal of R-0007" and one Owner item; balances show 0.00, never "−" (departs from AG-6-75 r4, AG-6-60 r7).
* **C — per currency** (AG-6-25 to -27): every amount carries its code; stats and summaries show "CAD … · USD …"; no total row, no conversion.
* **K — no card into trust** (P-567 (3)): no pay link, card form or "Card" method on a trust request, a deposit, or a deposit invoice; plans and autopay only on invoices for work done (P-578).
* **A — actions from `allowedActions`** (FO-7q): an action shows only when the viewer holds its key and its route is on `main`; absent, never disabled.
* **W — the API's words**: every refusal shows the API's own sentence on the field or panel that caused it (BIC-13); amounts always two decimals.
* **Look**: Studio shell, team tokens, Inter 14 px, sizes 12/14/16/18/24, sentence case, right panels for anything with a field, centred modal only for a yes/no; every screen draws empty, loading (skeletons), normal, long names, error with Retry, read-only with why, conflict (Reload) (EXPERIENCE §4).
