### Major

**M2. The dialog cannot know which choice to show. *Fix:* add gap G15, an FO-7q addition, as a read: the trust state `{answer, shows}` for any staff member (no write), with its contract row and a test per state. *Needed.* High.

Narrow DW7 to trust balances, which is the brief's wording ("never a negative trust balance"). Draw FO-7m DW4's bounce state, and add the guard line. *Needed.* High.

**M4. The contract lists routes no brief names as FO-7b's, and marks one route FO-7b does name as a question.** Brief L31: "never invented". ## What is sound
* **Ways out of trust.** Only Pay from trust (same payer, issued invoice) and Refund to client take money out of trust in any drawn flow. The database also refuses an operating refund of trust money.
* **Card and trust.** No card into trust: deposits refuse CARD, the pay page never takes a trust request, and there is no trust card account.
* **Pay page.** It has no amount field. The only part payment is a plan's due amount (FO-7p). With the account down there is no card form for the listed statuses.
* **Currencies.** Every figure is per currency, there are no total rows, and DW4 is real.
* **Wording.** The trust question is word for word AG-6-59 r2. No-with-money-held keeps refund, Pay from trust, To move and the monthly check (r6). P-634 (2)'s credit-note choices are right.
* **Invoices.** Government fee lines have no tax control. Tax has no rate list, QST is hidden. Write-off has no amount field. Pay from trust is pre-ticked at issue (AG-6-68 r2). Reverse entry is never offered on a card payment, and a stale preview is refused.
* **Contract.** About 45 of the 66 contract rows match their brief's route, key and words exactly: FO-7q, t, r, c, p, m, g, v, and FO-7s2/s5 for the copy. The gaps G1-G14 are honest.
