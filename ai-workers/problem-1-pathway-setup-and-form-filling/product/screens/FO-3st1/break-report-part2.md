## What held
* Time spent: `-5`, `1.5`, `0`, `1441`, 20 digits and full-width `１２` are all refused in the concept's words. ` 60 ` and `060` save 60. The 600-minute booking pre-fills 600, and every booking pre-fills its booked length (J-S5).
* Fee: `1e5`, `-1`, `$1500`, `1500.555`, Arabic-Indic digits and `1.500,50` are refused. `1,500.5` saves 1,500.50. `0` saves 0.00. `9999999.99` is the top value. A fee with no currency gives "Choose a currency for the quoted fee." and stores nothing. Only CAD and USD are offered (J-S6).
* Items: 121 characters is refused and 120 is accepted (it wraps inside its chip). The 21st item is refused. Arabic, Chinese and full-width text save and display correctly. Case and spacing duplicates are dropped. Blank notes are stored as `null`. Notes containing `<img onerror>` are shown as plain text.
* Double-click on Save, and Enter twice, give one save (the busy button blocks the second). Only two `requestSubmit()` calls in the same tick reach the server's own 409.
* Stale save across two tabs (two "first" saves, and v1 against v2): STALE_VERSION shows the words and Reload. Reload brings in the other person's version, and nothing is overwritten.
* No-show after a record: read-only under "Attendance changed to No-show.", with no form and no Next step. This survives a reload. Attended again makes the record editable with its values (J-S8).
* Who: Read-only (list key only) sees no section and gets no 403 text. A Case Officer with the edit key who is not the host can edit. The Billing and finance host can edit and gets no Next step (no `agent/retainer:draft`). A case call (Follow-up type) gets no retainer button. A prospect gets "Prepare retainer", which opens the prospect, and Back returns to the booking (J-S7).
* `?engage=1`: opens Engage once for an open lead and keeps other query parameters. A reload does not open it again. Read-only does not get the panel. An unknown lead shows "Lead not found". Back from the open panel returns to the booking, and Forward does not open the panel again.
* Layout: at 375 px the panel is 326 px with no sideways scroll, and fee and currency wrap. 1024 px is fine. At 320 px (about 400 % zoom) there is no sideways scroll, and at 200 % the form reflows. Keyboard: Tab order is time, notes, item, + Add, fee, currency, Save, and Enter in the item field adds the item and keeps focus.
