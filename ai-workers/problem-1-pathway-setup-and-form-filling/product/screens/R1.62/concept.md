## 1. Job and journey
**Job:** a client, usually on a phone and often abroad, signs in to their firm's portal and sees, in plain words, which of their cases are open, what stage each is at, and what the firm last told them, and when.
**Persona:** the client (Client A; the principal applicant, AG-15-1). **Sources:** AG-15-1, -3, -9, -28, -29, -30, -54 (build card), -37 (English only, strings in one place); SA-11 §3.11 and line "Portal consent screen acceptance"; SA7-D67 (4), (6) (CP-1-91); P-584 (4). **Journey:** J-62 W2, W3 (client side), W4, DW1 (§6).

## 3. Business rules
* **BR-1 Own only (part 1; AG-15-38; R1.62).** Home and case pages show only what `/api/portal/*` answers for this account. Any case id that is not the client's own (another client's, another firm's, unknown, malformed) shows the **same** page, "We can't find that case", with no case number, no firm-staff words and no hint that it exists. The page never builds a case's text from the URL.
* **BR-2 Planes never mix (P-584 (4); DW1).** The portal keeps its own session (never the staff `xr-staff-session`); a staff session in the same browser does not open the portal and the portal's session does not open staff pages. Portal answers are cached under their own query key (`["portal", …]`) and dropped on sign-out, pause or session end.
* **BR-3 No account discovery (OWASP Authentication cheat sheet; GOV.UK Passwords).** At sign-in, an unknown email, a wrong password and an unknown firm answer the **same** words. The account's state (paused, closed, not active) is said only after the password matched (part 2 must keep part 1's order: check password first, then status). The password field is cleared after a failed try; the email is kept.
* **BR-4 Password (GOV.UK Passwords and Password input; WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.8).** Set once on Activate: at least 8 characters, no other composition rule, paste allowed, "Show password" toggle, no "confirm password" field, `autocomplete="new-password"` (Activate) and `current-password` (Sign in), email `autocomplete="username"`. The upper bound and the common-password list are part 2's (Sample until set; the screen shows the server's words).
* **BR-5 Session states (part 1 codes).** `CLIENT_PAUSED` → the Paused page; `CLIENT_REVOKED` → "This portal account is closed"; `FIRM_CLOSED` → "This firm's portal is closed"; `FIRM_NOT_AVAILABLE` (403, PF-27 held or locked) → a neutral notice in place of the content; `SESSION_ENDED`, an expired token or `NOT_SIGNED_IN` → Sign in with "Please sign in again." Each in the server's own words where it gives them.
* **BR-6 Delivered (Lead 7 on #3701; AG-15-54 rule 1 "succeeds on any channel, whatever the client's portal status").** The portal is a delivery channel of its own: a "Tell the client" update counts as delivered, and moves "last update", when **the person's portal account is ACTIVE at the send, or its email was SENT or DELIVERED**. **Server change in part 4** (FO-14's files, its owner's OK): `tell()` records the portal channel when the account is ACTIVE (a nullable `fo_client_update.portal_delivered_at`, one expand-only migration) and stays ACCEPTED with no email; `delivered()` reads `o.state IN ('SENT','DELIVERED') OR u.portal_delivered_at IS NOT NULL` (a left join). A NOT_SENT update with no active portal still moves nothing (DW2).
* **BR-7 "Last update" per case (AG-15-54 rules 3, 4, 5; see Q2).** Each case shows "Last update: Fri 13 Nov 2026": the newest delivered update **tagged to that case**; a general update (no case) appears in the stream but sets no case's date. Date only, no time, in the firm's time zone (`platform.time_zone`; cases have no office yet: AG16-45's office zone is a gap, said in the PR). Never set by an automatic line (sign-in, view) or a government letter. When none: the line is not shown (rule 5) and the stream says "No updates from <firm> yet."
* **BR-8 Stream (FO-14; AG-15-54 "no automatic 'update received from IRCC' line").** Only a person's updates, newest first, at most 20 (part 3's `ENGAGEMENT_EVENTS`), each with its date, its case number (or "General") and its text as plain text (line breaks kept, never HTML). The client's own activity events are not shown.
* **BR-9 Brand (PF-13 part 3; AG-15-28).** The frame wears the firm's banner, logo, name, colour and favicon from `GET /api/public/firms/:firmKey/branding`, drawn once, the same way as the booking pages (`useDrawnBrand`, `brandStyle`, `useFavicon`, `BrandImage`, `samePath`, imported, not copied). No brand when the firm is unknown; ExpatReady's neutral colour until the firm sets one.
* **BR-10 Which firm (PF-25).** On a firm's own address, the Host names the firm (as `/book`); on ExpatReady's host the firm comes from the invitation link (`?firm=<key>`) or, on Sign in, a "Firm" field. **Server change in part 4:** register `/portal`, `/portal/*` and `/api/portal/*` on firm addresses (host routing, like `front-office/booking/public-paths.ts`).
* **BR-11 Consent (SA-11 §3.11; SA11-D5).** When the firm has the portal consent screen on, Activate shows its text and a required tick; a changed text is shown again after the next sign-in, before Home. Accepting is recorded with date, time and text version (part 2). It is not marketing consent and says so.
* **BR-12 Session token (SA7-D67 (6), CP-1-91).** Portal session tokens must not be readable by page script in Release 1. Part 1 answers a bearer token; see Q1.

* **Pages (routes in `web/src/modules/portal/routes.tsx`, paths start `/portal`):** `/portal/sign-in` · `/portal/activate` (token in the fragment `#t=…`, never in the path or a log) · `/portal/forgot-password` · `/portal` (Home) · `/portal/cases/:id` · states drawn in place: Paused, Account closed, Portal closed, Not available, Signed out, Session ended, Not found.
* **Sign in:** Firm (only without a firm address or `?firm`), Email, Password + Show, **Sign in**, link "Forgot your password?". Errors: summary at top ("There is a problem") + on the field, same words.
* **Activate (part 2):** "Set up your portal", the email it is for (read-only), **Create a password** + Show + hint "At least 8 characters. * **Forgot password:** see §6b: copy only in part 4.
* **Home:** "Your cases" (cards, whole card is the link, 44 px+; open first, then closed under "Closed cases"), each: what it is (`title`), case number chunked (`2026-000 123`, UXL-07), "Stage: <client name>" (or "Stage: not shown yet" when null), Open / Closed as a text badge, "Last update: <date>" (BR-7). Then "Updates from <firm>" (BR-8).
* **Case detail:** back link "Your cases", title, number, stage, Open since / Closed on, last update, "Updates on this case" (the stream filtered by `caseId`). Links to other modules' pages (questionnaire, documents, signing, payments; P-584 (4)) are **not** drawn until those pages exist (no dead links).
* **States every page draws (EXPERIENCE §4):** loading (skeletons, `sr-only` "Loading"; no "No cases" before data) · empty (no cases: "Your firm hasn't opened a case for you yet. When it does, it will show here.") · normal · long content (a 120-character case title wraps; a 2,000-character update folds after 6 lines with "Show all") · error (load failed: "We couldn't load your portal. Try again." + **Try again**) · read-only (the whole portal is read-only in part 4) · conflict: N/A (nothing is edited). Reduced motion respected. Focus moves to the new `h1` on every page change and state swap.

## 7. Questions (recommendation first)
1. **CP-1-91: token not readable by page script.** Part 1 answers a bearer token; storing it in sessionStorage breaks SA7-D67 (6). **Recommend:** part 2's sign-in and activation answers set an `HttpOnly; Secure; SameSite=Strict; Path=/api/portal` cookie and the client guard reads it (no token in the body); part 4's screens never hold a token. Until part 2, the local stand-in keeps the token in memory only (lost on reload), labelled stand-in. *(Missing decision: who builds the cookie; part 2 recommended.)*
2. **"Last update" per case, and the empty case.** AG-15-54 says "on their case", rule 3 "General sets nothing", rule 5 "never sent: line not shown"; part 3's `lastUpdate` is per person and the brief asks for "No updates yet". **Recommend:** follow AG-15-54 (BR-7): a date per case card from case-tagged updates, no line when none, and "No updates from <firm> yet." as the stream's empty state, which keeps the brief's reassurance. *(Two sources conflict; the founder settles it if the lead does not accept the recommendation.)*
3. **Consent text and Forgot password have no owner.** The firm's consent setting (SA-11 §3.11, on by default with ExpatReady's starter wording) is not on main, and the shape's part 2 list has no reset. **Recommend:** part 2 adds the consent check, accept and a Sample starter text (firm editing is a later row); Forgot password goes to PF-4pw2, with part 4's "Contact your firm" page meanwhile.
