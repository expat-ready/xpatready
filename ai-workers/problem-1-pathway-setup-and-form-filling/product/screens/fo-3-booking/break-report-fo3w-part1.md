## Majors
**M1. If the visitor changes zone, a whole day of real times can disappear (BR-3).** `type-page.tsx:32` keys the month's query without the zone, but `:36` works out the month's range in the zone in use when it fetched. After a change, `:86` keeps only the slots whose day falls in the month in the new zone. Slots that move out of the cached range are in no month at all. Steps: (a) browser `Asia/Dubai`, open `/book/maple/free-consultation`, (change) → `America/Toronto`, pick Mon 30 Nov. Expected 09:00-16:30 (16 times, as a fresh Toronto visitor sees). Actual: 09:00-14:30 (12 times); 15:00-16:30 are never shown, and not in December either. (b) browser `Pacific/Kiritimati` → change to `Etc/GMT+12`: Mon 30 Nov is **disabled**. A fresh `Etc/GMT+12` visitor sees 16 times that day. Evidence: S/t9-dubai-to-toronto-nov30.png, S/t5-switched-nov30.png; t5/t9 output. This happens with any move to a zone further west (London → Toronto, Dubai → Vancouver), and nothing tells the visitor.

**M2. A time zone the picker accepts can make booking fail, with "Please check the highlighted details." and nothing highlighted.** `ZoneLine.use` (`type-page.tsx:64`) accepts anything `Intl` takes (`time.ts:9`). That includes offsets such as `+05:30` and `Factory`. The server's zone check (`service.ts:101`, regex) refuses them. Because the slots query is not refetched on a zone change (M1), nothing fails until Book. The 400's issue `timeZone: …` is not in `FIELD_WORDS` (`:161`), so the summary shows only the server's generic sentence. Steps: (change) → type `+05:30` → Use this zone. "Times shown in +05:30" appears; pick a time, fill in valid details, Book. Actual: "Please check the highlighted details.", no field marked, and every retry gives the same. Evidence: S/t4-zone-_05_30.png. The visitor can only get out by guessing that the zone is the cause.

**M3. A lost network answer on "Book this time" shows nothing, and the next try says to "book again" although the booking was made.** `type-page.tsx:159-161`: a `fetch` failure throws a `TypeError`, not a `Problem`, so `p.issues.map` throws inside `catch` ("Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'map')"). No summary appears, the button returns, and the focus stays where it was. Steps: Playwright `route.fetch()` then `abort("connectionreset")` on the POST (the server answered 201) → nothing happens (S/t9-lost-answer.png). Click again → "This form was already sent. Reload the page to book again." with no confirmation (S/t9-resend.png). The visitor is booked but is told to book again, and a reload offers the times again (see m1). BR-6's wording assumes the confirmation is still on screen, which is not the case here.

**M4. On the confirmation, Reschedule and Cancel booking both lead to a dead end.** `type-page.tsx:221, 234-235` link to `/book/maple/manage#<token>`, which shows "This page is not available." until part 2 ships. The PR says so openly. It still breaks concept §6b ("parts … built with them, never left half"), and the demo updates itself on merge. Steps: book → Reschedule → "This page is not available."; browser Back → the times page, and the confirmation is gone. Recommendation: do not render the two links until the manage page lands. Fixing this is the builder's choice; it is not new scope.

## Minors
- **m1. Reload or Back after booking loses the confirmation and offers the same type again.** `booked` is held only in memory (`type-page.tsx:31, 48`). Reload → "Choose a time". Back → the list. Forward → step 1. Nothing on the page says the visitor is booked (the email does), so a second booking for the same person is one click away. t2 output.
- **m2. The steps are not browser history.** Browser Back on "Your details" leaves for `/book/maple` and throws away the chosen time and everything typed. Forward lands on step 1 with empty answers. Phone-first visitors use the system Back gesture. t2 output.
- **m3. A very long type name gives sideways scroll at 320 px and at 1024 px with 200 % zoom (BIC-20).** `type-page.tsx:94` (times subtitle) and `:229` (confirmation line) lack the `[overflow-wrap:anywhere]` that the list (`list-page.tsx:23`) and the firm name have. With the 80-character name: scrollWidth 530 px at 320 and 530 px at 512. The confirmation is 555 px at 320. Evidence: S/t1-_book_maple_long.png, S/t2-booked-320-long.png.
- **m4. Bidirectional data is not isolated.** The type name sits in running text with no `<bdi>`/`dir="auto"` (`type-page.tsx:94, 229`; `list-page.tsx:23`). A name holding U+202E (the DB check `fo_consultation_type_name_check` lets it through: API note) reverses ", 30 minutes, Video call" into "llac oediV ,setunim 03". Evidence: S/t7-booked-640-rtl.png. Even a plain Arabic name will reorder the numbers that follow it.
- **m5. The zone label uses old and misleading names.** `browserZone()` (`time.ts:7`) shows Chromium's own spelling: an `Asia/Kolkata` browser reads "Times shown in Asia/Calcutta" (the concept's Client I is in Delhi), `Asia/Kathmandu` → "Asia/Katmandu", `Europe/Kyiv` → "Europe/Kiev", `America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires` → "America/Buenos_Aires". `Etc/GMT+12` is shown raw, although it means UTC−12 (POSIX sign). The datalist (`time.ts:10`) holds `Asia/Calcutta` and no `Asia/Kolkata`, and no `UTC` or `Etc/*`. t3/t4 output.
- **m6. "Search for a city or zone" does not find a city.** Typing `Toronto` or `dubai` then Enter gives "Choose a time zone from the list."; only the exact IANA name (in any case) works. The label promises a city search.
- **m7. Focus is lost between steps.** After a time is chosen (`type-page.tsx:49`), after "Back to times" (`:174`) and after "Choose" on the list, `document.activeElement` is `BODY`. "Back to times" also resets the chosen day to the first day of the month. The month grid has no arrow keys, so reaching the times means tabbing through every free day (7 stops with 11 free days; about 20 in a full month). t6/t8 output.
- **m8. A 429 or any error on a month change replaces the whole page.** `type-page.tsx:44` passes the error to `Gate` when the new month has no data. The page then shows only "Please try again in a minute." (`frame.tsx:28`), with no month buttons, no times and no retry. It stays like that after the limit clears, until a reload. Evidence: S/t10-429-next-month.png.

## Notes (not findings for this PR)
- n1 (API). `bookPerIp` is 10 per 10 minutes and the page says "in a minute". Visitors behind a shared office or family NAT share that allowance. The `.ics` link puts the manage token in a GET path (`type-page.tsx:233`), so it reaches access logs.
- n2. The token reaches browser history only through the Reschedule/Cancel links (the URL the email carries anyway). It is never in the address bar after booking (held).
- n3. `/book` and `/book/` (no firm key) show the Studio demo shell with the role menu. These paths are not in the concept's list, so this is a note only.
