## 1. Job and journey
**Jobs:** a visitor books a free consultation at the right time in their own zone, and later moves or cancels it from the email with no login (AG-4-16..20, -36..38). A host sees their week, books for a caller, says who attended, records the consult and starts the next step in one click (AG-4-39, -49, -50, -63, -67). Each person keeps their own hours, time off and calendar (AG-4-62, -40). An Owner or Admin sets up booking types and timings (AG-4-4, -5, -8, -9, -66). **Personas:** visitor; hosts (Case Officer, Case Manager; Billing and finance may host but has no booking key); Owner and Admin. **Journeys:** §6 (J-P FO-3w, J-S FO-3st1, J-A FO-3st2, J-T FO-3st3, J-C FO-3s5).

## 3. Business rules (each a pass and a fail test; the id is the source)
* **BR-1 Address and list.** Until PF-25 lets a page on the firm's own host call the API, the pages live at `/book/<firmKey>` (the list), `/book/<firmKey>/<slug>` (a type) and `/book/<firmKey>/manage#<token>` (the address the emails already carry, `service.ts:647`); the share link and the embed code show the same (the FO-3w row allows this path). The list shows active public types ticked "Show on booking page"; case types never; no public type → the empty text (AG-4-16 rules 1-2, §12). The list and the tick need Row X1 (§6).
* **BR-2 One not-found.** Unknown firm, unknown or inactive slug, case type, a UUID: the same page "This page is not available." (AG-4-17 rules 2-3). No id on any page (AG-4-16 §10).
* **BR-3 Visitor's zone.** Times show in the browser's zone with "Times shown in <zone> (change)"; a change redraws labels only, never the chosen UTC slot; no browser zone → the zone the slots answer returns, labelled (the firm's today); the chosen zone is sent as `timeZone` (AG-4-18 rules 1-3, §12).
* **BR-4 DST.** Only real times (a skipped wall time has no slot; a repeated one shows once) (FO-3 `slots.ts`). The note shows on a day when the displayed zone's offset differs between that day's start and end.
* **BR-5 Consent.** The firm's sentence beside an unticked box; booking never needs the tick (AG-4-19 rules 2-4). A firm with no sentence: no box, and booking still works. `main` requires `consent: true` and always records consent (`service.ts:111, 223, 227`; `ports/arrival.ts:59-60`), so **FO-3w waits on Row X1**'s consent change; no interim ships on a public door.
* **BR-6 Protected, not puzzled.** Hidden honeypot, minimum fill time, rate limits; no CAPTCHA; WCAG 2.1 AA; every step works at 360 px; 44 × 44 px targets (AG-4-20 rules 1-4). Text by answer: 429 → "Please try again in a minute." (AG-4-20 rule 4); 400 with no field issue (honeypot, too fast, open too long) → the server's own sentence; 400 with field issues → each on its field; 409 `consent-wording-changed` → its sentence and the box unticked; 409 `slot-taken` → BR-7.
* **BR-7 Slot taken.** The server re-checks under its lock; taken → "This time was just booked." (AG-4-36 §12) + "Please choose another time.", answers kept, the next free times shown. A double click books once (form stamp).
* **BR-8 Manage link.** Opens the booking; reschedule and cancel only from SCHEDULED or RESCHEDULED and only before the self-service limit (AG-4-36..38 rules 4, 6). After a move the page carries on with the new token the answer returns; the old links stop (rule 3). If the limit passes while the page is open, the next action shows BR-9's text. The type's allow flags (rule 4) need Row X1; until then both actions show.
* **BR-9 Inside the limit.** No buttons; AG-4-37 rule 5's sentence with the hours and the firm's phone once Row X1 returns them; until then the server's sentence (`service.ts:240`).
* **BR-10 Bad link.** Bad, expired, voided or cancelled: one text, AG-4-36 rule 9 (the manage page writes it; the API's 404 stays identical).
* **BR-11 Embed.** The type page in an iframe is the same page (AG-4-16 rule 4).
* **BR-12 Who sees a booking.** `agent/booking:list` is needed to open booking lists at all (AG-4-67 rule 4; Lead B's FO-3sr correction, 15 Nov); with it, the host always sees their own and others follow the record rule (rules 1-3). A host without the key (Billing and finance) has no Bookings menu entry; their own booking opens by its link, and its attendance is answered there (Q4). `main` lets the list key alone open every booking (`service.ts:335, 633`), so every staff read comes from FO-3a part 2's feed, which applies the record rule; FO-3sr's routes are used for writes. Fail test: AG-4-67 test 1.
* **BR-13 Staff book.** Free times first, from FO-3sr (the host is chosen by the pick rule). "Pick another time", host choice and the warning dialog (closed day, outside hours or time off, inside notice, overlap with "<person> <time>", busy on own calendar; "Book anyway" saves, "Cancel" saves nothing) need **Row X2** (AG-4-63 rules 2-4); FO-3st1 waits on it so the screen is built whole. No email → the note (rule 7). The zone sent: the person's zone when known, else the host's office zone, shown and changeable.
* **BR-14 Attendance asked, never guessed.** On the booking once the meeting has ended; pinned on the calendar only for the viewer's own bookings as host, at end + 30 min (AG-4-39 rules 1-2, 7); the home-screen item of rule 2 is AG-11's home screen, not this row; one click; no answer changes nothing; changeable later by the host or with `agent/booking:edit` (rules 3-5); buttons only for those; never for a cancelled booking.
* **BR-15 Consult record.** Opens only after Attended; time spent pre-filled with the booked length; notes; recommended from "Interested in" with "+ Add"; quoted fee optional, CAD or USD only, currency pre-filled; assessment read-only to staff; later No-show marks it "Attendance changed to No-show" (AG-4-49 rules 1-9, §12).
* **BR-16 Next step is a button** that opens the owning area's own screen; nothing is sent from here (AG-4-50 rules 1-5, §10). Lead: "Engage and prepare retainer" opens FO-4's Engage panel, then the retainer draft pre-filled with the record's items and fee; "Close lead" opens FO-4's Close lead panel (reason required, `agent/prospect:lose`). Prospect: "Prepare retainer". Case: no retainer button. Retainer buttons hidden without `agent/retainer:draft`; "Close lead" hidden without its key. "Request documents" is not rendered until documents are live (FO-4 BR-10). How the retainer draft is reached (rev 16.16 (b)'s `prepareRetainer` is written at the Attended click, before the record exists) is Q3.
* **BR-17 Own hours.** Named sets, one default, each with its zone; the only or default set cannot be deleted; windows `start < end`, no overlap; time off whole or part days on the same page; an Owner or Admin picks a colleague; changes never move a booking (AG-4-62 rules 1-8).
* **BR-18 Own calendar (AG-4-40, -56).** Connect Google or Microsoft on every plan; tick calendars to check (main pre-ticked), pick "Add my bookings to" (main pre-set); without one, ExpatReady hours are used (AG-4-40 rules 1-4).
* **BR-19 Booking types.** Name 1-80, description up to 1,000, length 5-480 min, active on or off, "Show on booking page"; web address made from the name, unique, a clash adds `-2`; `manage` is reserved; Duplicate adds "(copy)"; Retire (switch off) hides it from the page and pickers, bookings stay; Delete is soft; edits never change past bookings; a stale save is refused (AG-4-9 rules 1-7). Preview as a booker shows the same times for a date and zone, with the empty-day reason, and writes nothing (AG-4-8). Edit `agent/booking-event-type:edit`; view `:list`. Notice longer than the window → the editor warns (AG-4-4 §12).
* **BR-20 Timings.** Six firm defaults (reminders 24 h and 1 h, notice 4 h, window 60 days, gaps 0 / 10 min, self-service 4 h); type value, else firm value, else default; under the type's Advanced each shows "Uses firm default (<value>)" with "Change for this type" and "Reset to firm default"; a change applies to new slots and bookings only (AG-4-4, -5, -66 rules 1-3, 7-8). Per-type gaps, notice and window need `:configure-availability`; reminders and self-service `:edit` (§8). The firm screen's key is open in AG-4-4 §8 (Q2).

## 4. Layout (patterns from EXPERIENCE §3; tokens from TEAM-PATTERNS)
* **Public (Dialog / wizard, three steps on one page, phone first):** the firm's logo and colour (PF-13). List: a card per type (name, length, ways to meet, Choose). * **Booking detail (Side panel 520 px, sections):** When (office zone and the booker's), Who, Attendance, Consult record, Next step; "More" menu: Reschedule, Cancel booking (dialogs).
* **New booking (Dialog):** person (pre-filled from the lead, prospect or client), type, way to meet, the zone line, free times, Book; with Row X2 also host and "Pick another time".
* **My account → Availability (Workspace):** set list left, week editor right with its zone, Time off section below; colleague picker for Owner and Admin. **Calendar** section (FO-3s5). **Settings → Booking types** (Library list + drawer: Definition · Hosts · Advanced (allow flags with Row X1; timing overrides) · Share · Preview) and **Timings** (firm defaults form). Read-only (Case Manager, Case Officer, Read-only): the same pages with values as text and no buttons; no `:list` key: no menu entry.
* **States drawn:** empty (no public types; no bookings this week; no hours; no time off; no booking types), loading skeletons (month grid, week, list), slot taken, bad link, inside the limit, DST day, zone change, read-only, refused save on the field.
