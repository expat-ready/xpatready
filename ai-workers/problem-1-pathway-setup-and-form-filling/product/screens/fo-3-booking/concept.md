## 1. Job and journey
**Jobs:** a visitor books a free consultation at the right time in their own zone, and later moves or cancels it from the email with no login (AG-4-16..20, -36..38). A host sees their week, books for a caller, says who attended, records the consult and starts the next step in one click (AG-4-39, -49, -50, -63, -67). Each person keeps their own hours, time off and calendar (AG-4-62, -40). An Owner or Admin sets up booking types and timings. **Personas:** visitor (public), Case Officer and Case Manager (hosts), Owner and Admin (set-up, colleagues' hours). **Journeys:** §6 J-P1..P9 (FO-3w), J-S1..S8 (FO-3st1), J-A1..A5 (FO-3st2), J-T1..T5 (FO-3st3), J-C1..C4 (FO-3s5).

## 3. Business rules (each a pass and a fail test; the id is the source)
* **BR-1 Address and list.** `/book` lists the firm's active public types ticked "Show on booking page"; `/book/<slug>` opens one; case types never show; no public type → the empty text (AG-4-16 rules 1-2, §12).
* **BR-2 One not-found.** Unknown firm, unknown or inactive slug, case type, a UUID in the address: the same page "This page is not available." (AG-4-17 rules 2-3). The page never shows an id (AG-4-16 §10).
* **BR-3 Visitor's zone.** Times show in the browser's zone with "Times shown in <zone> (change)"; changing the zone redraws only labels, never the chosen UTC slot; no browser zone → the host set's zone, labelled; the chosen zone is sent as `timeZone` (AG-4-18 rules 1-3, §12).
* **BR-4 DST.** A day with a clock change shows only real times (a skipped hour has no slots; a repeated hour once) and says so under the day (AG-4-18; FO-3 `slots.ts` AG-4-1 rule 8).
* **BR-5 Consent.** One unticked box with the firm's sentence; booking never needs it (AG-4-19 rules 2-4). *Gap, see Q1:* FO-3's book route on `main` requires `consent: true` and names it "agree"; the page cannot book with the box empty until that changes.
* **BR-6 Protected, not puzzled.** Hidden honeypot, minimum fill time, rate limits; refused → "Please try again in a minute." and nothing saved; no CAPTCHA; WCAG 2.1 AA; every step works at 360 px; 44 × 44 px targets (AG-4-20 rules 1-4).
* **BR-7 Slot taken.** The server re-checks under its lock; taken → the form keeps every answer and shows the next free times in place (AG-4-36 §12; FO-3 `slotTaken`). A double click books once (form stamp).
* **BR-8 Manage link.** `/book/manage#<token>` opens the booking; reschedule and cancel only from SCHEDULED or RESCHEDULED, only before the self-service limit, only if the type allows each (AG-4-36..38 rules 4, 6). After a reschedule the old links stop and the new email carries the new one (rule 3).
* **BR-9 Inside the limit.** No buttons; AG-4-37 rule 5's sentence with the limit in hours and the firm's phone (Q2).
* **BR-10 Bad link.** Bad, expired, voided or cancelled: one text, AG-4-36 rule 9 (the manage page writes it; the API's 404 stays identical).
* **BR-11 Embed.** The type page in an iframe is the same page; the builder shows the share link and the iframe code per type (AG-4-16 rule 4).
* **BR-12 Who sees a booking.** The host always; others by the record rule plus `agent/booking:list` (AG-4-67 rules 1-4). Read-only people see, never change.
* **BR-13 Staff book any time, warned.** Free times first; "Pick another time" allows any; one dialog lists every reason (closed day, outside hours or time off, inside notice, overlap with "<name> <time>", busy on own calendar); "Book anyway" saves, "Cancel" saves nothing; no email → the note (AG-4-63 rules 2-4, 7).
* **BR-14 Attendance asked, never guessed.** Shown on the booking once the meeting has ended and as one home item at end + 30 min (AG-4-39 rules 1-2, 7); one click; no answer → nothing changes; changeable later by the host or `agent/booking:edit` (rules 3-5). Not for cancelled bookings.
* **BR-15 Consult record.** Opens only after Attended; time spent pre-filled with the booked length; notes; recommended from "Interested in" with "+ Add"; quoted fee optional, CAD or USD only, currency pre-filled; assessment shown read-only to staff; later No-show marks it "Attendance changed to No-show" (AG-4-49 rules 1-9, §12).
* **BR-16 Next step is a button.** Lead: "Engage and prepare retainer", "Close lead"; prospect: "Prepare retainer"; case: none until documents are live; "Request documents" hidden until AG-09's module is live (FO-4 BR-10 precedent); retainer buttons hidden without `agent/retainer:draft` (AG-4-50 rules 1-5, §12).
* **BR-17 Own hours.** Named sets, one default, each with its zone; the only or default set cannot be deleted; windows `start < end`, no overlap; time off whole or part days; an Owner or Admin may edit a colleague's; changes never move a booking (AG-4-62 rules 1-8).
* **BR-18 Own calendar.** Connect Google or Microsoft on every plan; tick calendars to check (main pre-ticked), pick "Add my bookings to" (main pre-set); without one, ExpatReady hours are used (AG-4-40 rules 1-4).

## 4. Layout (patterns from EXPERIENCE §3; tokens from TEAM-PATTERNS)
* **Public (pattern: Dialog / wizard, three steps on one page, phone first):** header with the firm's logo and colour (PF-13). `/book`: cards per type (name, length, ways to meet, Choose). `/book/<slug>`: month grid left, the day's times right (stacked at 360 px), zone line above; form (given names, family name, email, phone only for a phone call, way to meet, consent box); confirmation (when in the visitor's zone, way to meet and join details, Add to calendar, Reschedule, Cancel). Manage page: the booking card with Reschedule · Cancel, or the limit text, or the bad-link text.
* **Staff calendar (pattern: Catalogue with a view switch; no calendar library):** Day · Week · List; "Mine / Team" switch shown only with `agent/booking:list`; host filter; "Times in <office zone> (<office>)" line; booking blocks name the person and type; "Did they attend?" items pinned above the grid; New booking primary.
* **Booking detail (Side panel 520 px, sections, no tabs):** when (office zone and the booker's zone), who, type, way to meet, status chip, then Attendance, then Consult record, then Next step; Reschedule and Cancel in the panel header menu.
* **New booking (Dialog):** person (pre-filled when opened from a lead, prospect or client), type, host (pre-filled), way to meet, free times, "Pick another time"; warning dialog.
* **My account (Workspace):** Availability (set list left, week editor right with its zone; time off list below), Calendar section (FO-3s5). Settings → Booking types (Library list + drawer: Definition · Hosts · Advanced · Share) and Timings (firm defaults form + "Overridden on <n> types" list).
* **States drawn:** empty (no public types; no bookings this week; no hours; no time off), loading skeletons (grid and list), slot taken while choosing, link expired or voided, inside the limit, DST day, zone change, read-only, refused save on the field, conflict (booking moved by someone else: reload).

## 4b. Best in class (Unverified; the drafter checked no sources)

## 7. Questions (for Lead B; none for the founder: P-560 settles consult then retainer)
* **Q1 Consent box.** FO-3 on `main` refuses a booking without `consent: true` ("Please tick the box to agree."); AG-4-19 rules 2-3 say the box is optional marketing consent. **Recommend:** a one-line fix in FO-3w's PR is outside its touch list, so add it to FO-3a part 1 (`consent` optional, `marketingConsentGiven` stored); until then FO-3w draws the box as AG-4-19 says and qa marks J-P3 with the box empty blocked.
* **Q2 Limit text needs data.** `GET bookings/:token` returns `canChange` and its own sentence, but not the limit hours, the firm's phone or the type's allow flags. **Recommend:** FO-3a part 1 adds `selfServiceLimitHours`, `allowReschedule`, `allowCancellation` and `firm {name, phone, email, address}`; until then the page shows the API's sentence.
* **Q3 `/book` index and confirmation data.** No route lists a firm's public types, and the slots answer has `meetingLink: null` and no Add-to-calendar file. **Recommend:** FO-3a part 1 adds `GET api/public/firms/:firmKey/book` and the join details; Add to calendar downloads an .ics built from FO-3's `ics.ts` by the same route family.
