## What held

* **Rule (1), never HTML.** The tagline `<b>bold</b><img src=x onerror=alert(1)> …` is shown as literal text. There was no dialog and no page error in any tab. An 81-character tagline is refused (422); 80 characters fit.
* **Rule (2), neutral look.** Firm B shows `#1f4e79` on the rule and the buttons, with no images, no tagline, the app favicon and no request to firm A's branding.
* **Firm isolation in the app.** In-app navigation from A to B (pushState) gives B's neutral look and B's favicon, with no A colour or image. **Back** restores A's full brand and favicon; **Forward** restores B's neutral look. Going in-app to `/` restores `/brand/favicon-dark.svg`, and there is only one `<link rel=icon>`. Moving between type pages from A to B showed A's name for one tick with no brand, then B's name with B's look. A's brand never appeared on B.
* **Rule (3), not found.** `/book/no-such-firm`, `/book/SAMPLE-FIRM`, `/book/<img …>` (encoded), `/book/..%2Fsample-firm`, `/book/sample-firm%2F..%2Fsample-firm` and `/book/sample-firm/nope` all show the not-found state with no colour, image or tagline and the app favicon. The key is encoded into the read, so no path escape. `/book/%2E%2E` is normalised to `/` by the browser before the app sees it. On the manage page, a bad token, an empty `#` and a missing token show "bad link" with no brand.
* **Firms that are not ACTIVE or TRIAL.** With firm A set to `SECURITY_LOCK` or `PAYMENT_HOLD`: not found, no brand, the app favicon, and the image door answers 404.
* **Rule (4), images that fail.** An aborted logo request removes the logo, with 0 broken images. A banner that never answers leaves a plain 112 px band, not a broken icon. A 1×1 banner and a 3000×20 banner fill the band with `object-cover`.
* **Rule (6) and contrast.** White text on `#7a1f4e`, and on the hover shade `rgb(98,25,62)` (20% darker, checked), is above AA. In dark scheme the buttons keep `#7a1f4e` with white text (`text-primary-foreground` is fixed white).
* **Alt text.** The logo has `alt="<firm> logo"` (except for #1 and #4); the banner has `alt=""`. Neither image takes focus.
* **Keyboard.** Tab reaches "Choose" first and Enter opens it.
* **Widths.** No sideways scroll at 375 px (an 86-character unbroken name plus the 80-character tagline and a logo), nor at 1024 px or at 200 % zoom (512 and 720 CSS px). Long text wraps (`overflow-wrap:anywhere`).
* **The full journey.** On firm B, the type page, the details step, the confirmation and the manage link all carry B's neutral look. On firm A, the list page, the type page and the details step carry A's brand. The manage page with a valid link shows the right brand when the key matches. With a bad link it shows none.

## Verdict
**Changes.** The worst findings are two Majors: #1 (another firm's brand drawn around a booking by editing the key in the address) and #2 (rule (5) fails, with a 158 px layout jump and a colour flash).
