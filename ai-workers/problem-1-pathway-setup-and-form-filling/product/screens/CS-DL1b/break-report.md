## What held
* **DW7 redelivery:** a redelivery on the same day while the first is still unread adds no record. Redelivered the day after its day: 0 told. Queuing the same key again after its run is refused. A round-0 job redelivered after a reopen tells nobody.
* **DW8, close and reopen in one minute:** with one sweep, the round-0 jobs ran and told nobody, and round 1 fired once each. With sweeps between: cancelled, then re-armed. Close/reopen/close/reopen with sweeps between: each of the three reminders fired exactly once (r0 and r1 cancelled, r2 done). close→reopen→close before one sweep: nothing fired (only m1). Reopened after the deadline: nothing armed.
* **DW11, closed-day runs:** deadline Sun 7 Mar with Thu 4 and Fri 5 closed gives 23 Feb, 2 Mar and 3 Mar. A firm open only on Saturday falls back to Mon–Fri (1, 8 and 10 Mar for Wed 10 Mar). The suite's run of 262 closed days holds.
* **A→B→A before any fire:** A's set fired once each (25 Feb, 4 Mar, 8 Mar). B's jobs did nothing.
* **Stage moved while jobs wait:** the old stage's three jobs told nobody. The new stage's deadline (Thu 18 Mar) fired once each on 9, 16 and 18 Mar, at 14:00Z before the DST change and 13:00Z after it.
* **Team member reassigned between arm and fire:** the new officer (user-T) and the lead representative were told. The former officer never was.
* **firm_id null:** no job and no error. A forged job carrying firm A's id told nobody.
* **Two firms at once, cursor:** a malformed `case.closed` row (bad caseId), plus `person.merged` and other event types interleaved between firm A's and firm B's closes: both closes were consumed and the bad row was logged and skipped. Event `seq` is assigned under an advisory lock held until commit (pf3 migration), so the cursor cannot skip a late commit. A restart (a new instance with `seen=0`) replays the outbox and leaves round 1 waiting. Two sweeps and an `arm` at the same moment queue three jobs.
* **DST, America/Toronto, 14 Mar 2027:** that day is a Sunday, so no reminder can land on it (`isOpenDay`). 09:00 is 13:00Z. Deadline Sun 14 Mar gives 4, 11 and 12 Mar at 14:00Z. Tue 16 Mar gives on-the-day at 13:00Z. Arming at 02:30 local during the change night gives 15 Mar 13:00Z.
* **Firm B in Asia/Tokyo next to firm A in Toronto:** B fired at 00:00Z (09:00 JST) on 25 Feb, 4 Mar and 8 Mar, to firm B's staff only. A fired at 14:00Z. The `withFirm` path (`readFirmCalendar`) and the sweep's firm-key path agree on the zone.
* **Scale:** 400 open cases on a cited route take 1.6–1.9 s per sweep, sequentially, every 10 minutes. This is a background job, so no §9 budget applies.
* **Not a finding (outside the spec):** a case with no OFFICER or LEAD_REPRESENTATIVE ends its jobs `done`, tells nobody and logs nothing.
