## What held
Both kinds open a draft, and odd spellings, the wrong role and a signed-out caller are refused. `LogAlerts` emits the new name and refuses the old one. A Sunday covers every chain exactly once across 24 runs. The slice follows the UTC hour the job was created in, across retries and late starts. Duplicate and concurrent runs are harmless. A failing slice still checks its other chains, writes no heartbeat and fails its job. Slices stay far under 15 min on 1.06M lines. The 30-minute check is unchanged.

No Blocker or Major. Four Minors (7, 8, 13, 16).
