Assert that the sign-in either got 401 or that its token answers **401 SESSION_REVOKED after the lift**. Today it gets 200. Add a timing loop: 30 rounds of a lock and two sign-ins, then a lift, asserting that no 200 sign-in token works afterwards. Today about half the rounds fail.
* **M2:** Lock, move the clock 10 min, then `firmLocal.issue(sId, { authTimeMs: clock.now(), secondStep: true })`. Assert `me` gets 401 FIRM_LOCKED during the lock and **still 401 after the lift**. Today it gets 200. One fix covers M1 and M2: the lift also writes one revocation per staff member at `max(now, last_sign_in_at)` under the same staff row lock, so everyone signs in again after a lift. Re-reading the firm status inside `recordSignIn` alone fixes M1 only.

* **m3:** Lock, `GET /api/public/invitations/:token` should give `state: NOT_VALID`. Also, a refused `session/callback` for a locked firm should leave its code redeemable after the lift.

## What held

* **No deadlocks and no 5xx.** None in the builder's 12 rounds, nor in mine: T2, 60 rounds of a lock against two sign-ins; T5, 25 rounds of locking `sample-firm` against sign-ins of user-R, user-O and user-T at once; T4, 20 rounds of five lock/unlock calls at once; T6, 25 rounds of a lock against a transaction shaped like the webhook (billing advisory lock, read firm, move subscription and firm). `pg_stat_database.deadlocks` stayed at 0 throughout. T4's 184 `firm.locked`/`firm.unlocked` events strictly alternate. Two locks at once give one 200 and the rest 409.
* **Billing lock order.** The lock, the lift, `webhook.ts:138` and PF-11c's close (`closure.ts:109-110`) all take `plat_billing:<firm>` first. In T6, every lift that followed a hold from the webhook returned **PAYMENT_HOLD**. Code reading of PF-11c: the close re-reads `exitClock` under the lock, so it finds a locked firm "changed" (null clock). The lock's own audit line also makes the copy stale.
* **Status after a lift (T8).** TRIAL + subscription ACTIVE gives ACTIVE with `firm.activated`. ACTIVE + OVERDUE gives ACTIVE with no extra event. ACTIVE + ENDED gives PAYMENT_HOLD with a new `firm.payment_hold`. TRIAL + PAYMENT_HOLD gives PAYMENT_HOLD with `firm.payment_hold`. PAYMENT_HOLD + ENDED gives PAYMENT_HOLD with no new clock. Only signup creates subscription rows (`firms/signup.ts:350`), so "newest" and "live" pick the same row.
* **Pre-lock tokens.** A token signed in before the lock is revoked: the revocation is dated at least `last_sign_in_at`, read under the staff row lock, so it stays 401 after the lift. On Cognito (code reading), a refreshed access token keeps a pre-lock `auth_time`, so a pre-lock refresh token stays refused.
* **Locked token, every route.** I ran the full app on :4472, signed in user-S, locked `sample-solo`, and called all **298 routes that declare platform access**. That includes those added on main since 25 Nov: imports, find, file uploads (`@All *rest`), money plans, write-offs and statements, case and client notes, milestones and video settings. **296 answered 401 FIRM_LOCKED.** The other two are the sign-in doors, refused with 422 for the body and 401 SIGN_IN_CODE_REFUSED. No route answered 2xx. A firm token on `/unlock` got 403 FIRM_SESSION_REFUSED. The builder's lines cover Support, Admin and Billing (403) and no token (401).
* **Bodies (T7).** A 500-character reason is accepted. 501 characters give REASON_TOO_LONG. A Chinese and Arabic reason with an emoji is accepted. An extra field such as `firmId` gives 422 INVALID, an array 422, zero-width characters only and a number 422 REASON_REQUIRED, and JSON `null` 400. An upper-case uuid works. PROVISIONING, ABANDONED and DELETED give 409 NOT_LOCKABLE on lock and 409 NOT_LOCKED on unlock, and nothing moves. A malformed or unknown id gives 404 (builder line).
* **Invitations.** `accept` on a locked firm gives 410 (builder line). It is serialised with the lock through the firm row's FOR NO KEY UPDATE.
