## What held

* **Who may call:** Admin, Support, Billing, Content publisher and Auditor got 403 MISSING_PERMISSION on both lock and unlock, each with a REFUSED line (10 lines). A firm Owner's token got 403 `firm-session-refused` from the plane wall. No token got 401, a forged token 401, an Owner whose grant had ended 403, and a deactivated Owner 401 STAFF_NOT_ACTIVE. Upper-case `/API/SYSTEM/…` was still guarded (Support 403). A GET on the route got 404.
* **Reason:** `{}`, `null`, `""`, whitespace, control or bidi characters only, a number and an array all got 422 REASON_REQUIRED. 501 characters got REASON_TOO_LONG. `[]` got 422 INVALID and a bare string 400 BODY_NOT_JSON. Nothing moved on any of them, and each 422 wrote a REFUSED line with its code.
* **Ids:** a malformed id, a dashless uuid, padded ids, a 5,000-character id, `../` and quote-and-semicolon ids all got 404 before the body was read. An unknown uuid got 404 "No such firm.". An upper-case uuid worked and was stored lower-case. Locking firm B left A's staff at 200 and wrote no line on A.
* **Concurrency:** 10 locks at once gave one 200 and nine 409 ALREADY_LOCKED, with one event and one firm line. Six rounds of 3 locks and 3 lifts at once stayed serialised: the events and the firm lines alternate locked/unlocked with consistent before/after, and there was no deadlock.
* **The locked token:** I swept all 743 routes found in the controllers. All 269 guarded firm routes answered 401 FIRM_LOCKED. That includes `GET /api/platform/me`, `session/sign-out`, `me/terms` and terms accept, every `@HoldOpen` billing route and the banner, `audit/export`, front-office money and retainers, and the keyed `/api/runtime` case routes. Every other path refused the bearer token (403 link-only, or 401 on `/api/link`). No route answered 2xx.
* **Sign-in:** the local door and `session/callback` were refused with FIRM_LOCKED, and the reason never appeared. Sign-in worked again after the lift.
* **States:** PROVISIONING and TRIAL lock and lift back to themselves. ABANDONED and DELETED give 409 NOT_LOCKABLE. A second lock lifts to the newest prior status. `module_event` is insert-only (trigger), so the prior status cannot be lost.
* **Audit per change:** one `plat_system_audit` ALLOWED line (target `from`/`to`), `platform.bypass` plus `platform.firm.locked`/`.unlocked` on the firm's chain (actor `ourstaff:<id>`, OUR_STAFF), and the event written last, all under one correlation id. Refusals write a REFUSED line outside the rolled-back transaction.
