    How it works panel (opens from the list footer link)

    How complaints work here. Record each complaint when it arrives. The handler acknowledges it, adds handling steps, records when the response was sent and closes it with an outcome. Everything stays on the record. A complaint is overdue only while it is not acknowledged by its date; once acknowledged it shows as Open however long it waits. The 2-business-day acknowledgement period and the lists of channels, topics and outcomes are Sample until the College's by-laws are read. Unconfirmed: no counsel.
