## Major

**M1. A double click on "Record the complaint" records the complaint twice, and neither copy can ever be deleted (BR-7).**
Steps: as R, open the drawer, fill in name, How it came, About and summary, then double-click "Record the complaint" (Playwright `dblclick`). Expected: one POST and one complaint (STANDARDS [M] "Idempotent creates"). Actual: 2 POSTs and 2 rows ("Dbl dblclick" ×2 in `plat_complaint`). Two synchronous `click()`s also gave 2 rows (`NNNNNNNNNN Ünïcödé…`, ids `100f0bc1…` and `e9fcdf3d…`, 2 ms apart) and 2 "Complaint recorded." toasts. Pressing Enter twice in a field gave 1 POST (held).
Where: `web/src/modules/platform/complaints/record-drawer.tsx:55`. `onSubmit` calls `save.mutate()` without checking `save.isPending`. The button's `busy` (`:54`) only disables it after a re-render. The POST sends no idempotency key either (`:34`).

**M2. Once a firm has more than 100 complaints, the Overdue chip shows "0+" in orange while complaints are overdue, and Closed shows "0+".**
Steps: seed 106 newer complaints so the 3 overdue and the closed ones fall past row 100 of `view=all`. Open `/platform/complaints?view=all`. Expected (concept §4/§6 G1): "100+" once `next` is set. Actual: chips read `Open 100+ · Overdue 0+ · Closed 0+ · All 100+`, and opening Overdue lists 3 rows under a chip that says 0+. Overdue is the "needs you" number this screen exists to show, and it reads zero. The newest-first `view=all` page is the page least likely to hold the overdue ones.
Where: `list-page.tsx:23-25` appends "+" to every partial count. The orange test `!== "0"` (`:84-85`) colours "0+".

**M3. When the session ends mid-drawer, the typed complaint is lost without a word about whether it was recorded.**
Steps: as T, open the drawer and type name, channel, topic and summary. (a) Sign in as T elsewhere (API), then press "Record the complaint". (b) Separately, let the stored session reach `expiresAt` while typing. Expected: what was typed is kept (EXPERIENCE BIC-13) or at least "Not recorded" is said, and after signing in again the person can carry on. Actual (a): the drawer vanishes and the page shows "Signed in elsewhere". Nothing says the complaint was not recorded, and after "Sign in again here" the list opens with no drawer and an empty form. Actual (b): the drawer vanishes mid-typing and the sign-in card shows.
Where: the frame swaps out its children on a missing token (`web/src/modules/platform/frame.tsx:91-94`, PF-6). This unmounts `ListBody`, which holds the drawer's state (`list-page.tsx:61`, `record-drawer.tsx:23`). The drawer keeps no draft.

## Minor

* **m1. A time typed in the drawer near a DST change is stored an hour off.** `zonedToIso` takes the offset at the wrong instant. Firm zone America/Toronto: typed `2026-03-08T03:30` is stored as 08:30Z, which shows back as 04:30. Typed `2026-11-01T03:00` is stored as 07:00Z, which shows back as 02:00, and the whole 02:00–05:59 window is off. Europe/Paris `2026-03-29T01:30` shows back as 00:30. An untrue fact on a never-deleted register. `contract.ts:66-71`. Reproduced with the function body in Node.
