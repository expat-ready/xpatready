## Objections

### Blocker
None.

### Major
**M1. The Person filter has no source, and the person who left is missing from it.** *Breaks:* the Admin's story "When a rep leaves, I want to see in the activity log what they changed" (AG-16 `:468`). *Example:* a Case Officer deactivated on 10 Nov does not appear in the Person list on 16 Nov; and a custom role holding only `agent/audit:read` cannot fill the list at all. *Evidence (fact):* the concept's contract names no route for the options (`concept-draft.md:65`); the board lists three active people (`draft-1.html:94`); AUD-4's filter is "an actor word of the firm's staff" with no list route (`design/briefs/AUD-4.md:74`); the only staff list on `main` is `GET /api/platform/staff`, guarded by `agent/staff:manage` (`api/src/platform/staff-read/routes.ts:11`), not `agent/audit:read`. *Fix:* ask AUD-4 (not merged; its brief already reads names by `(firm_id, staff_id)`, `AUD-4.md:75`) to return the firm's distinct actors of the range, deactivated included, with the read; until then, each line's Who opens "Show only this person's changes", which sets the filter from the line and needs no list. A test: a deactivated person's lines are reachable by the filter. **Needed for the proof.**

**M2. A firm's lines can stay on screen after the session changes.** *Breaks:* "never another firm's line" (BR-1) on the client side, where the server rule cannot help. *Example:* firm A's Owner leaves the page open; the session ends (401, "Signed in elsewhere") and the sign-in card shows in the same tab (`session.ts:67-81`: the token is per tab, a 401 shows the card again); firm B's Owner signs in there; until the next fetch, the mounted page still holds firm A's 50 lines and its expanded raw `detail` with personal data in full. *Evidence:* the concept covers the 401 state (`concept-draft.md:34`) but not what is held in memory; AG-13-43 makes the raw detail full personal data. Confidence medium (depends on PF-6's frame remounting on sign-in). *Fix:* key every read and the page state by the session's firm and staff id from `/me`; clear lines, cursor and open rows on `endSession`. Add DW1b: sign in as firm A, end the session, sign in as firm B in the same tab: no firm A text is in the DOM at any moment (asserted before B's first answer). **Needed for the proof.**

**M3. The read-back sentence does not read every filter, so a filter still hides lines silently.** *Breaks:* the concept's own promise "a filter that hides lines is never silent" (`concept-draft.md:32`) and EXPERIENCE §2.7 "counts say what they count" (`EXPERIENCE.md:24`). *Example:* an Owner sets Advanced › Done by "Firm staff", collapses Advanced, later presses "ExpatReady staff access": the list is empty (actor type and the shortcut contradict, `AUD-4.md:74`), the sentence says "ExpatReady staff access from 9 Nov to 16 Nov" and names no Advanced filter; a link with `?entityKey=` opens with Advanced closed and the sentence silent; the export confirm names only the dates (`draft-1.html:158`), so the Owner believes the file is complete. *Evidence:* board 1's sentence has person, area and dates only (`draft-1.html:106`); chips exist only for the shortcut (`draft-1.html:141`); TEAM-PATTERNS asks active-filter chips for every filter (`design/TEAM-PATTERNS.md:13`). *Fix:* the sentence names every active filter, Advanced included, and each has a removable chip; Advanced opens when one of its filters is set; "Done by" and the shortcut cannot contradict (pressing the shortcut sets Done by to "ExpatReady staff" or clears it); the export confirm repeats the sentence. Test: each filter set from the URL alone appears in the sentence. **Needed for the proof.**

**M4. The walk asserts support-session lines that only AUD-3b writes, and AUD-3b is not a dependency.** *Breaks:* W1 cannot pass when AUD-4, AUD-1c part 2 and PF-6 part 1 are in, as the brief says (`AUD-4s.md:3`). *Example:* step 3 expects "the seeded support session's start, change, end" (`concept-draft.md:71`); board 3 shows "Started a support session: reason … (ticket …)" and "Ended … after 18 min; 1 change made" (`draft-1.html:146-148`); those lines, the reason and the duration come from `plat_support_session` in AUD-3b (`BACKLOG.md:1119`, "scope to confirm"), which neither the brief nor the concept waits on. *Fix:* W1 asserts what the brief says: lines written through the port with an `OUR_STAFF` context and none of the firm's staff (`AUD-4s.md:31`); session start, end, duration and reason show when AUD-3b's lines exist, and the PR's contract gap says SA-2-29 is met in part until AUD-3b. **Needed for the proof.**

**M5. "ExpatReady has been told and is looking into it" promises a duty the build does not carry out.** *Breaks:* AGENTS rule 4 ("Legal points are marked Unconfirmed: no counsel") and the concept's own line "No word on the page promises a legal duty" (`concept-draft.md:62`). *Example:* after a forced break (DW4) the Owner reads that someone is investigating; in the build an alert line `audit.chain_integrity_failure` is raised (`AUD-1c.md:138`) and no person or process is named to act on it. A firm with a broken record may also read it as ExpatReady accepting a notification duty. *Evidence (fact):* copy at `concept-draft.md:57`, `draft-1.html:166`. *Fix:* "ExpatReady's integrity alarm was raised. Nothing you do here changes the log."; no "looking into it", no "notified" of anyone by name; mark the wording "Unconfirmed: no counsel" in the concept. The keep sentence is sound (below). **Needed for the proof.**

**M6. A stale integrity check still shows green.** *Breaks:* EXPERIENCE §2.11 [M] "Freshness visible … overdue in orange" (`EXPERIENCE.md:28`). *Example:* the checker stops (job down in the demo); 5 hours later the pill is green "Records intact — last checked 5 h ago", a reading the Owner trusts. *Evidence:* the concept has three states only (`concept-draft.md:26,67`); the checker runs every 30 minutes (`AUD-1c.md:138`). *Fix:* a fourth display (not a new route): over 60 minutes since `lastCheckedAt` (two missed runs, Sample), orange "Last check overdue — last checked 5 h ago", by shape and text; a test with `checked_at` set back. **Needed for the proof.**

**M7. Keyboard path: hidden "Details" buttons and lost focus.** *Breaks:* EXPERIENCE §2.12 [M] (`EXPERIENCE.md:29`) and WCAG 2.2 2.4.11 (focus not obscured). *Example:* a 600-character summary (AUD-4's limit) kept on one line with ellipsis, as the concept asks (`concept-draft.md:32`), pushes the "Details" button that follows it out of the clipped cell (`draft-1.html:57,113`): the button takes focus while invisible. After "Show more" on a 2,000-line range, focus stays on the button, now under 50 new lines, with no word of how many are shown; after Escape on the export confirm the concept does not say where focus returns. *Fix:* "Details" in a fixed place at the row's start (or the When cell is the disclosure button); after "Show more" focus moves to the first new line and a `role=status` says "Showing 100 lines"; the confirm returns focus to "Export CSV"; the integrity pill's `aria-describedby` points to a real element (`draft-1.html:87`). Add to the walk: Tab through a line with a 600-character summary; Show more twice. **Needed for the proof.**

## What is sound
* Server-first rules: firm from the session, never from the request; `platform` lines never shown; read by `firm_id`, not `chain_key` (BR-1, `AUD-4.md:59-60`); one-year range stopped in the form and by the API's 400.
* Read-only with no edit, delete or hide control (SA-11-14 "nothing in the product deletes an audit entry").
* The keep sentence is SA-11-14's own product wording, "for security", and SA-11-14 says "no legal floors": it promises no legal duty. "Records intact" is SA-11-34's wording and the concept rightly avoids "tamper-proof" and "compliant".
* Export order: confirm, line committed before data, snapshot so the count and the file agree (`AUD-4.md:77`); CSV formula escaping in AUD-4; 2,000 lines is well under the 100,000 Sample cap.
* "Show more" by cursor with DW5 over equal times (AG-13-31); no page numbers.
* No immigration values, no AI, no new route or table: sides (1) product logic and (5) immigration fit find nothing (an audit page holds no program, form or fee).
* Best in class: the GitHub row is read verbatim with its date; the other three are marked **Unverified** with the reason (proxy 403) and a re-read on the Mac before the label. That is honest and matches P-392 (checked before labelling the screen PR). Their "Beats" claims (Clio "edits not shown without Support") stay out of the PR body and demo until re-read.
* Every state the brief lists is drawn; capability throughout, no simulated-person control.
