## 1. Job and journey
* **Job:** the firm's Owner or Admin sees who changed what in the firm, read-only, and proves it with an export (AG-16 §3.6: "The firm reviews its own log read-only (config changes, exports, connection changes, escalation and AI changes)").
* **Stories (AG-16 personas, `:467-468`):** Owner: "see in the activity log who changed what"; Admin: "when a rep leaves, I want to see in the activity log what they changed". Plus SA-2-29: every support session in the Owner's history (support person, start and end, duration, reason or ticket, changes made).
* **Persona and key:** holders of `agent/audit:read` (Owner and Admin by default; AG-16-26, SA-6-33). Everyone else sees no menu item and a plain no-access page.
* **Journey:** Settings › Security & your data › Activity log → last 7 days, newest first → narrow by person or area (or press "ExpatReady staff access") → open a line for the change in words, raw before and after under Advanced → Export CSV (confirm) → the export itself is a new line.

## 2. Ten layers
* **Data:** `audit_event` (AUD-1a: `firm_id`, `actor_type`, `support_session_id`), read only through AUD-4's `firmLog` (firm from the session). Integrity from AUD-1c's chain state. Keep period: AUD-5's keep-periods read, else one written sentence. Nothing new.
* **API:** AUD-4 `GET /api/platform/audit`, `GET /api/platform/audit/export`; AUD-1c `GET /api/platform/audit/integrity`. No new route.
* **UI (new):** `web/src/modules/platform/audit/` in PF-6's frame; one line in PF-6's `routes.tsx`; the "Security & your data" index page and menu item if this ships before PF-11s (Lead 61's ruling, comment 6900882697 on #1875).
* **Team's code:** the built read API (`audit-read.controller.ts:90,100`, AG-16-26) has no screen; team list patterns (`FilterLayout`, `EmptyState`, `NoAccessState`, `ConfirmActionDialog`) are reused by name.
* **Rules, engine, AI, governance:** no AI on this page; no write except the export's own line (AUD-4's service writes it).

## 3. Business rules (the server enforces; the page shows)
* **BR-1 firm-wide, own firm only.** Every line of the session's firm, never another firm's, never a `platform` line (AG-16-26, SA-6-33; AUD-4 DW2, DW3). Pass: Owner sees Admin's lines. Fail: firm B's line ids in the address show nothing of B.
* **BR-2 read-only.** No edit, delete or hide control anywhere (AG-16 §3.6 "read-only"; SA-11-14 "nothing in the product deletes an audit entry").
* **BR-3 date range required, at most one year, default the last 7 days** (Sample limit, AUD-4). Stopped in the form with the limit named; the API's 400 shown if forced.
* **BR-4 "ExpatReady staff access"** shows only our staff's lines (actor type ExpatReady staff, or inside a support session) (SA-11-34's shortcut, applied to the firm's page; SA-2-29). Pressing it clears Advanced › Done by (they would contradict); with a Person also set, the sentence names both and the empty state says "No ExpatReady staff changes by <person>". **SA-2-29 is met in part:** the session's start, end, duration and reason show once AUD-3b writes them; until then the shortcut lists our staff's own lines (contract gap).
* **BR-5 the change in words** as "field: old → new", the raw before and after under Advanced (SA-11-30). A line with no pair shows its summary only; an action no module named shows its code under Advanced (AUD-4 DW7), never a blank.
* **BR-6 export is logged and confirmed.** Confirm "This export is recorded in the activity log" once per export; the file has the filtered lines, personal data in full (AG-13-43: "no masked or unmasked variant"); the new line appears at the top afterwards. Over 100,000 lines → "Narrow the dates" (Sample limit, AUD-4).
* **BR-7 integrity shown plainly.** "Records intact — last checked N min ago"; red with plain words and the affected range on a failure; "Not checked yet" before the first check; **orange "Last check overdue — last checked <t> ago" + "Checks run every 30 minutes; the last one is late. The lines are still shown." · not checked: "The first check runs within 30 minutes of the first line." when over 60 minutes have passed** (two missed 30-minute runs, Sample; EXPERIENCE §2.11); detail on click (SA-11-34). The firm's own chain only (AUD-1c DW7); the detail says which areas the check covers (demo case lines chain under `sample:`, AUD-1c).
* **BR-8 keep period stated:** "Your activity log is kept for 7 years for security." (SA-11-14, AG-16-66), from AUD-5's read when on `main`, else written once with its source.
* **BR-9 people keep their names.** A deactivated person's lines keep their name (SA-11-14); our staff show "ExpatReady support" (in a session: the session's name; AUD-4's gap); jobs show "The system".
* **BR-10 no skip, no repeat.** "Show more" by the cursor; equal times never repeat a line (AG-13-31; AUD-4 DW5). No page numbers.
* **BR-11 times in the firm's time zone** (`platform.time_zone`), named once in the read-back sentence and in the CSV's time column header.
* **BR-12 every filter is visible.** The read-back sentence names every active filter, Advanced included, and each has a removable chip; Advanced opens by itself when one of its filters is set (also from the address); the export confirm repeats the sentence (the file holds exactly those lines).
* **BR-13 the page holds only the signed-in person's firm.** Every read and the page state are keyed by the session's firm and staff id from PF-6's `/me`; on session end (401 or sign-out) lines, cursor and open rows are cleared before the sign-in card shows, so a different firm signing in the same tab never sees them.
* **BR-14 people who left can still be picked.** The Person list is the people who acted in the range, deactivated included and marked "left the firm" (asked of AUD-4, see §6); each open line also offers "Show only this person's changes".

## 4. Layout (`design/draft-1.html`, boards 1-6)
Pattern: **Library list** (`EXPERIENCE.md` §3) without a side panel. **Where we chose differently (recorded, P-48):** a line opens in place, because a line has four facts and a raw block, and a drawer would hide the list a person is scanning. Four primary filters plus the shortcut exceed `FilterLayout`'s two, because the date range is required and person and area are the two stories' own questions; the rest sit under Advanced. Page card in PF-6's frame; trail Settings › Security & your data › Activity log; title, one muted subtitle; on the right the integrity pill and Export CSV (the only action). Filters: From, To, Person, Area, the "ExpatReady staff access" toggle; Advanced: action, record type, record reference, done by. Under the filters, **one sentence that reads the filters back** ("Showing everyone's changes in all areas from 9 Nov to 16 Nov 2026 (Toronto time)") so a filter that hides lines is never silent. Table: When · Who · Area · What happened (summary, then the change chip), 44 px rows. **The When cell is the line's disclosure button** ("Details" sits there, never after a long summary), so focus is never on a hidden control; long text is one line with the full text in the open row. "Show more" under the table; after it, focus moves to the first new line and a `role=status` says "Showing 100 lines". The export confirm returns focus to "Export CSV"; Export is disabled until the file arrives (no second file, no second line); the file is fetched with the session token and saved (the routes take a bearer token). The keep sentence is under the table. Filter state in the URL (team rule). At 1024 px the four columns fit with no sideways scroll; Advanced wraps below.

**States (board 6):** loading (skeleton + `role=status`); nothing in range (names the dates, "Clear filters"); no lines yet (first-use wording); range over one year (on the field); no access (403: "You do not have access to the activity log", who to ask); export too large; load error with Retry; session ended elsewhere (PF-6's `signed-in-elsewhere.tsx`, reused); integrity broken, overdue, not checked yet.

**Where it saves the Owner effort or prevents a wrong reading:**
* The read-back sentence means a forgotten filter never hides lines silently.
* "ExpatReady staff access" is one click to answer "did your support people touch our data?" (SA-2-29).
* The integrity pill says plainly whether the record can be trusted. None of the four products was confirmed to show this, so we make no tamper-proof claim, only "Records intact — last checked".
* The export confirm prevents a surprise line in the log.

## 5. Copy
* Title "Activity log"; subtitle "Who changed what in your firm. Read-only."
* Shortcut button "ExpatReady staff access"; tooltip: "Show only changes made by ExpatReady staff, including support sessions."
* Integrity: "Records intact — last checked 12 min ago" · "Records could not be confirmed intact" + "A check on <date> found a change in the lines from <from> to <to>. ExpatReady's integrity alarm was raised. Nothing you do here changes the log." (wording Unconfirmed: no counsel; it promises no investigation or notice) · "Last check overdue — last checked <t> ago" + "Checks run every 30 minutes; the last one is late. The lines are still shown." · not checked: "The first check runs within 30 minutes of the first line." · "Not checked yet" + "The first check runs within 30 minutes of the first line."
* Range error: "Pick dates at most one year apart (this range is <n>)."
* Empty: "No changes from <from> to <to>" + "Nothing matches these dates and filters. Try a wider date range or clear the filters." First use: "No activity yet" + "When your team changes settings, roles or connections, each change is listed here."
* No access: "You do not have access to the activity log" + "Ask your firm's Owner or an Admin." · Person action: "Show only this person's changes" · after Show more: "Showing <n> lines".
* Export confirm: "Export these lines as CSV?" + the read-back sentence + "This export is recorded in the activity log." Buttons "Cancel" / "Export CSV". Too large: "This export is too large" + "<n> lines match; one export holds at most 100,000. Narrow the dates and export again."
* Keep: "Your activity log is kept for 7 years for security." No word on the page promises a legal duty: "for security" is the product's wording (SA-11-14); no "compliant", "legally required" or "tamper-proof".
