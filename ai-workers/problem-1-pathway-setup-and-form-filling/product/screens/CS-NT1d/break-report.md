## Held
- **caseScope:** with `view.all` taken away, an out-of-scope Case manager gets 404 on notes, people, timeline, versions, POST and PUT pin. A person on the team through `view.own` (case_team_member by actor id) still reads (200). Another firm's Owner gets 404 on people. A malformed id, a missing case, `%00`, a 5,000-character id, or a case-client id on the people route are all 404. An upper-case id is 200.
- **Mention check:** naming someone out of scope, DEACTIVATED or SUSPENDED is 400 "Mention people who can open this case." and nothing is written, on an add and on an edit. Another firm's staff or no such id is 400 "people of your firm only". `null` or a string instead of a list is 400. Upper-case plus duplicate plus the writer's own id tells one person once. The people list leaves out deactivated, suspended and out-of-scope staff, and puts the case team first.
- **Idempotency and conflicts:** 6 parallel adds with one key and one mention store 1 note and send 1 notification, and the other 5 replay. The same key with other content is 409. 4 parallel edits on version 1 give one 200 and three 409 `NOTE_CHANGED`. Edit as the officer on the Owner's note is 403. The double Add in the browser is covered by the builder's test.
- **edited:** a pin alone gives `edited: false`. A text change gives true. A text change back to the version-1 words gives false, as concept r2 states literally ("differ from version 1"). Whitespace-only changes are "unchanged".
- **Panel:** the error stays on the box with the words kept (m6 case). The 5,000-character unbroken note at 1440 px causes no sideways scroll. No sideways scroll at 1024 px or at 1024 px with 200 % zoom (512 CSS px): page 512/512, panel 478/478. Show older pages to the end with no repeats on both All and Documents (240 lines, a third uploads, the rest `viewed`: 80 of 80, then the end line). No page errors in any walk.
- **Client visibility and personal data:** the people read returns only staffId, name and onCase (no emails). Signing back in as a **different** person does not carry over the first person's draft. A client note naming DEACTIVATED or SUSPENDED staff is saved but sends them no notification. The mention bell for the case officer links to the case they can open.
- **Contracts:** all four zod shapes parse the live answers in every walk.
