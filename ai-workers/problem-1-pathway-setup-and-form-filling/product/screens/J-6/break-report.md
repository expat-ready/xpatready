## Findings

**F1 (Major, J-6 claim; rule in FO-2): "a Case Officer with own sees only theirs" is false once an unassigned lead exists. The walk passes only because its data has none.**
Repro: as the Owner, add a lead, then set `fo_lead.owner_staff_id = null` (an unassigned arrival). As `user-O`, `GET /api/front-office/leads?view=all` returned `["Uma Unassigned", …, "Omar…"]`, `?view=unassigned` returned 200 `["Uma Unassigned"]`, `GET /leads/<id>` returned 200 with the person's email and phone, and `POST /leads/<id>/take` returned 200. After the Owner deactivated the second Case Officer, `user-O` also opened that person's lead (200) and the screen offered "Take it".
Expected under the walk's claim (`qa/built/J-6-part1.md`: "sees only theirs"): own leads only. Actual: own leads plus every unassigned lead and every lead of a deactivated person. The cause is in `api/src/front-office/sales/common.ts:329`: `seesUnassigned` is true for anyone holding `agent/prospect:view.all`. The walk keeps that key on the Case Officer and switches off only the firm setting. This is FO-2's AG-2-13c rule 9 and BR-4, so the rule itself may be right. The finding is that J-6's "own" is the setting, not an own-only key. `j_6.ts:75` seeds every lead with an owner, so W2 (`j_6.ts:138`) never meets this case. Either the claim is narrowed, or the walk gets an unassigned lead and a judge for it.

**F2 (Major, J-6 claim; conflict between FO-2 and PF-6b): "own versus all" means two different things for the same viewer and the same area (agent/prospect).**
Repro, with the setting off: as the Owner, `GET /api/front-office/leads/<second Case Officer's lead>` returned 200, but `GET /api/front-office/retainers/<retainer on the second Case Officer's prospect>` returned 404 (the Owner's own retainer returned 200). Leads give Owners and Admins "all" whatever the setting (`common.ts:326-328`, `admin ||`). Retainers need `view.all` and the setting on, Owner included (`retainers/service.ts:166-167`), and PF-6b's own sweep expects that ("off: view.all holder (user-R)": own retainer only). The walk's W1 (`j_6.ts:133`) proves the leads rule and A4 re-runs the retainer rule; both pass, and they contradict each other. The leads routes also carry no `@OwnOrAll("agent/prospect")` (`sales/routes.ts:16,25`), so PF-6b's DW7 "every marked route swept" never sees them. Which rule wins goes to the founder or Lead B (SA-6-24 against AG-2-13c). It is not settled here.

**F3 (Major, PF-6b ruling 2 (b); J-6's MONEY line covers only one door): money keys handed out through an invitation write no money-keys audit line.**
Repro:
1. As the Admin, `POST /api/platform/staff/invitations {"roles":["BILLING_FINANCE"]}` returned 201.
2. `POST /api/public/invitations/<token>/accept` returned 200, `roles:["BILLING_FINANCE"]`.
3. The new person's `/platform/me` lists 15 money-area keys (invoice:void, refund:create, payment:record, …).
4. The audit lines for the invitation and the new staff id are only `invitation.created`, `invitation.accepted` and `staff.sign_in`. `platform.staff.money_keys_granted` was written 0 times.

Expected: as with `PATCH …/roles`, where the line is written (`platform/identity/service.ts:239-244`), a role that brings money keys writes its own line. Actual: the accept path creates the grants with no such line (`platform/identity/session/service.ts:96`). The invite path has none either (`service.ts:329-343`). J-6 A3 tests only `PATCH /roles`.

**F4 (Minor, J-6 proof quality): A3's "MONEY fail" half is not a money check.**
`j_6.ts:99` has the Case Officer try to give Billing/Finance and expects 403. The Case Officer lacks `agent/role:assign`, so the guard refuses any role change at all (MISSING_PERMISSION), money or not. Its "fake" is the literal `{status: 200}`, not an answer from a build without the money rule. A build with no money rule at all would still pass A3. A money-specific fail would be a person who holds `role:assign` and is refused for the money role. In this build nobody but the Owner and Admin holds `role:assign`, and the Admin holds every Billing/Finance key. So `PATCH /roles` by the Admin, `["CASE_OFFICER","BILLING_FINANCE"]`, returned 200 (the money line was written). An Owner-only rule for money keys exists nowhere in code, which matches PF-6b's ruling as written. If R1.6 means more ("money keys never come with another key"), that is a question, not a fix.

**F5 (Minor, FO-2, AG-2-86 rule 3): Take tells a person who may not see a lead that it exists and who owns it.**
Repro: as `user-O`, `GET /leads/<second Case Officer's lead>` returned 404. `POST /leads/<same id>/take` returned 409 ALREADY_TAKEN "Sample Officer Two … already has this lead." with `ownerStaffId`. The same happened for the Owner's lead (uppercase id), returning 409 "Representative (Sample) …". Firm B's lead returned 404. So inside the firm, "refused by URL, the same 404 as another firm's" holds for GET, close and reopen, but not for take. The cause is `leads.ts:257`: the loser is named for anyone with `seesUnassigned`, even about a lead they never could see. You need the id (a uuid) to do this. J-6 W3/A1 test only GET.

## What held
- **List, `?view=`:** `mine`, `all`, `closed`, a missing view, `view[]=all` and `view[x]=all` all gave only own (plus unassigned, F1). `ALL`, `bogus`, an empty view and `all&view=closed` were refused with 422. On screen, a bogus view falls back to a normal tab.
- **Lead id variants:** uppercase, `{…}`, a trailing `/`, `.json` and `not-a-uuid` gave a 404 or "Lead not found" on screen, never the lead. Mixed-case paths `/API/FRONT-OFFICE/…` and `//front-office//` reach no API route (the web page or a 404).
- **Actions on another person's lead:** close, reopen and their uppercase forms returned 404; assign returned 403 MISSING_PERMISSION; the assignees route returned `[]` for the Case Officer. Four parallel takes of one unassigned lead gave one 200 and three 409, with one audit line.
- **Possible match:** adding someone else's person by email answered "A person you cannot see". With `confirmedNew`, the read showed "Possible match with a person you cannot see". A known `personId` from someone else's lead returned 404. The KNOWN_PERSON answer still confirms that the email is on file, by design (AG-2-4 rule 6).
- **Retainer show and changes (`@OwnOrAll`):** with the Case Officer's token, show, an uppercase id, edit, send and download-link on another person's retainer returned 404; drafting on another person's prospect returned 404; firm B returned 404 on both retainers. After the second Case Officer was deactivated, their retainer stayed 404 for `user-O`.
- **Roles:** Case Manager to roles returned 403. The Admin got 403 OWN_ROLES on itself, 403 OWNER_ONLY for the Owner role, 403 ROLE_BEYOND_OWN_KEYS for Case Manager (licence keys), and 404 for firm B's staff. Firm B's Owner on a firm-A person returned 404.
- **Invitations:** the Case Officer got 403 on revoke and invite.
- **Tokens:** a re-signed-in person's old token, an earlier-instance token and a deactivated person's token were refused with 401 (SIGNED_IN_ELSEWHERE or STAFF_NOT_ACTIVE). A swapped `sub` (same signature), an empty signature and `alg:none` were refused with 401. Local sign-in with firm B's key and a firm-A email or actorId was refused with 401. A truly expired token could not be made without the server's random key (not tested). The local sign-in by email alone is the PF-4 stand-in, not a finding.
