FO-2b (API only, no schema change; map row 98): Corporate prospects.
* Engage a lead as Corporate (`POST /api/front-office/leads/:id/engage/corporate`): a company name is required (422 at `companyName`); a company with the same normalised name ("pine bakery, ltd." = "Pine Bakery Ltd") is offered (409 `COMPANY_MATCH` with the offers) and nothing links by itself until the person picks it (`companyId`) or chooses New company (`newCompany: true`). The prospect starts in the Corporate pipeline's first stage (Engaged); the enquirer is linked as the company's PRIMARY contact (OTHER when the company already has one) and stays labelled Lead.
* `GET companies/match?name=` gives the offers alone. Company contacts: `GET|POST companies/:id/contacts` (a person on file or a new one, checked against people first), `POST company-contacts/:id/role` (changing a role to PRIMARY makes that person primary and repoints the company's open prospects), `POST company-contacts/:id/end`; one person may hold several roles, never one role twice, and the primary contact cannot be removed.
* Change type (`POST prospects/:id/change-type`, Individual ↔ Corporate) keeps the number, owner, lead, notes and history and the same stage key (else the nearest earlier stage); refused once a signature exists (409 `SIGNED`), while a retainer is out (409 `RETAINER_SENT`) or while one is being prepared for the current client (409 `RETAINER_OPEN`: Agreements cannot discard a draft yet).
* The boards: `GET pipelines/corporate/board` lists only Corporate prospects, `GET pipelines/individual/board` only Individual ones, each with its eight stages in order.
* `prospect.won`'s subject part (`ports/events.ts` `ProspectWonSubject`, built by `wonSubjectOf`): `COMPANY` with the company's name and its live contacts with their roles, or `PERSON`.
* Every change writes one audit line in its transaction; a failed audit line rolls the change back. Test: `tests/check_fo2b_corporate.ts` (61 checks).
