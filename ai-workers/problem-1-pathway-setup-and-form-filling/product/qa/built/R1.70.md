R1.70 (CS-D7c + CS-D7, runtime, configurator and case side; PR expat-ready/xpatready#3180): a document type carries cited translation, notarisation, expiry and max-age defaults, set only as a library change a second person approves and refused without its official quote and source (the database refuses an uncited published version); each case document carries its language, translation and notarisation (Not required, Required, Provided with its detail) and expiry date, starting from its type's defaults in the case's release. On the case page's Documents panel the line's menu → **Document flags…** sets them (keyboard only; one audit line per flag changed). A document in a language other than English or French, on a type with the rule, adds one "Translation + translator's affidavit" line under its person (English or French takes it away); an expired document, or one uploaded longer ago than its type allows, shows a warning on its line and blocks nothing.
