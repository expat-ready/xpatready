FO-13i (#2011; Code SOR/2022-128 s. 24(2)(a), Unconfirmed: no counsel; migration: one key; API only, the panel is FO-13s): staff record how a client's identity was checked.
* `POST /api/front-office/people/:personId/identity-checks` (key `agent/identity-check:record`: Owner, Admin, Case Manager, Case Officer, Sample) records the method, the document seen (type, issuing country, expiry, **only the last 4 characters** of its number) and the details corroborated (name, home address, phone, email, business details). The number field takes only the last 4 (more → 400 naming it; a `documentNumber` field → 400); the note refuses anything holding 6 or more digits in all, in any script or spacing, or a 6+ character word with a letter and 3+ digits (400 naming `methodDetail`). A number spelled out in words, or of 5 digits or fewer, is not caught.
* An optional copy (PDF, JPEG or PNG by its content, at most 10 MB, Sample) is stored with the check in one insert. It opens only through a 5-minute link for a person with `agent/document:show` who still sees the client (`POST …/identity-checks/:id/copy/download-link`, `GET /api/front-office/identity-copies/:token`); expired → 410, another firm or person → 404.
* `GET …/people/:personId/identity-checks` lists the checks, newest first, to anyone who sees the client (their lead or prospect, a company's contact or a retainer's signer); others get 404. A check never changes: a correction is a new check.
* Send on a retainer still succeeds without a check, and now answers "No identity check is recorded for <signer>." in `warnings` (also on the audit line).
* Nothing of the document number, the copy or its link reaches a log, an audit line or the `identity.checked` event.
* Proof: `tests/check_fo13i_identity.ts` (25 checks, DW1-DW6).
