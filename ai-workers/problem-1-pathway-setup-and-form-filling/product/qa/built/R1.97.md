R1.97 (AUD-1c part 1 #2129, part 2 the checker, AUD-2 part 2 the archive, J-97, part 5 the alert's name and the weekly slices, part 6 the heartbeat alarm; runtime and deploy): every audit line is on a hash chain at the database. There is one chain per firm, one for the platform and one per demo workspace. The insert trigger sets `seq`, `prev_hash` and `entry_hash = sha256(prev_hash || canonical(line))`; a writer never does. A checker runs every 30 minutes from each chain's last verified place, and weekly from seq 1 (in 24 hourly slices on Sunday, each well under the job runner's 15 minutes). It names a changed line (hash), a removed line (gap) or removed newest lines (missing), each with its chain and seq. Each break raises one alert `audit.chain_integrity_failure` (SA-11-6) with ids and the range only, and every completed pass writes the heartbeat `audit.chain_verify_ok`; the CDK alarm `xr-<stage>-audit-chain-verify-missing` pages when none is seen in an hour (synth only). `GET /api/platform/audit/integrity` (`agent/audit:read`) gives the firm's own chain: INTACT, BROKEN with the range, or NOT_CHECKED. `GET /api/system/audit/integrity` (`system/audit-log:list`) gives every chain. Every day, each firm's and the platform's new lines go to the write-once archive as a JSON Lines file plus an anchor, kept 7 years (AUD-5). The checker compares the newest anchor with the database, so a line rewritten after export with its hash recomputed is still caught. The archive's S3 adapter writes with Object Lock COMPLIANCE in ca-central-1, If-None-Match and a SHA-256 checksum; it is proven on a recorded bucket (`tests/fixtures/aud2-s3/`). On a copy the archive is the fake (Sample).
