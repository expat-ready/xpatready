FO-3w (part 1 #1921: list, type page, form and confirmation; part 2 #1982: the manage page): a visitor opens `/book/<firm>` and books a free consultation in their own zone, with no login. From the link in any booking email (`/book/<firm>/manage#<token>`), or from Reschedule or Cancel booking on the confirmation, the booker sees "Your booking: <day, time> <zone> time, with <firm>." and can do two things. Reschedule: pick another free time of the same type → "Booking updated…"; the page carries on with the new link and older links show "This link is no longer valid…". Cancel: confirm, with an optional reason (≤500, never shown back) → "Your booking is cancelled…". Within the type's self-service limit the page shows "It is less than <n> hours before your meeting…" with the firm's phone and no buttons. A type that turns off reschedule or cancel shows "This booking cannot be changed online…". `/book/<firm>/manage` is routed before `/book/<firm>/<slug>`, so "manage" is never read as a type (QA-496). Acceptance: `tests/check_fo3w_booking_browser.ts`, `tests/check_fo3w_manage_browser.ts`.
