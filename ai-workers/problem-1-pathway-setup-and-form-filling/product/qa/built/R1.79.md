R1.79 Firm dashboards and reports: PRs #3291 (PF-R1), #3642 (CS-R1), #3734 (PF-R2fo), #3812 (PF-R2cm) and #3864 (PF-R3). A firm's staff open Reports (/platform/reports) and see their role's dashboard of sales, case and money figures within their access, filtered by dates, person and client type, money per currency; a holder of the Export permission downloads a table as CSV, recorded in the activity log.
