R1.110 (the client identity check): FO-13i part 2 (#2305) and FO-13s part 1. On a lead or prospect page, a person can record how they confirmed the client's identity, with an optional copy; see the checks and open the copy; and see the retainer Send's no-check warning until one exists. An expired document is refused on its field (the firm's own rule, P-735). J-110 walks it.
