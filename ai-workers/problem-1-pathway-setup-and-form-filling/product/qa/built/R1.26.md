FO-4b (#2451, merged) and FO-4w (PR #2525): the public enquiry form end to end by screens. An Owner or Admin sets the sites that may show the firm's form and copies the snippet (Sales › Settings › Web form); on those sites the snippet draws the form, takes a fresh submission id per draw and sends the page's campaign facts (utm, click id, landing and referring page) onto the lead's attribution; elsewhere, or with the script blocked, visitors get the link to the hosted form; a bot (hidden field, too fast, too many) is thanked and nothing is stored; the same send twice is one lead; each field's message is on its field; no cookie or storage. Proof: tests/check_fo4b_embed_browser.ts, tests/check_fo4w_web_form_settings_browser.ts, tests/journeys/j_26.ts.
