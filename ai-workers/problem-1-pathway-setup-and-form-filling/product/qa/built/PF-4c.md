PF-4c part 1 (#2052; runtime, permissions, API only; AG-1-69, SA-3-39): an Owner or Admin can invite up to 20 people in one form, `POST /api/platform/staff/invitations/batch` with rows of name, email and roles. Every problem shows on its own row at once (a bad email or role, the same email twice, someone already on the staff or already invited, an Owner row, a role beyond the inviter's own keys) and then nothing is sent; one seat check covers the whole form ("7 of 10 seats after these invites … Remove 1 person or upgrade your plan", 409, nothing created). Otherwise each person gets their own invitation and email (greeting by name; the name is never stored); a failed email leaves that invitation pending. Part 2 (the licence-number format check) and the Invite screen (PF-6 part 2) are separate. Run `tests/check_pf4c_batch_invite.ts` (labels `PF-4c DW1`-`DW5`).
