PF-19b part 2 (#1956; runtime, map rows 48-49; Release 1 row R1.28 "withdrawing one keeps the others"): a marketing or service email now carries a one-click unsubscribe link (List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post; none on transactional mail). Opening the link shows one Unsubscribe button and changes nothing; pressing it (or the mail client's one click) withdraws only that person's email marketing consent through ConsentPort (via UNSUBSCRIBE): their WhatsApp opt-in and WhatsApp marketing consents stay live and service email still goes; the next marketing email is held back as SUPPRESSED "WITHDRAWN: MARKETING". The same link a second time, a changed link, or another firm's link answers 404 and records nothing; one IP's tries are rate limited. On test and production servers email goes out through Amazon SES (ca-central-1) instead of the local demo inbox; SES's bounce and complaint notices (SNS, signature checked first) put a hard-bounced or complaining address on the firm's suppression list once. MailPort over MessagePort lands staff and system email as TRANSACTIONAL; the app's staff mail stays on PF-4b's own adapter until Lead B rules on keeping invitation tokens and codes out of stored bodies (review M1).
