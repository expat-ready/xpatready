FO-3pa (#1830; API only, one additive migration): a visitor opens a firm's booking list (`GET /api/public/firms/<key>/book`: the shown types by name, length and ways to meet, no ids); books without ticking the marketing box (no consent stored) or with it (consent with the wording id); sees the firm's name, email, phone and address on the list, type page and booking answers, and the self-service limit in hours on the type page and booking answers; gets the join link once a video booking is confirmed, never on the type page; downloads the booking's calendar file by the link (`…/bookings/<token>/ics`, the same invite as the email); and cancels by the link with an optional reason (up to 500 characters, kept in the audit sentence only). A type can be hidden from the page (`showOnBookingPage`) or refuse reschedule or cancel by link (`allowReschedule`, `allowCancel`: "This booking can't be cancelled here. Please reply to your confirmation email or call the firm."). The screens are FO-3w's.
