3. **Observation, Minor (Q4, embed vs hosted):** the two are not kept apart in the source. Both write door `WEB_FORM`, source `WEBSITE` (shown as "Website form", contract.ts:43), `sourceDetail` = the form's name, intake key `form:<id>:<submissionId>`, and consent source `WEB_FORM` (arrival.ts:62). As a result, the Source filter cannot split hosted from embed leads, and a hosted lead arriving with `?utm_campaign=` loses the campaign. A founder question, not a bug: is "came by the embed" a filterable source or only attribution?
