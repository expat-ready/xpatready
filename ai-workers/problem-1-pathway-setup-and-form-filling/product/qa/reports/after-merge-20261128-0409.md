## The changes, read against the code (underneath)
**Upload limits.**
- All four routes set `fileSize: MAX_DOCUMENT_BYTES` for multer ≥ 2.3, which accepts exactly the limit and refuses one byte more. The intended result is that exactly 20 MB is taken and 20 MB + 1 is refused (413).
- Its tests: c64 "a file of exactly 20 MB is taken" and c82 B149 "over 20 MB refused". B149 passed here. The exactly-20 MB case needs setup, so it rests on 46101513889 and nightly 46099862629.

**SNS addresses.**
- Real SNS shapes still pass:
  - the certificate: `https://sns.<region>.amazonaws.com/SimpleNotificationService-<id>.pem`
  - the SubscribeURL: `/?Action=ConfirmSubscription&TopicArn=…&Token=…`
- Look-alike hosts, other paths, other actions, ports and user info are refused (400, nothing fetched).
- **By design, two cases also refuse:** China and GovCloud regions, and any AWS region added after the list was written. SNS then retries the notice. The product sends from ca-central-1, so this does not affect Release 1. Noted only.

**Intake and validated.**
- Multer always gives a real list, so a person's upload is unchanged.
- The new refusals reach only callers that do not use multer.
