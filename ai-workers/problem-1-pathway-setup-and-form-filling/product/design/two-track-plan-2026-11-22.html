How rows get to "proven" faster

  • Nearest-to-done first: a row with 2 parts left beats a row with 10.
  • Journeys are written before the build ends, so qa only runs them.
  • A label goes on only after a green run (a red label cost 34 minutes today).
  • Records changes that other PRs cite go first in the queue.
  • Database changes take turns per area, not one turn for the whole product.

How we watch and adjust

Watch

  • A check every 10 minutes (03, 13, 23 ... past the hour); the full report at 03 and 33.
  • Each check opens claude.ai/code with "Show more" clicked until it is gone, reads every lane and its model label, and answers each waiting lane.
  • Each check updates both boards above: the five steps per row and each row's time against the estimate.

Adjust: the trigger and what I do (tightened 14:5x, P-798)

  • A lane that ended its turn or waits for input → answered at the next check (10 min at most), not after 90 min.
  • A question for the tech lead → answered at the check that sees it (10 min), not 30.
  • Approved but not in the queue for 2 checks (20 min) → read Mergify's reason and fix it, not after 60.
  • A step older than twice its usual time today (measured by flow-measure) → find what holds it and add or move a builder.
  • A lane with work left and no push for 30 min → send its next step; still silent at the next check → replace it.
  • More than 10% of merge batches failing → keep PRs that touch the same checks apart.
  • qa behind by 2 or more rows → add a qa lane.
  • Weekly plan at 96% → every lane finishes its step, pushes and stops.
