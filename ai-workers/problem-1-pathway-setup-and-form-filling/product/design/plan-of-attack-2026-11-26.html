How a capability gets to Works

A capability (an R1 row) is a chain of parts. Each part is one PR of under 800 changed lines (P-610). It passes these stations in order. The row counts only when its last part is proven on main by qa (P-339, P-636: only Proven counts).

1 · Shape

Row ready

Lead: duplicate check, touch list, acceptance test, "Done when", founder decisions answered (P-526).

2 · Build

Cloud builder

One row per session, fresh branch, pushes each step; checks its own change in a browser (OS-72).

3 · Review

Fresh Opus reviewer

Approves an exact head. A breaker also runs for money, schema, permissions or a new screen (P-303).

4 · Label

review: approved

Only on "Reviewer: Approve <sha>" and green checks (P-385). The Lead reads migrations and tools first (P-423).

5 · Merge queue

Mergify batches

Pipeline queue ahead of product (P-827). A failed batch needs a green full run before the PR is relabelled (P-731).

6 · Proof

qa on main

Gate A: walk by screens. Gate B: pack green on main, re-run, fresh reader. An open Major keeps it Partial (P-305).
