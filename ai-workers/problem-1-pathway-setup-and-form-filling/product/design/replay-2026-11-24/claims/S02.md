M1. Major. api/src/front-office/sales/public.ts:47 (`ipOf`), used at :62, :82 and :120; api/src/main.ts sets no `trust proxy`; deploy/container-entry.mjs:9-11.
- Wrong behaviour: the visitor address is the TCP peer, so in the container build every visitor is 127.0.0.1. The "per address" limits become one global limit, and the consent and receipt address hash is the same for everyone.
- Input and result: in the container, `container-entry.mjs` pipes 0.0.0.0:8080 to 127.0.0.1:4400, and main.ts listens on 127.0.0.1 only. `req.ip` is therefore always 127.0.0.1.
  - Any 30 posts in 60 s, across all firms and all forms, give everyone else 429 on the 31st. One bot closes every firm's form.
  - 120 page reads a minute in total do the same to the read.
  - The 60-per-form limit can never be reached.
  - Every `ip_hash` within one process is identical, so it is useless as evidence.
- Clause: FO-2 "the 31st post in 60 s from one address → 429; the 61st on one form token → 429"; checklist 9 and 10. The test injects `ip` into the service, so it cannot see this.
- Confidence 72. IN this change (the booking door has the same habit beside it).

M2. Major. public.ts:173-182 (`attributionOf`), with common.ts:21 and :30.
- Wrong behaviour: an invisible, captured campaign value refuses the whole enquiry, although the code's own rule says "captured, not typed: cut to length, never refused".

Result: 422 "utmCampaign: one line, no control characters." on a field the visitor cannot see or change. An unexpected `clickIdKind` gives a 400 saying to reload the page. Reloading keeps the same campaign link, so every retry fails and the lead is lost. Only over-length values are handled.
- Clause: AG-2-8a "attribution present on a form lead"; checklist 6 and 7.
- Confidence 55. IN this change.

M4. Major. public.ts:64 and :83-85; api/src/main.ts:244.

The test only checks the header on the API call. The form page itself is not in this change, so the harm starts when it ships.
- Clause: "a page outside the allowed sites → refused (and the served page carries `frame-ancestors`)"; checklist 1.
- Confidence 42. Clause left unmet by this change.
