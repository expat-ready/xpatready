# S11 (blind claims, verbatim; model output)

Paths: CA = repo/api/src/front-office/booking/consultation-agreement/

**M1. Critical. CA/service.ts:247. The 24 h reminder can never be sent in the running app.**
- Wrong behaviour: `runReminder` sends class "SERVICE" with no `person`. The real adapter refuses that: api/src/platform/messages/send/mail.ts has `if (m.class !== "TRANSACTIONAL" && !m.person) throw new MessageRefused(...)`.
- State and result: any unsigned agreement 24 h before its meeting. The job throws on every try and no reminder reaches anyone. Booking's own host mail passes `person: {kind:"STAFF", id}`. The suite uses a fake mail that lacks this rule, so it passes.
- Clause: "the reminder 24 h before the meeting while it is unsigned"; checklist 9, 12.
- Confidence 96. IN this change.

**M2. Major. CA/service.ts:279-293, controller.ts:51-56. The reads skip the booking record-visibility rule.**
- Wrong behaviour: `read`, `forBooking` and `forPerson` check only firm plus `agent/booking:list`. The booking module's `staffBooking`/`seesBooking`/`seenWhere` (FO-3vr, AG-4-67) answers 404 for a booking whose record the person may not see.
- State and result: "everyone sees all" off, and staff X holds `agent/booking:list` but neither hosts the booking nor owns the prospect. `GET /bookings/:id` gives 404, but `GET by-booking/:id` gives 200 with person id, status and signatory. `by-person/:id` likewise lists every agreement of a person they cannot see. Conversely a host without the list key, who may read their own booking (rule 2), gets 403 on its agreement.
- Clause: checklist 2, 3.
- Confidence 88. IN this change.

**M3. Major. CA/service.ts:266 (also 56). A paper-signed agreement shows as unsigned.**

- State and result: staff use FO-5's `POST retainers/:id/signed-offline` on a CONSULTATION agreement (that route accepts the kind), so status is SIGNED_OFFLINE. The booking and person reads say `signed:false`, "Agreement not signed yet".
- Clause: "the booking and the person show 'Agreement signed' or 'Agreement not signed yet'"; checklist 2.
- Confidence 88. IN this change.

**M4. Major. CA/service.ts:337-361 and 447-492. Ending the agreement through FO-5's doors does not end its signing.**
- Wrong behaviour: `openAs` and `apply` check only the signature request's status, never the agreement's or its version's.
- State and result: staff withdraw (`retainers/:id/withdraw`), edit or record offline signing while the request is SENT or VIEWED. FO-5 leaves the request live, and this service ignores `retainer.withdrawn`, `request_ended` and `signed_offline`.
  - The client's old link still records consent, emails a code and gives a signing link.
  - Their "signed" event then updates `fo_retainer` from WITHDRAWN, SIGNED_OFFLINE or DRAFT to CLIENT_SIGNED. `fo_retainer_write` raises, the transaction (receipt included) rolls back, and `signingEvent` throws on every provider retry.
  - The client has signed a withdrawn document at the provider and nothing is recorded.
- Clause: checklist 2, 5, 7.
- Confidence 78. IN this change.

**M5. Major. CA/service.ts:97, 418-436, 464-492. Cancelling the booking does not end the signing.**

- State and result: client signed, then booking cancelled. `POST :id/host-link` still answers 200 with a link. The host signs, the agreement becomes SIGNED and the client is emailed a signed copy for a cancelled consultation. A client already holding the provider link can also still sign.
- Clause: checklist 5 (every exit ends what it started).
- Confidence 72. IN this change.
