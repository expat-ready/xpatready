M1. Major. `tools/check_pdf_opens.py:49-56` (`reader.pages`, `page.get("/Type")`), called from `api/src/case-checklist.ts` `pdfOpens`/`checkedCaseFile`.
- Wrong behaviour: the "opens, whole" check passes PDFs that are damaged inside. It only requires `%%EOF` in the last 1024 bytes, at least 1 page, and `/Type /Page` on the pages pypdf managed to flatten. It never compares the page count with the page tree's `/Count`. It never resolves `/Contents`, streams or resources. The docstring claims "every page is there".
- Input and result:

  - (b) A PDF whose content or image stream objects are missing or overwritten. The page dictionary is intact, so it is stored.
- The tests only cut the tail, pad the tail, or use a header with nothing behind it. Nothing damages the middle.
- Clause: R1.67 "a damaged PDF is refused"; checklist 1, 6, 12. Confidence 70 (the null-kid skip is pypdf behaviour from knowledge; part (b) is visible in the script). IN this change.
