**M1. Major. api/src/front-office/compliance/identity/routes.ts:33 and :41. The fault is IN this change. Confidence 80.**
- Wrong behaviour: both routes that hand out the identity-document copy ask only for `agent/document:show`. They send the bytes as an attachment without requiring the separate seeded `agent/document:download` key.
- Input and result: a READ_ONLY staff member holds `document:show` but not `document:download` (prisma/migrations/20261114015002_pf3_platform_tables/migration.sql:706-709 against :731-735). Take one who sees the person (firm "everyone sees all" on; READ_ONLY holds `agent/prospect:view.all`). POST `identity-checks/:id/copy/download-link`, then GET `identity-copies/:token`, returns 200 with the full passport file. It should be 403.
- The sibling retainer file routes require their own `agent/retainer:download` key.
- The test hides this. Its READ_ONLY user is tried only against another person's link, with "everyone sees all" off, and BILLING_FINANCE lacks `document:show` altogether.
- Clause: "access only for people who may see the client's documents" and "a person without the key cannot read the copy"; checklist 3, 12.

**M2. Major. api/src/front-office/retainers/service.ts:503-517 (`send`), with `read()` left without the warning. The fault is IN this change. Confidence 60.**
- Wrong behaviour: the missing-check warning is computed inside `send` and returned only after the retainer is already SENT. Nothing shows it before sending.

The draft and edit answers return only `signerFor` warnings. POST send commits SENT and the `retainer.sent` event, and only then answers "No identity check is recorded for …". The sender cannot act on it before the agreement goes out.
- Clause: "before a retainer is sent, a missing check shows a warning, never a block"; checklist 1. (The "allowed with the warning recorded" half is met.)

**M4. Major. api/src/front-office/compliance/identity/service.ts:62-68 and :156-160 (`kindOf`). The fault is IN this change. Confidence 50.**
- Wrong behaviour: the copy is accepted on its first 3 to 8 bytes only. This bypasses the app's shared upload check (api/src/platform/files/file-type.ts, "the one file-type and size check every upload door calls"), which refuses damaged or headerless JPEG and PNG and images over 89.5 megapixels.
- Input and result: a 3-byte file `FF D8 FF`, a bare 8-byte PNG signature, or the 5 bytes `%PDF-` returns 201 with `hasCopy: true`. The database refuses any update or delete of the check, so the unreadable "copy" stays on the compliance record for good.
- The neighbouring signed-offline upload at least requires `%%EOF`.
- Clause: checklist 6 ("refuse … damaged … before anything is stored").
