**M1. Major. api/src/front-office/retainers/service.ts:616-621 (`winProspect`), triggered by api/src/front-office/sales/prospects.ts:201-212 (`move`) and :288-296 (`undoConvert`)**
- **Wrong behaviour:** a stage move or Undo convert that runs at the same time as "Signed offline" makes the signature silently fail to win the prospect.
- **Input or state:** a prospect in ENGAGED with a SENT retainer.
  - T1 is `move` to QUALIFIED and holds the prospect row lock.
  - T2 is `signedOffline`: it sets the retainer to SIGNED_OFFLINE, then runs `select … from fo_prospect p join fo_stage s on s.id = p.stage_id … for update of p` and waits.
  - T1 commits. Postgres re-checks the join against the old `s` row, so no row comes back.
- **Wrong result:**
  - `if (!p …) return false`, and T2 commits. The retainer is SIGNED_OFFLINE, which cannot be undone. The prospect stays open with no WON, no `prospect.won` line and `prospectWon: false`.
  - The same happens when `undoConvert` holds the lock. Its signed count does not see T2's uncommitted update, so Undo succeeds and the signed client ends in an ACTIVE stage.
- **Why it belongs to this change:** the author fixed this exact join-and-lock fault in `locked()` (comment "break M1", lines 164-165) but left the sibling door. This change adds the moves that make it reachable.
- **Clause:** checklist 2 (every door), 10 (concurrency); requirement "Undo convert while the client is empty".
- **Confidence:** 80. Left unaddressed beside the change, newly reachable because of it.

**M4. Major. api/src/front-office/sales/prospects.ts:219-234 (`lost`)**
- **Wrong behaviour:** Mark lost neither ends nor checks the prospect's live retainer, so a lost prospect keeps a signable agreement.
- **Input or state:** (a) a prospect with a SENT or VIEWED retainer is marked lost. (b) A prospect with a CLIENT_SIGNED retainer is marked lost; this file's own `SIGNED` set counts that as signed.
- **Wrong result:**
  - (a) The retainer stays SENT. `Retainers.send`, `edit` and `signedOffline` never check the stage (only `draft` refuses LOST). A later Signed offline flips LOST to WON and wipes the lost reason and note (service.ts:624).
  - (b) A client who has signed is listed as Lost with no refusal or warning, while Convert and Undo treat the same retainer as signed.
- **Clause:** checklist 5 (every exit ends what it started, here and in linked records), 2.
- **Confidence:** 45. IN this change.
