**M1 — Major — ports/jobs.ts:11-18, 36-37 (JobOptions, `cancel`); fake at `queue()` and `cancel()`**
- Wrong behaviour: job idempotency keys and cancellation are not scoped to a firm. The port takes `firmId` but ignores it for uniqueness, and `cancel(key)` takes no firm at all. One firm's job can therefore be silently dropped or cancelled by another firm's. The "key must carry a uuid or the firm id" rule is only a comment: `JobOptions.key` accepts any string of 1–200 characters.
- Concrete case:
  - `enqueue("invoice.reminder", {}, {key:"invoice:INV-0001", firmId:A})`, then the same name and key with `firmId:B`, returns `{queued:false}`. Firm B's job is never stored, and no error is raised.
  - If only B's job is waiting under that key, firm A's code calling `cancel("invoice:INV-0001")` marks B's job cancelled and returns true.
  - No test covers two firms with the same key.
- Checklist: 4 (firm wall), 6 (refuse bad input before it is stored), 12.
- Confidence: 50 (the behaviour is certain; the hazard is acknowledged in a comment and left unenforced, and there is no production caller yet).
- IN this change.
