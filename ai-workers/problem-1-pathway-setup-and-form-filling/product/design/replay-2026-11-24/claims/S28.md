# S28 (blind claims, verbatim; model output)

Findings, S28 (slice-1)

**M1. Major. api/src/platform/billing/exit-notices.ts:46-48 (with exit-clock.ts:42). IN this change.**
- Wrong behaviour: a firm held because its subscription ENDED is told to "add a card or pay the open invoice" and that this "at any time before then restores your account with all its data". Neither step can restore it.
- Input and result: Stripe `canceled` gives subscription ENDED and firm PAYMENT_HOLD (webhook.ts FIRM_OF, hold reason "stripe canceled").
  - exitClock folds every reason except the address one into "payment needed", so all four notices carry this promise.
  - In webhook.ts, SUB_MOVES.ENDED is `[]`. findSubscription excludes ENDED rows for a card Checkout, so useCardAndResume is never called. A linked row refuses any other subscription id. plan-change answers NO_LIVE_SUBSCRIPTION, and no route in api/src creates a new subscription for an existing firm.
  - The firm adds a card, stays on hold and still faces deletion. Firms that chose to cancel get the same text.
- Clause: "cancel", "the hold ending on the paid invoice", the 90-day deletion clock; checklist 1 and 5 (seriously misleading message).
- Confidence 85.

**M2. Major. api/src/platform/billing/exit-clock.ts:35,42, used at exit-notices.ts:46. IN this change (account.ts's hold reason has the same staleness beside it).**
- Wrong behaviour: the notice's instruction comes from the reason on the event that started the hold. webhook.ts:159 writes that event only when the firm status changes, so a cause that changes during the hold gives the wrong remedy.
- Input A: the trial pauses (reason "stripe paused"). The Owner adds a card, and the resumption invoice is a draft with taxNeedsAddress. refresh sets reason ADDRESS_NEEDED and holds the draft, but the firm is already PAYMENT_HOLD, so no new event is written. Days 16, 46 and 83 say "add a card or pay the open invoice". A card exists, the invoice is a draft, and the needed business address is never mentioned.
- Input B: an address hold, the address is saved, the invoice finalizes and its payment fails. Every later warning still says to add the business address and never says to pay.
- Clause: business address step and hold ending on the paid invoice; checklist 5 and 8 (seriously misleading message).
- Confidence 82.

**M3. Major. api/src/platform/billing/exit-notices.ts:71-95. IN this change.**
- Wrong behaviour: a notice that reached nobody is recorded as sent.
- Input and result: a sole-Owner firm whose billing_email equals the Owner's (sign-up's default), with that address suppressed after a hard bounce. Then refused = 1 of 1 and retry = 0.
  - The line "The day-83 deletion warning was sent" is written (after.sent = 0), and runFirm returns sent: 83.
  - exitClock.noticesSent lists the day, so it is never tried again, even if the address or Owner changes the next hour.
  - An empty recipient list (no active Owner or Admin and a null billing_email) does the same with recipients: 0.
  - noticesSent is documented as what the day-90 deletion job reads, so a firm can look fully warned when no one was. No alert is raised.
- Clause: departure notices at days 0, 16, 46, 83; checklist 5 and 7 (wrong result, data at risk).
- Confidence 80.

**M4. Major. api/src/platform/billing/exit-notices.ts:74-92. IN this change.**
- Wrong behaviour: MailPort.send resolving only means the outbox row is QUEUED, but the notice is then permanently recorded as sent. A later delivery failure is never retried or reflected.
- Input and result: the provider is down, so messages.ts countRefusal ends each row FAILED "send failed" after 5 tries. firm.exit_notice_sent is already written, so dueNotice returns null. The fixed idempotency key would return the same FAILED row anyway. The warning is lost for good while noticesSent and audit say "sent". An asynchronous BOUNCED row is the same.
- Related: if a partial-failure retry runs after a row went FAILED, MessagesMail throws MessageRefused. refusedFinally then counts a provider outage as "a suppressed or withdrawn address".
- Clause: checklist 7 (a thrown provider error must reach a correct final state).
- Confidence 72.

**M5. Major. api/src/platform/billing/exit-notices.ts:74. IN this change.**
- Wrong behaviour: the `held` answer (firm daily cap) is ignored, so a held notice is recorded as sent "now" and can go out days later. Other callers check heldOf for this (identity/service.ts:390, email-sender.ts:43).
- Input and result: entering PAYMENT_HOLD drops the cap from 2000 to 50 (DAILY_CAP; only ACTIVE gets 2000). A firm that sent over 50 emails that day, or has a queued backlog, has its notices kept QUEUED "daily limit" until a later day. Audit says sent at the sweep's time.
- Nothing cancels the held row. If the firm pays meanwhile, it still receives "Your subscription is on hold … data will be permanently deleted", contradicting the header's "A hold that ends sends nothing more".
- Clause: notice days 0, 16, 46, 83; checklist 5 and 7.
- Confidence 72.

**Minors**
- m1. exit-notices.ts:4-5: says the date is in the firm's zone "as its billing page shows it", but web billing-contract.ts:40 `day()` uses the browser's zone, so email and page can differ by a day.
- m2. exit-notices.ts:53,79: only MessageRefused is final. An address the DB check accepts but MailMessage's z.email refuses (for example `accounts@localhost`) throws ZodError every hour, so that day's notice is never recorded, against the header's "never holds back the others".
- m3. exit-notices.ts:126: the handler discards sweep()'s `failed` count, including firms whose bypass line could not be written. The job always reports success, with log lines only and no runner retry, dead letter or alert.
- m4. exit-notices.ts:72-82: hold status is not re-read between recipients, so a payment landing mid-loop still mails the rest. The post-send recheck only skips the audit line, leaving sent mails with no record.
- m5. tests/check_pf5_exit_clock.ts uses only fakeMail (always delivered). No test covers MessagesMail's QUEUED, held or FAILED results, all recipients refused, zero recipients, an ended subscription, or a hold whose cause changes.
