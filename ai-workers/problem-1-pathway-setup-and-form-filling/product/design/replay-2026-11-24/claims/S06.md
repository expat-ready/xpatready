**M1. Major. `repo/api/src/case-close.ts:200-212` (`reopen`) and `repo/api/src/case-stages.ts:211-260` (`start`, POST cases/:id/stages/next)**
- **Wrong behaviour:** two other routes turn a non-counting case back into an active one without asking `EntitlementPort.check` or taking the `case-open:<firm>` lock. The limit this change binds in `createCase` can therefore be exceeded.
- **Input and wrong result (reopen):** a firm on a 10-case plan has 10 active. Withdraw one (9 active). Open one through `createCase`: 201, 10 active. Then POST reopen on the withdrawn case: 200, status OPEN. By `case-count.ts` that case counts again, so 11 are active on a limit of 10. Repeating this gives any number.
- **Input and wrong result (next stage):** an OPEN case with every filing of its CURRENT stage decided does not count. Open a replacement up to the limit, then start the first case's next stage. The new CURRENT stage has no filing, so the case counts again and the firm is over its limit, with no 409. The stage also moves `combinationId`, so it can enter the corporate group unchecked.
- **Clause:** checklist 2 (every door). R1.20 "Every gated action asks one check". PF-15c title "a firm's case limits hold". SA-3-39 hard limit.
- **Confidence:** 65. Left unaddressed beside the change; the row's own wording covers only opening a case.
