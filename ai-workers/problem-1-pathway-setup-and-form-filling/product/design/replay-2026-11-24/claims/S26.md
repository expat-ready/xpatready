**M1. Major. api/src/platform/firms/signup.ts:145-164 (signUpWork). IN this change.**
- **Wrong behaviour:** a sign-up with a new email reads staff and firm contacts across every firm on the bypass role. It writes no audit line at all if the identity provider then throws.
- **Input and result:** the email is unknown and `identity.register` throws (provider down, password refused, or, for the local stand-in, its `mail.send` fails). `knownAccount(email)` at the top has already run `platformStaff.findFirst` and `platformFirm.findUnique` under `platform.firms.signup`. The only `touchPlatform` is inside the later pending-row transaction, which is never reached. `later()` only logs a console warning. A cross-firm read happened with 0 `platform.bypass` rows. The code's own comment says the lookup and the pending row are "one step with one line". The line is tied to the row insert, so the lookup alone is unrecorded.
- **Clause:** R1.4 "the bypass role … every use writes an audit line"; checklist 4, 5, 7. The suite only tests an audit-write failure, not a throwing provider.
- **Confidence:** 80.
