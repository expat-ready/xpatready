**What to build, part 1 (batch invite):**
* `POST /api/platform/staff/invitations/batch` (key `agent/staff:manage`, as the single invite; the row says `/api/platform/invitations/batch`, and this path sits beside `main`'s single invite `staff/invitations`, for Lead B to note), body `{ rows: [{ name?, email, roles }] }`, 1-20 rows; no file upload of any kind (AG-1-69).
* **`name`:** `platform_invitation` has no name column and this part changes no schema, so the name is used only in that row's email greeting ("Hello <name>", 1-100 characters, header-safe) and is **never stored**; the person gives their own name when they accept (PF-4b). Lead B confirms, or a later row adds the column.
* Inside **one `inFirm` transaction** (the firm row lock already serialises two batches of one firm, and a batch against a single invite):
  1. every row's email and roles checked as `invite` checks them (`emailOf`, `rolesOf`), but **per row**: a bad email or role list becomes that row's flag (`INVALID_EMAIL`, `INVALID_ROLES`), never a 400 for the whole form; `roleRules` run per row (an inviter never gives a role they may not give). **No Owner in a batch:** a row giving Owner is flagged `OWNER_BY_SINGLE_INVITE` ("Invite an Owner on their own: it needs a reason and Confirm it's you"). This is a new rule for the batch only, from PF-6's "no Owner at invite"; on `main` an Owner may invite an Owner through the single invite with a reason and step-up (`check_pf4_identity.ts` ~355 expects 201), and that stays unchanged;
  2. **per-row flags:** the same email twice in the form (`DUPLICATE_IN_FORM`, flagged on the second and later rows), already on staff (`ALREADY_STAFF`), already invited (`ALREADY_INVITED`), as `invite`'s checks;
  3. **one seat check for the whole form:** `seatCheck(tx, firmId, n)` with `n` = the rows that would be sent. Over the limit: 409 `PLAN_LIMIT_REACHED` with `seatsLimit`, `seatsUsed`, `afterInvites` and `removeCount` ("7 of 10 seats after these invites"; "Remove 1 person or upgrade your plan"); **nothing is created**.
  4. **Any flagged row → nothing is sent** (422 `ROWS_FLAGGED` with every row's flag), so the screen shows all problems at once; with no flags, one invitation per row (`insertInvitation`) and one audit line per row (`auditInvitation`), all in the transaction.
* After the commit, one email per row by `emailInvitation` (PF-4b's after-commit send; a failed send leaves that invitation pending, `emailed: false` on that row). This is how "outbox rows last" in the row reads here: identity writes no outbox row for an invitation on `main` (only an audit line), and this part adds none.
* `seatCheck` and the per-row checks stay one code path with `invite` (refactor `invite` to call the same row check; no copy).
