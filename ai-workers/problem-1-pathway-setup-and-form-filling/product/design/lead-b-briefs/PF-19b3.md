## What `main` has (15c75cd52; so nothing is built twice)
* **Schema:** `MsgOutboxState` already has `DELIVERED`, and `MsgEventKind` already has `DELIVERED` and `OPENED` (`platform-messages.prisma` lines 98-115). **No schema change and no migration.**
* **Bounces:** `channels/email/bounces.ts` handles SES `Bounce` and `Complaint` notices at `POST /api/public/messages/ses-notices`. A hard bounce sets `BOUNCED` and suppresses the address. Every other kind answers `"ignored"` (line 88). **Nothing writes `DELIVERED`.**
* **Outbox rows** carry `subject_kind` and `subject_id`, which name the record the message was sent from.
* **No staff route or screen shows a message's state.** The demo inbox (`demo-inbox.ts`) lists SENT rows only.

## What to build
1. **Delivery notices:** `bounces.ts` also accepts SES `Delivery`:
   * the outbox row moves `SENT → DELIVERED`;
   * one `msg_event` `DELIVERED`, idempotent by `providerEventId`;
   * a Delivery that arrives after a Bounce never overwrites `BOUNCED`. States only move forward: `QUEUED → SENT → DELIVERED`, and `BOUNCED`/`FAILED` are final;
   * the same signature and topic checks as Bounce, unchanged.

   Rename the file only if the reviewer asks.
2. **Test events on the demo copy:** the local adapter (`channels/email/local.ts`) emits a Delivery notice for each sent message through the same handler, so the demo shows `DELIVERED` without SES. The fake provider's test event is the "provider's test events" in R1.48. Addresses on a Sample bounce list (one constant in the test seed) emit a hard Bounce instead.
3. **The staff read:** `GET /api/platform/messages?subjectKind=&subjectId=` (paged 50, newest first). Each message has its purpose, class, channel, the address masked to its first letter and domain, the state, the state reason, the events with times, and the from-name.
   * It checks that the caller may see the subject: a `FO_PERSON` or lead through the front-office `show` key and its own-or-all rule, a case through `agent/case:show`. It adds **no new permission key**.
   * Another firm's subject, or one the person may not see, answers 404 with the same words.
   * The body text is never returned. Transactional bodies stay sealed (PF-19e).
4. **The staff view:** a small `MessagesPanel` in new `web/src/modules/platform/messages/` that lists the read above, with the state as a word and a quiet colour (never colour alone).
   * It is **mounted on the front-office lead page** (`web/src/modules/front-office/sales/lead-page.tsx`, one line), because that is the record J-48 walks.
   * Mounting it on the case page is the case side's line, for Build Lead C to add in its own row.
