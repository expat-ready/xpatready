## Part 1: plan changes and trial controls (`runtime: PF-16 part 1 — plan changes and trial controls`)
The BACKLOG row's rules for:
* `POST /api/platform/billing/change-plan`, Owner only, with a preview from the upcoming invoice;
* a raise applies at once with proration;
* same or lower books for the renewal with no refund, only after `checkDowngrade` passes;
* monthly → yearly at once with a fresh year; yearly → monthly at the anniversary;
* a trial switch is immediate and free, and the trial end is unchanged;
* **"Start my subscription now"** (`agent/subscription:upgrade`, card required). A failure leaves the trial as it was and tells the Owner;
* `GET /api/platform/billing/banner`;
* the Customer Portal's plan switching is **turned off** in the Stripe set-up script, while card, invoices and cancel stay there.

**Touch list:** `api/src/platform/billing/plan-change/*.ts`, `api/src/features/platform-billing-rules.ts`, the set-up script under `api/src/platform/billing/`, `tests/check_pf16_billing_rules.ts` (part 1 cases).

## Part 2: billing emails (`runtime: PF-16 part 2 — billing emails`)
Fixed ExpatReady templates (English, versioned in code; firms cannot edit them) go to SA-4-53's two recipient sets:
* plan change;
* lower plan booked;
* subscription started early;
* trial extended;
* card expiring in 30 days (a PF-14 job);
* cancellation confirmation;
* yearly → monthly.

They are **sent through `MESSAGES_MAIL` as TRANSACTIONAL** (the one sending path; PF-19e seals them). Stripe Smart Retries are set within 14 days by the set-up script.

**Touch list:** `api/src/platform/billing/emails/*.ts`, the job's registration line, `tests/check_pf16_billing_rules.ts` (part 2 cases).

## Done when

* an Admin changing the plan → 403;
* a raise is charged once;
* a lower plan with too many staff → refused with the conflict;
* a lower plan books for the renewal and the lower limits apply at once;
* monthly → yearly starts a fresh year;
* a trial switch charges nothing and keeps the end date;
* "Start now" with no card → refused;
* a failed early start leaves the trial unchanged;
* each notice goes to the right recipient set, once (fake clock);
* the card-expiring notice goes once, 30 days before.
