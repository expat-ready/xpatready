* **Confirm a check with hidden matches** `POST …/:id/confirm` (`agent/conflict:check`, by **another** active staff member who may see the hidden matches): gives the result (and disclosure text) once, with the confirmation; `NO_CONFLICT` is refused until confirmed (`fo_conflict_check_hidden_confirmed_check`). The checker confirming their own check → 403. The written-consent reference is set once (the signed retainer's id), never changed.
* **Gates on engagement** (the row; supported by t8 §1b as above): `POST retainers/:id/send` and `…/signed-offline` (and the prospect convert, when on `main`) refuse without a recorded check for that party (409 with the reason and the section), and refuse on `CONFLICT_CANNOT_ACT`; `POTENTIAL_CONFLICT` fills the retainer's s. 24(3)(o) item through a known placeholder `conflict.disclosure` (added to `placeholders.ts`); a change to the party's name, email or phone after the check asks for a new one.
* **A conflict found after signing** (rev 16.42; s. 35(1)(c) is the representative's decision): a new check on an existing client that gives `POTENTIAL_CONFLICT` or `CONFLICT_CANNOT_ACT` is shown on the person and on the case for the representative; nothing ends by itself.
