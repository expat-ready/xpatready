**What to build:**
* **Part 1, the registry and publishing (schema + runtime).** `TemplateRegistry` (in `api/src/platform/messages/templates/registry.ts`): a module registers a template kind with its fields (name, label, source record, `sensitive: true` for passport and financial fields, which can never be registered for EMAIL or WHATSAPP, SA5-D29) and its must-keep fields. The platform registers `firm.email_signature` (rev 16.40, read from PF-13's setting; empty until PF-13 lands) and the firm's name and contact fields. **Drafts in their own table:** `msg_template_version` is write-once on `main` (`20261115004500_pf19a_platform_messages/migration.sql`, trigger `msg_template_version_write_once BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE`; `published_by`/`published_at` NOT NULL), so a draft is never a version row: a new `msg_template_draft` (firm, **nullable for drafts of ExpatReady's own templates**, as `msg_template` and `msg_template_version` have; template, subject, body, must-keep, edited by, edited at; editable, never sent) in one migration in `platform-messages.prisma` (P-600), and **publishing inserts a new `msg_template_version` row** from the draft and removes the draft. PF-19a's trigger is not changed. A send only ever uses a version row (AG-12-65). Routes under `/api/platform/templates` (seeded on `main` by PF-3: `agent/template:list` and `:show` to read, `:edit` (and `:create` for a new firm kind) to draft, `:publish` to publish, `:preview` for preview and test, `:view-usage` for where-used): copy-on-edit of an ExpatReady template into a firm draft (AG-12-45), publish with SA-5-25's firm refusals (unknown field, a field not allowed in the channel, no English body, a must-keep field removed), each with the reason; a published version never changes. * **Part 2, preview and test (runtime).** `POST …/:id/preview` with `{ record?: { kind, id } }`: the record must be one the person may open (each module's read check through the registry; else the one not-found); without one, a Sample record (marked Sample); the answer shows the filled subject and body, missing must-fill fields highlighted, and **every value HTML-escaped** in the HTML body (rev 16.25: a value with `<script>` renders as text). `POST …/:id/test`: sends the previewed message to the caller's own staff email through `MessagePort` (TRANSACTIONAL) with "[Test]" at the start of the subject; one line on the template's history; never on a client record.
