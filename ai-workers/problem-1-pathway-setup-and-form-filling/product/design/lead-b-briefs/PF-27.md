**What to build, part 1:**
* **Routes** (new `api/src/platform/firm-lock/*.ts`, registered in new `api/src/features/platform-firm-lock.ts`): `POST /api/system/firms/:id/lock`, `/unlock`, `/close`, each with **the lock key PF-4s settles** (the row says `system/security:lock`; PF-4s's brief may seed `system/firm:suspend`/`reinstate` from SA-07 Appendix A, and SA-02 §10 has a separate `system/firm:close`: follow PF-4s's outcome; for Lead B to confirm) (Platform Owner only, PF-4s's system key table) and a **reason** (`reasonOf`'s 1-200 characters, `identity/service.ts` ~66-72, reused; none → 400). A Support or Billing person → 403.
* **Lock:** in one transaction under the firm's row lock: the firm → `SECURITY_LOCK` from `TRIAL`, `ACTIVE` or `PAYMENT_HOLD` (others refused: `DELETED`, `PROVISIONING`, `ABANDONED`, already locked → 409 with words), **the prior state kept** in new `platform_firm_lock` (firm_id, `prior_status`, **`hold_days_remaining`** (when locked from `PAYMENT_HOLD`: the days left on PF-5's 90-day hold clock, so the countdown is **paused**, SA-02 §6), `locked_at`, `locked_by`, `reason`, `lifted_at`, `closed_at`, `closure_due_at`; own area file `prisma/schema/platform-firm-lock.prisma`, named `platform_*` for PF-12's `FAMILIES`, with its policy; the reason is ours and is never shown to the firm), **one `platform_session_revocation` row per staff member of the firm** (reason `security-lock`), so every live token answers 401 on its next request; one audit line **on our own staff's chain with the reason**, and an ids-only line on the firm's chain (firm staff may read their own chain, and SA-2-34 keeps the reason from them); `EVENT.FIRM_LOCKED` outbox row last. After the commit, the Owner's **"Account security-locked"** email by `MailPort` (neutral: no reason, no detail; the words are ours, labelled Sample until SA-05's text is read).
* **Staff sign-in refused while locked:** `PlatformIdentity.localSignIn` (`identity/service.ts` ~139-153; `session/service.ts`'s `callback` only calls it, so that file is not touched) and the token check (`identity/service.ts` ~96-115) refuse a `SECURITY_LOCK` firm's staff with a plain message ("This firm's account is locked. Contact ExpatReady support."), never the reason. Both already select the firm in their query (the token check ~102, the sign-in ~142), so adding `status` to those selects costs no extra round trip.
* **Unlock:** the state comes from **the subscription's current state, not blindly from `prior_status`** (billing may have moved the firm to hold while it was locked, #1959's breaker m8): in hold → `PAYMENT_HOLD` with the hold clock **resumed from `hold_days_remaining`** (SA-02 §6); otherwise `prior_status` (`TRIAL` stays `TRIAL`). `lifted_at` set, audit lines as for the lock, `EVENT.FIRM_UNLOCKED`; staff sign in again (their old tokens stay revoked).
* **Close:** only on a locked firm; sets `closed_at` and `closure_due_at = closed_at + 90 days` (database clock); the firm stays `SECURITY_LOCK`; audit line; `EVENT.FIRM_CLOSED` (ids and the due date only); the Owner's email that a full export may be requested within the 90 days (SA-2-37). **A legal hold pauses the clock**: PF-11c reads `LegalHoldPort.isHeld` when it runs, so this row only records the due date. **No countdown runs while a firm is locked and not closed** (SA-2-34).
* **Events:** `FIRM_LOCKED`, `FIRM_UNLOCKED`, `FIRM_CLOSED` added to `ports/events.ts` (lines only) with ids-only payloads.
