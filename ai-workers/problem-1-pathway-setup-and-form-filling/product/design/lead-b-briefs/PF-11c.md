* **Schema:** own area file `prisma/schema/platform-firm-closure.prisma` and one migration folder: `platform_firm_deletion` (firm_id unique, `deleted_at`, `archive_keys` text[], `modules_purged` jsonb (counts only), `backup_expires_at` = `deleted_at` + **35 days** (SA-2-21), `reason`: `PAYMENT_HOLD_90 | CANCEL_90 | CLOSED_LOCK_90`), and `platform_firm_closure_block` (firm_id unique, `alerted_at`: the job's own once-only mark, so one alert is raised per blocked firm, not one per daily run); named `platform_*` for PF-12's `FAMILIES` (the row's `plat_firm_deletion` would not be checked; say so in the PR), with its policy; back-relation lines under a new slot comment in `platform.prisma`. It holds no personal data.
* **The daily job** (`JobPort.recurring`, a time outside 01:00-03:00): finds firms whose clock has run out: `PAYMENT_HOLD` (or cancelled) for 90 days of **hold time** (PF-5's clock, **excluding days the firm spent locked**: PF-27 pauses it and keeps `hold_days_remaining`, SA-02 §6), or a closed lock past `closure_due_at` (PF-27); **and `DELETED` firms with no `platform_firm_deletion` row** (a purge that stopped half way is finished on the next run). Per firm, in order, stopping at the first gate that fails:
  1. **Legal hold:** **any active hold with this `firm_id`, whatever its scope** (firm, case, person, category or date range; SA-2-21: "refuses while **any** active legal hold exists on the firm"; SA-11-18). PF-21b's `isHeld` with `{ kind: "FIRM" }` matches only firm-scoped holds, so this needs a query such as `LegalHoldPort.anyInFirm(tx, firmId)` (contract gap, for PF-21b's owner). Held → nothing removed, the clock **paused** (an audit line `firm.closure_held`), resumes when every hold is released.
  2. **The archive copy:** the firm's full export (PF-11's job, the same package) written to the write-once archive through `AuditArchivePort` at `firms/<firmId>/export-<date>.zip`, kept as the conflict note under the rules says until Lead B and the founder settle it (floored classes only; the full copy no longer than `backup_expires_at`); **and the notices for its reason recorded as sent**: for `PAYMENT_HOLD_90` and `CANCEL_90`, the notices PF-5 sends (the PF-5 row's day-0/30/60/85; SA-2-38 and SA-02 §3.4 name days 16, 46 and 83: PF-5's row and the requirement differ, a question for Lead B, and this job checks whatever PF-5 records); for `CLOSED_LOCK_90`, **PF-27's close email** (the only notice a closed lock sends). Missing any → nothing removed, `AlertPort.raise('firm_closure_blocked', …)` once (a new kind added to `ALERT_KINDS` in `ports/alert.ts`; the once-only mark in `platform_firm_closure_block`), and the job waits.
  3. **Close:** the firm → `DELETED`; every staff session ended (a `platform_session_revocation` row per staff member) and every client session (through the portal's port once PF-4p exists; until then, staff only, said in the PR); sign-in refused.
  4. **Purge:** each registered purger runs in its own transaction (so one module's failure stops the rest and is retried next run, never half a module; the job picks the firm up again because it is `DELETED` with no deletion row), **children before parents**, asking `KeepPeriodPort` for floors: a floored class stays in the archive copy, not in the live database.
  5. **Record:** `platform_firm_deletion` written, and **a deletion manifest** (firm id, deleted at, reason, the deletion row's fields; no personal data) written to the write-once archive through `AuditArchivePort` (`firms/deletions/<firmId>.json`), so a restored database can learn of it; `platform_firm` row kept (status `DELETED`); **audit lines are never purged** (BR-1, BR-8) and the firm's chain still verifies.
