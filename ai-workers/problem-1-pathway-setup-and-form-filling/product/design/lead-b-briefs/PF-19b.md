**Lead B ruling (platform contract §9 owner), from a check of `main`:** `msg_outbox` has `rendered_hash` but **no subject or body column**, so the worker cannot rebuild a message after commit. **The rendered message (subject, text, HTML, attachment keys) is stored through `FilesPort` at `msg/<firm id>/outbox/<outbox id>.json` inside the caller's transaction step, before the outbox row is committed**, as PF-19m stores email bodies; the worker reads it, checks its SHA-256 against `rendered_hash`, and on a mismatch marks the row `FAILED` (reason "content changed") and sends nothing. No schema change, so no schema baton. If FilesPort cannot be written in the caller's transaction, write it first and let a rolled-back transaction leave an orphan file that a later sweep may remove (no send, since no outbox row); say which in the PR.
