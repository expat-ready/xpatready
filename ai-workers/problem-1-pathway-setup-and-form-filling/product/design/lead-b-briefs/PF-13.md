Rules:
  - a colour whose contrast on white is under 4.5:1 is refused with the ratio, e.g. "Contrast 3.1:1 on white; at least 4.5:1 is needed.";
  - images: PNG or JPEG only, checked by their first bytes, not the name; **metadata stripped by walking the file's structure, no new library** (Lead B, rev 16.40): JPEG keeps SOI, APP0 (JFIF), APP14 (Adobe), DQT, SOFn, DHT, DRI, SOS with its scan data, and EOI, and drops APP1-APP13, APP15 (EXIF, XMP, GPS) and COM; PNG keeps IHDR, PLTE, IDAT, IEND, tRNS, cHRM, gAMA, iCCP, sRGB and pHYs and drops every other chunk (eXIf, tEXt, iTXt, zTXt, tIME, unknown); a JPEG with an arithmetic-coded frame (SOF9-SOF11, SOF13-SOF15, which need DAC) → 415; bytes after JPEG's EOI or PNG's IEND are dropped (the polyglot tail); a file whose structure does not parse → 415; images are served with `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`; no re-encoding until a real need; **SVG is refused** (the row's "no SVG scripts" is met by not taking SVG at all; re-encoding needs an image library, which is not in `package.json` and needs the STANDARDS §1 gate: a PF-L-style row if Lead B wants re-encoding);
  - logo at most 2 MB, favicon at most 256 KB (Sample limits); larger → 413 naming the limit;
