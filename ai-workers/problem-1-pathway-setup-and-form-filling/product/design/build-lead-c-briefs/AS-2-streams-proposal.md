## The card (one question, for the founder)
**"Which provincial streams should the Release 1 assessment cover, one per province, assessment only (no forms)?"**
1. **(Recommended) As proposed:**
   * Ontario: Workforce Priority (points).
   * British Columbia: Skilled Worker (score out of 200).
   * Saskatchewan: International Skilled Worker (60 of 110 to apply).
   * Alberta: Opportunity Stream as eligibility only (Alberta publishes no points).
   * Manitoba: Skilled Worker, with values Sample until its page can be read.
2. **As proposed, but Alberta through its Express Entry stream** on the federal score (minimum 300), instead of the Opportunity Stream.
3. **Other streams:** the founder names them.

**Why 1:** every stream is open today and published by its own province. Four of the five are read and cited. Alberta's own pages give requirements but no points, so "eligibility only" is honest rather than invented. Manitoba is the one gap, labelled until read.
