# The College's file-keeping periods and licence number, read at source (Lead B (4), 15 Nov 2026)

Asked by Lead 52 (S1 question 6; open item 16; P-579, AGENTS rule 4). Read from the Mac on 15 Nov 2026, 09:2x-09:3x IST. **Legal points: Unconfirmed: no counsel.** In particular, these are 2021-001 regulations first issued by the College's predecessor council. They are still published on the College's own site and are enacted under its by-law, but whether each remains in force unchanged is a legal reading.

## 1. Client files: six years after the file is closed

* **Source:** College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants, *Client File Management Regulation* 2021-001, https://college-ic.ca/ICCRC/Assets/Documents/Regulations/Client%20File%20Management%20Regulation.pdf (PDF dated 4 Mar 2022, read 15 Nov 2026). s. 1.1: "enacted pursuant to section 3.1 of the By-law".
* **s. 7.2 (page 7):** "A Client File shall be retained in a secure and accessible location for a minimum of six (6) years after the File has been closed."
* **s. 7.1, when a file may be closed:**
  * (a) fees or refunds are settled;
  * (b) the client's property is returned;
  * (c) the client is told in writing that the retainer has ended and that their documents and property were returned.
* **s. 7.3:** keep a list of closed files with their closing dates. **s. 7.4:** set a projected destruction date when the file closes, and record it in the file.
* **s. 10.1, a record of destroyed files:** the client's name and address, a brief description of the service, the closing date and the actual destruction date.
* **Why the Code points here:** Code SOR/2022-128 s. 37 makes a licensee "keep and preserve records in accordance with the by-laws made under the Act" (laws-lois.justice.gc.ca, current to 2026-10-19, read 15 Nov 2026). The Code itself sets no number of years.
* **Checked and found silent:** the new *College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants Regulations* SOR/2026-68 (current to 2026-10-19, read 15 Nov 2026) set no keep period for licensees' files.

## 2. Client (trust) account: monthly reconciliation, records kept six years

* **Source:** *Client Account Regulation* 2021-001, https://college-ic.ca/ICCRC/Assets/Documents/Regulations/Client%20Account%20Regulation%202021-001-Final-EN.pdf (PDF dated 2 Mar 2022, read 15 Nov 2026). s. 1.1: enacted under s. 3.1 of the By-law.
* **s. 9.1(a):** a licensee "shall reconcile each month's balances of all Client Accounts … promptly and in no event later than ninety (90) calendar days after each month's end."
* **s. 9.1(b):** each reconciliation and its supporting documents (bank statements and the list of money held per client) are kept "for no less than six (6) years".

## 3. The licence number

* **The Code calls it the "registration number."** Code s. 23(2)(a) (consultation agreement) and s. 24(3)(a) (service agreement) each require "the licensee's name, registration number, address, telephone number and email address".
* **The College's public register calls it the "College ID"** (https://register.college-ic.ca/, read 15 Nov 2026: search by College ID; results show it). **The register publishes no format.**
* **SOR/2026-68 s. 22(c)** (not yet in force; it comes into force on the first anniversary of registration, s. 57(2)) lists "the licensee's identification number" in the register, with no format.
* **Secondary only, not at source:** consultant firms' pages describe it as "R" followed by six digits. That is **Sample**, not a rule.

## What this changes in the rows

1. **AUD-5 and PF-11c (keep periods):**
   * Client files: **6 years after the file is closed**, cited above. The clock starts at closing, which needs all three s. 7.1 conditions met.
   * Trust reconciliations and their supporting documents: **6 years**.
   * The 7-year audit and financial floor already in the rows is longer, so it stands.
   * The rows should record the projected destruction date at closing (s. 7.4) and keep a record of destroyed files (s. 10.1). That applies to the case side's documents (CS-D4, a reminder only, never automatic deletion) and to PF-11c's deletion record.
2. **FO-7m (monthly trust check):** the College's own regulation requires a monthly reconciliation within 90 days of each month's end (s. 9.1(a)). This strengthens Lead B's Q1 ruling: the screen should show months not yet reconciled and how many days remain of the 90.
3. **Licence number (PF-3/PF-4c, FO-3v step 0, the s. 23/24 agreement fields):**
   * The label is **"College ID (registration number)"**.
   * It is stored as text, trimmed and upper-cased.
   * A soft hint, never a block, appears when it is not "R" followed by six digits. That format is Sample until the College publishes one.
   * Every consultation and service agreement prints it (s. 23(2)(a), s. 24(3)(a)). FO-5b's s. 24(3) checklist already covers that.
