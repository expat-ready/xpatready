**Where the requirement or the code corrects or narrows the row (the release Lead confirms each in the approval):**
* **A decision does not close the file.** The row's test "`case.closed` is written once with reason `DECISION` or `WITHDRAWAL`" does not match AG-17 §6.A. There, a decision moves the file to Decision and then After decision; the file closes later, by staff with a reason (AG17-D30) or by itself when the after-decision items are done. CS-C1a has the same rule (a decision closes nothing; `close` needs every filing decided and a reason). So: **Withdraw** writes `case.closed` (`WITHDRAWAL`, CS-C1a's code). A decision writes the decision and moves positions, and no close. Closing an LMIA file is CS-C1a's close route, and this row adds what **AG-17-86@bde8bcc0** requires on close: every still-open position becomes `LAPSED` or `NOT_APPROVED`, and reminders on recorded dates keep working.
* **Staff choose which positions are not approved.** **AG-17-39@7f769295**: "staff SHALL choose which specific positions are marked 'Not approved'; the remainder become Approved". A named worker on a not-approved position is unlinked (AG-17-43). A `REQUESTED_WITH_WORKER` position that is approved becomes `WORKER_ASSIGNED` (§6.B). The decision takes `not_approved: [position ids]`, whose count must equal requested − approved.
* **Refused or withdrawn moves every Requested position** (**AG-17-85@2de08d79**): each becomes `NOT_APPROVED` and any named worker is unlinked.
* **A DECISION row needs a filing.** CS-C1a's `case_decision` refuses a `DECISION` without `filing_id` (migration check: `kind = 'DECISION' AND filing_id IS NOT NULL`). The LMIA file's filing is the one CS-98b's step 0 confirmed. If that step found no filing, this row cannot start (stop and ask).
* **The decision goes through CS-C1a's service, not a copy.** `CaseClose` (`api/src/case-close.ts`) opens its own transaction per route. This row needs the decision and the position moves in **one** transaction, so `CaseClose` gains one exported method that takes the caller's `tx` (`recordDecisionIn(tx, ctx, caseId, body)`), and its route calls it. That is a hook in CS-C1a's file. **Question for the tech lead:** OK this hook (recommended), or a second decision path here (a duplicate of CS-C1a's checks).
* **Submission uses C8.2's record.** **AG-17-36@9b097e82** asks for date, application number, the submitted application copy (uploaded, never re-typed), other documents and submitter. `FilingRecord` (`submission.prisma:51-75`: `submitted_on`, `application_number`, `fee_receipt`) and `api/src/submission.ts`'s routes already record this per filing. This row calls them and adds the LMIA's own effects: the clock reads "satisfied" (§6.C), positions stop changing, and the "Fee: awaiting request" item opens (AG-17-42). Missing sign-off asks **one confirmation** and never blocks (AG17-D46, §6.A).
* **The fee is narrower and richer than the row.** **AG-17-42@9a73ca04**:
  * the processing fee is **positions × the current fee, less exemptions**, in the case's Government fees section;
  * it carries a payer (employer, or the firm on the employer's behalf), a status (due → paid → receipt) and the receipt;
  * **only if the firm paid**, one action creates a non-taxable government-fee line on the employer's invoice and a linked AG-06 `Expense`;
  * submission creates a "Fee: awaiting request" item;
  * a MAJOR warning appears only if a logged due date passes unpaid;
  * nothing appears before submission.
  The row's "offered to client money as a bill-to-employer line" therefore applies only when the firm paid. The fee stays with the employer and never goes on a worker's case (AG-17 BR-15). The fee's state needs a table. Part 3 adds `corp_government_fee` to `case-corporate.prisma` with its own migration (additive, P-600 (b)).
* **The validity clock has no reminder while its length is Sample.** **AG-17-40@86898d99** starts the clock on approval, with its length from the validity table and reminders at lead times. P-412 F13 (b) says a worked-out Sample date is shown only, with no reminder until the rule is cited. So with a Sample validity row, the end date shows "Sample" and nothing is registered. With a cited row, the reminders are calendar-date reminders (AG-17-73, Lead 61) that **survive the file's close** (AG-17-86: reminders on recorded dates keep working).
* **Officer contact makes a task** (**AG-17-37@d4fd3289**): a request with a response deadline creates "a dated task for the file owner". That is PF-29b's `TaskPort.create` with CS-T1's case host, which is why part 2 waits for them.
* **Re-apply follows refusal or withdrawal** (**AG-17-41@d07d4284**). It makes a new file on the **current** pack version, with an optional Copy from a previous LMIA (CS-98b part 2's copy) and a `REAPPLICATION_OF` link. Unlike Change stream, it is a new active file, so CS-C1b's plan-limit check applies. The refusal's root cause (pack list) and note are optional.
