* **The words (SA-2-38, SA2-D13, same file lines 476 and 65):** only our own facts: the deletion date and where to download or pay, plus "keep your own copy for as long as your professional rules require". * **The daily job:** a `JobPort.recurring` sweep (outside 01:00-03:00) over held firms, with its `job:<queue>` entry in `BYPASS_USES` (`firm-scope.ts`) and its reason. Each due notice is sent once, keyed `firm-exit:<firmId>:<holdEventSeq>:day-<n>`, and recorded by one audit line `firm.exit_notice_sent` (actor `system:<door>`; day, deletion date). A notice missed while the job was down is sent on the next run, only the newest due one, never one after the deletion date. A hold that ends (paid → `ACTIVE`) sends nothing more; a later hold starts a new clock with its own day 0. A firm locked, deleted or active gets none (PF-27 owns the lock's pause).
