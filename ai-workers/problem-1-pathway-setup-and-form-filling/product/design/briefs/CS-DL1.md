* Row (1) gives a stage's or milestone's **deadline** a counting mode `CALENDAR | WORKING`. **AG-7-94@2b63ee12** rules 1-2: a government or legal date is a calendar date that business hours and closed days **never move**; rule 5: only a reminder (or an approval due date) set as "N working days before" is counted back over open days. **AG-7-96@894c1c8b** rule 2: a deadline is the anchor plus N **calendar** days. * Row (3) says closing cancels the reminders. **AG-7-34@8e902706** rule 3: on closing, pending **case** reminders stop, but reminders on **recorded dates** (a permit expiry) keep working; edge "Duplicate beats a miss: a reminder on a recorded date is kept". **AG-7-13@66abfeb9** rule 5: on reopening, reminders resume. This brief stops the stage-deadline reminders on close (they are case clocks) and leaves no recorded-date reminder to cancel (none exists on `main`). **Question for the tech lead:** is a confirmed government deadline (AG-7-96, when `CaseMilestone` exists) a "recorded date" that survives a close? ## Part 2 (CS-DL1b): reminders as jobs, stopped by a close and resumed by a reopen (expected ~450 lines)
* **Reminders:** for each current stage's deadline with a date, one `JobPort` job per reminder, key `deadline:<case>:<stage>:<offset>`, run at 09:00 in the firm's zone on the day `countBack` gives. Offsets **Sample: 7 and 2 working days before, and on the day** (the day itself: the last open day on or before it, AG-7-94 r6). Recipients: the case's `OFFICER` and `LEAD_REPRESENTATIVE` team members (`CaseTeamMember`). Each job calls `NotificationPort.notify(tx, { staffId }, NOTIFICATION_KIND.<deadline kind>, { kind: "case-stage", id })` once (AG-13-15), from `ports/notifications.ts` (PF-18np); this row adds its kind to `NOTIFICATION_KIND` (never a string literal). A re-run or a redelivered job sends nothing new (the job key, and the port's one per person, per item and kind). The port promises no delivery: never assume the notification is shown.
* **Skip-if-stale at fire time (AG-13-14):** a job whose case is closed, whose stage is no longer current, or whose deadline date changed does nothing.
* **Close and reopen (AG-7-34 r3, AG-7-13 r5):** a consumer of `case.closed` cancels the case's pending deadline jobs; a consumer of `case.reopened` re-arms the jobs still to come; a reminder whose day has passed never fires late (AG-7-101 r5's rule, applied the same way). A deadline whose date changes (a key detail re-recorded) re-arms with the new date.
