**Where the requirement and `main` correct or narrow the row (the release Lead and Lead B confirm each in the approval):**
* **There is no current requirement card for plans**, only the departure. AG-06 v2.1 withdrew them (trace t6, line 56: "withdrawn by the requirement itself (H:65, A:1200 rule c)"). The older Doc-3 (`01-techno-functional/05-Doc-3-Agent-Console.md` §3.3, lines 2660-2683, and its §2 list) describes a `PaymentPlan` with ordered `Installment` rows, "draw mode" and "single-invoice mode", and a key `agent/billing:plan-manage` (line 2995). P-578 fixes the shape: **instalments on one issued invoice** (Doc-3's single-invoice mode); no draw mode, no plan seeded from a retainer schedule (P-578 says "invoice for services given"; a retainer deposit is never one).
* **The key is `agent/invoice:issue`, and no key is added** (Lead B, rev 16.36, #1879 comment 6901053560): AG-06's D3 withdrew plans together with their own key `agent/payment-plan:manage`, and **P-578** is the recorded departure that keeps plans; it brings back the plans, not a catalogue key. `agent/invoice:issue` is in PF-3's seeded catalogue on `main`. Create, cancel and staff Stop autopay all check it; the client's Stop goes by the pay-link token. (Doc-3 line 2995 spells the old key `agent/billing:plan-manage`; either way it is not added.)
* **Instalment status is derived, never stored** (AG-6-1 rule 7's principle, FO-7a's design): instalment *k* is **Paid** when the invoice's net paid (payments less refunds and reversals, plus trust applied) reaches the sum of instalments 1..*k*; **Due** on its date; **Overdue** the day after its date in the firm's time zone (`platform.time_zone`), or, with autopay, after its retry fails; **Upcoming** otherwise. So any payment (a card by link, an offline payment, Pay from trust on an earned invoice) counts toward the earliest instalments, and a credit note or write-off shrinks the last instalments first. **Question for Lead B:** confirm that Pay from trust on a planned invoice is allowed and counts (recommended: it is a payment of an issued invoice; "never on trust money" means a plan never charges, collects into or is built on trust, P-567 (3)).
* **What "the amount" of an instalment link is.** The link's hosted payment is for that instalment's due part (its cumulative scheduled amount less net paid, never more than the invoice's balance due), not "the full amount due" (departure above). FO-7ts part 1 stores **one** `pay_link_token_hash` per invoice (rev 16.35 (b)), so the instalment link is the invoice's link and the pay page computes the due instalment from facts at the moment it opens; a separate link per instalment would need a token table: see Contract gap.
* **Never on a deposit, a trust request or trust money** (P-578, P-567): a plan on an invoice FO-7b's predicate calls a deposit → refused; a trust request is not an invoice (its id → 404); a card is never saved for or charged into trust (`received_into = OPERATING` always).
* **Pay links "by email or WhatsApp"**: through `MessagePort.send` (PF-19b part 1, on `main`; `Channel` `EMAIL | WHATSAPP` in `ports/consent.ts`), class `TRANSACTIONAL`. WhatsApp needs FO-11 (the channel's adapter, opt-in): until FO-11 merges, email only, and a firm's WhatsApp choice is not offered. Never SMS (P-574).
* **Consent "wording versioned"**: `ConsentPort`'s wording store is for messages (purposes `SERVICE | MARKETING`), not a card-on-file authority, so the autopay consent is client money's own record: the wording is a committed text file per version, its SHA-256 stored with the consent. A card is saved only for a plan whose consent row exists before the hosted payment is created; the webhook stores the saved method only when the payment names that consent.
* **Routes** under **`/api/front-office/money/`** (Lead B, rev 16.33); the client's public pay page is FO-7c's `/api/public/pay/:token` and FO-7s4's screen.
* **Events** (AG-6-33 rule 3: names by the backend lead): proposed `payment_plan.created`, `payment_plan.cancelled`, `payment_plan.instalment_overdue`, `payment_plan.autopay_stopped`; card charges reuse `payment.succeeded` and `payment.failed` (FO-7b, FO-7c). Lead B confirms.
* **What `main` holds:** FO-7a's money tables (`money_invoice`, `money_payment` with `stripe_payment_intent_id` and `idempotency_key` unique per firm); `ports/messages.ts`, `ports/consent.ts`, `ports/jobs.ts`, `ports/clock.ts`; `stripe` 23.1.0 in `package.json` (PF-L).
