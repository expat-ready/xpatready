**Where the requirement and `main` correct or narrow the row (the release Lead and Lead B confirm each in the approval):**
* **"Shortfalls listed in red" (AG-6-74 rule 5) changes under P-607 (1).** No case or party goes below zero (FO-7a's check), so the check lists **each open `REVERSAL_SHORTFALL` line in red** with its reversal and case ("CAD 1,500 must move from operating to trust", Sample words), and lists any case balance below zero in red as a guard that should never show. The row leaves rule 5 out; it is a requirement, so it is built.
* **The requirement's line kinds map to FO-7mv's** (rev 13 names): APPLIED = `TRUST_APPLIED`, MISMATCH = `WRONG_ACCOUNT`, FEE_COVER = `CARD_FEE_ON_TRUST`. **Waiting to move** = open `TRUST_APPLIED` and `WRONG_ACCOUNT` items, trust → operating added and operating → trust subtracted, **plus open `REVERSAL_SHORTFALL` items (operating → trust, subtracted)**: without them the month after a bounced cheque shows a false difference, which AG-6-74 §2 exists to prevent. **Fees not yet covered** = open `CARD_FEE_ON_TRUST` items (0 in R1: card into trust is Held, P-567 (3)).
* **In transit is typed in R1** (AG-6-74 rule 7: no payout facts; there is no trust card account, P-567 (3)), default 0; the row says so.
* **"At that date"** (rule 12: "parts computed at that date") **as Lead 61 ruled (ruling 7):** entries count when created on or before the end of the statement date in the firm's time zone (`platform.time_zone`, `api/src/platform/settings.ts`); a move counts when its batch's `transfer_date` is on or before that date; a batch undone on or before that date counts as undone (the undo batch's `transfer_date`, by the same test). The service computes the cut-off instant and the check stores it (`as_of`).
* **The parts are the database's.** The save trigger computes the case balances, waiting and fees itself for `(firm, currency, as_of, statement_date)` and replaces what the caller sent (as FO-7a does with `seq`), computes expected and difference, and refuses a non-zero difference with no note. So the screen, the route and the history can never disagree.
* **Its own area file** (P-600 (a)): `prisma/schema/client-money-checks.prisma`, as the row says; the composite foreign key to `money_payment_settings` needs **one back-relation line** on `MoneyPaymentSettings` in `client-money.prisma` (DEVELOPING.md "a back-relation field the other model needs is the one line you ask its owner for"), which is why part 1 waits for that file's baton.
* **Trust off** (AG-6-59 rule 5): no monthly check while trust is not `YES`, except `NO` with money held or To move not empty (rule 6), when it stays. With trust off and nothing held, the routes answer 404 and no menu names it.
* **Key and routes:** `agent/payment:record` (AG-6-74 §8). Routes under **`/api/front-office/money/`** (Lead B, rev 16.33).
* **The concept:** FO-7s0's row lists (a)-(g) and no monthly check; FO-7m's screen needs one. FO-7s0's brief adds it (its item (h)).
