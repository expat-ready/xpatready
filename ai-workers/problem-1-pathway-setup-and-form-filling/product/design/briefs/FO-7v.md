**Where the requirement and `main` correct or narrow the row (the release Lead and Lead B confirm each in the approval):**
* **It needs schema; the row says API only.** `money_payment` is insert-only but for the cheque's "Cleared" tick (FO-7a); there is no reversed flag and no `PaymentReversal` (**AG-6-75@fb763ef8** §6 `RECORDED → REVERSED`, §7 `PaymentReversal`). And the shortfall needs a to-move kind (P-607 (1)) that FO-7mv's enum (`TRUST_APPLIED | WRONG_ACCOUNT | CARD_FEE_ON_TRUST`) lacks. The row's "(kind MISMATCH)" would reuse `WRONG_ACCOUNT`, whose source is a `WRONG_ACCOUNT` correction of a deposit (FO-7mv rule 3): reusing it would let a reversal open a wrong-account move out of trust. So part 1 adds **`REVERSAL_SHORTFALL`**, always `OPERATING_TO_TRUST`, sourced on the reversal.
* **FO-7mv's rule 2 blocks the reversal as written.** FO-7mv refuses a + correction (money returned to trust from a withdrawal) while that withdrawal's moved amount would exceed what is left ("undo the move first"). A reversal of a deposit whose application was already moved needs exactly that return, so the invoice goes back to due (AG-6-75 rule 3). Part 1 amends FO-7mv's rule, in the database: **a + correction on a moved withdrawal is accepted only when the same transaction holds a reversal whose shortfall covers the moved excess** (moved net of undone batches − what is left on the withdrawal). Nothing else may return moved money. Lead B owns FO-7mv's rules; this is the change that brief's parallel-safety line foresaw ("FO-7v … may add a to-move line kind for a shortfall").
* **What the ledger shows after a shortfall** (P-607 (1), departing from **AG-6-75** rule 4's red "−CAD 1,500" and **AG-6-60@e7a7709d** rule 7): the party's trust balance ends at 0, never below; the invoice is due again; one open **operating → trust** line equal to the shortfall is on To move; one Owner item. The monthly check stays true: expected = balances (0) + waiting (the line is operating → trust, so subtracted) = −shortfall, which is what the bank shows after the bounce (AG-6-74 §3; FO-7m counts the kind).
* **Which withdrawals a deposit "funded"** (AG-6-75 rule 3) is not knowable from a pooled per-party ledger (FO-7a's balance is per party and currency, entries do not name the deposit that paid them). **Question for the tech lead and Lead B:** recommended: **only as much as needed**: if the party's balance covers the deposit, one deposit correction and nothing else is undone; otherwise correct the party's withdrawals in that currency written after the deposit, newest first, until the balance covers it (the last one in part). The plan is one function, so the preview shows exactly this.
* **Refunded money cannot be corrected** (a correction never names a refund, P-603). If the withdrawals after the deposit do not cover it (for example the deposit was refunded to the client before the cheque bounced), the deposit correction is capped at what the balance then allows, and the **uncovered remainder joins the shortfall line**. **Question (same):** accept (recommended: the ledger never goes negative and the monthly check stays true), or refuse the reversal with words naming the refund.
* **A payment with refunds of its own** (an operating payment partly refunded, then found recorded by mistake): AG-6-75 is silent. **Question:** refuse with "This payment has a refund; use a credit note or reverse the refund first" (recommended; a refund already sent money out) or reverse only the unrefunded part.
* **A payment whose extra was kept as credit** (FO-7ts part 2's `KEPT` entry, AG-6-21 rule 3): FO-7ts's credit kinds are `KEPT | APPLIED | REFUNDED`, with no reversal kind. **Question for Lead B:** refuse the reversal with "Part of this payment is kept as credit; refund or apply the credit first" (recommended until a kind exists), or add `REVERSED` in a FO-7ts step.
* **Card payments** are refused with 409 "Use a refund" (AG-6-75 rule 1), at the route and in the database (method `CARD`, or a Stripe payment intent id). A `CARD_TERMINAL` payment (FO-7ts part 1, rev 16.36) is recorded by hand from the firm's own terminal: **question for Lead B:** reversible as a hand-recorded payment (recommended: AG-6-75 rule 1 covers "payments recorded by staff", and the terminal's refund is outside our processor), or refused like `CARD`.
* **The preview is the plan:** one function builds the plan; the preview answers it with a hash; the reverse takes the hash and refuses 409 "Changed since the preview" when the plan built under the lock differs (AG-6-75 §10 "Preview before commit").
* **Who gets the Owner item:** no port lists staff by role (`ports/staff.ts` holds `StaffContext` only). See Contract gap.
* **Keys and routes:** `agent/payment:record` (AG-6-75 §8). Routes under **`/api/front-office/money/`** (Lead B, rev 16.33: "Money routes use `/api/front-office/money/*`"; batch 8's briefs still say `/api/client-money/`), code in `api/src/client-money/reversals/` (P-575 (1)).
* **Event:** the row names `payment.reversed`; AG-6-75 §9 leaves the name to the backend lead: Lead B confirms it in the approval.
* **What `main` holds:** FO-7a's tables and triggers (`money_trust_entry_write`: the party lock first, corrections capped, a return frees the invoice's trust cap again, the balance never below zero; `money_payment`, `money_refund`). Not on `main`: `api/src/client-money/`, FO-7mv's move tables, `money_move_line_write`, `money_to_move`, FO-7ts, PF-12, `ports/notifications.ts`.
