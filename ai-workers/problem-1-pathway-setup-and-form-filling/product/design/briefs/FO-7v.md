**Where the requirement and `main` correct or narrow the row (the release Lead and Lead B confirm each in the approval):**
* **It needs schema; the row says API only.** `money_payment` is insert-only but for the cheque's "Cleared" tick (FO-7a); there is no reversed flag and no `PaymentReversal` (**AG-6-75@fb763ef8** §6 `RECORDED → REVERSED`, §7 `PaymentReversal`). And the shortfall needs a to-move kind (P-607 (1)) that FO-7mv's enum (`TRUST_APPLIED | WRONG_ACCOUNT | CARD_FEE_ON_TRUST`) lacks. The row's "(kind MISMATCH)" would reuse `WRONG_ACCOUNT`, whose source is a `WRONG_ACCOUNT` correction of a deposit (FO-7mv rule 3): reusing it would let a reversal open a wrong-account move out of trust. So part 1 adds **`REVERSAL_SHORTFALL`**, always `OPERATING_TO_TRUST`, sourced on the reversal: FO-7mv's `money_move_line.source_entry_id` becomes nullable, null only for this kind, and a new `source_reversal_id` is required for it (a CHECK: exactly one of the two).
* **FO-7mv's rule 2 blocks the reversal as written.** FO-7mv refuses a + correction (money returned to trust from a withdrawal) while that withdrawal's moved amount would exceed what is left ("undo the move first"). A reversal of a deposit whose application was already moved needs exactly that return, so the invoice goes back to due (AG-6-75 rule 3). Part 1 amends FO-7mv's rule, in the database: **a + correction on a moved withdrawal is accepted only when it names its reversal and the moved excess it creates is counted against that reversal's shortfall.** Each such correction carries `reversal_id`, which the trigger requires to name a reversal written earlier in the same transaction. Its `shortfall_used_minor` is **the database's**: the trigger computes it (the moved amount, net of undone batches, less what is left on the withdrawal after the correction) and replaces any value the writer gives, as FO-7a does with `seq`. The trigger keeps the sum per reversal at or below its `shortfall_minor`. Nothing else may return moved money. Lead B owns FO-7mv's rules; this is the change that brief's parallel-safety line foresaw ("FO-7v … may add a to-move line kind for a shortfall").
* **What the ledger shows after a shortfall** (P-607 (1), departing from **AG-6-75** rule 4's red "−CAD 1,500" and **AG-6-60@e7a7709d** rule 7): the party's trust balance ends at 0, never below; the invoice is due again; one open **operating → trust** line equal to the shortfall is on To move; one Owner item. The monthly check stays true: expected = balances (0) + waiting (the line is operating → trust, so subtracted) = −shortfall, which is what the bank shows after the bounce (AG-6-74 §3; FO-7m counts the kind).
* **Which withdrawals a deposit "funded"** (AG-6-75 rule 3; a pooled per-party ledger does not say): **Lead 61, ruling 4: the newest funded withdrawals first, only as many as needed, all listed in the preview.** If the party's balance covers the deposit, one deposit correction and nothing else is undone; otherwise the party's withdrawals in that currency written after the deposit are corrected newest first until the balance covers it (the last one in part).
* **Refunds stay** (a correction never names a refund, P-603). **An operating payment** partly refunded is reversed for its amount less its own refunds (**Lead 61, ruling 5b**). **A trust deposit: the reversal covers only what is still held** (**ruling 5a**, Lead 61 agreeing Lead B's reading; the ledger is pooled, so later refunds are not subtracted). After the withdrawals are corrected (ruling 4), the deposit correction is the deposit's amount, capped by the party's balance at that moment. **Any part of the deposit already gone, refunded or applied and moved, is the shortfall**: one operating → trust To move line and the Owner item (P-607 (1)). So `shortfall_minor` = the moved excess of the corrected withdrawals + the part of the deposit the balance could not cover. Bank and ledger then agree after a bounce: with one 1,500 deposit and a 600 refund, the balance 900 is corrected, the shortfall is 600, and the bank's −600 is the expected −600.
* **Kept credit is taken back first** (**Lead 61, ruling 5c**; FO-7ts part 2's `REVERSED` credit kind). When part of the payment was kept as credit (`KEPT`, AG-6-21 rule 3), the reversal writes a `REVERSED` entry for it first. If that credit was already applied to an invoice, the application is reversed before it, and that invoice returns to due; the preview lists it.
* **The reason codes map to FO-7mv's `correction_kind`** on the deposit correction: `RETURNED` → `BOUNCED`; `RECORDED_BY_MISTAKE`, `WRONG_CLIENT` and `OTHER` → `ENTRY_ERROR`. **A reversal never writes `WRONG_ACCOUNT`**, which would permit a move out of trust (FO-7mv rule 3). The + corrections on withdrawals carry no kind, as FO-7mv has it.
* **Card payments** are refused with 409 "Use a refund" (AG-6-75 rule 1), at the route and in the database (method `CARD`, or a Stripe payment intent id). A `CARD_TERMINAL` payment (FO-7ts part 1, rev 16.36) is reversible **only with the reason "Recorded by mistake"** (**Lead 61, ruling 6**); any other reason → 409 "Use a refund".
* **The preview is the plan:** one function builds the plan; the preview answers it with a hash; the reverse takes the hash and refuses 409 "Changed since the preview" when the plan built under the lock differs (AG-6-75 §10 "Preview before commit").
* **Who gets the Owner item:** no port lists staff by role (`ports/staff.ts` holds `StaffContext` only). See Contract gap.
* **Keys and routes:** `agent/payment:record` (AG-6-75 §8). Routes under **`/api/front-office/money/`** (Lead B, rev 16.33: "Money routes use `/api/front-office/money/*`"), code in `api/src/client-money/reversals/` (P-575 (1)).
* **Event:** the row names `payment.reversed`; AG-6-75 §9 leaves the name to the backend lead: Lead B confirms it in the approval.
* **What `main` holds:** FO-7a's tables and triggers (`money_trust_entry_write`: the party lock first, corrections capped, a return frees the invoice's trust cap again, the balance never below zero; `money_payment`, `money_refund`). Not on `main`: `api/src/client-money/`, FO-7mv's move tables, `money_move_line_write`, `money_to_move`, FO-7ts, PF-12, `ports/notifications.ts`. FO-7a's `tests/check_fo7a_money_schema.ts` asserts the count of `*_minor` columns; part 1 changes that one assertion (see Touch).
