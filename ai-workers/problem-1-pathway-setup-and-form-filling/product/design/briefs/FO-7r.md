**Where the requirement and `main` correct or narrow the row (the release Lead and Lead B confirm each in the approval):**
* **Keys the row leaves out.** View the summary `agent/invoice:show`, **send a statement `agent/invoice:issue`** (**AG-6-28@58f2a8ef** §8); write-off `agent/invoice:void` (**AG-6-76@575112a7** §8, as the row says); exports `agent/billing:export` (**AG-6-31@c963bccc** §8). All four are in PF-3's seeded catalogue on `main`.
* **A write-off takes the remaining balance, not a typed amount.** AG-6-76 rule 1: "Writes off the remaining balance of one issued invoice, with a reason". The route takes **the invoice party's trust lock and then the invoice row**, in that order, as FO-7a's `money_issued_invoice` does (`pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('money_trust:' || firm || ':' || party || ':' || currency, 0))`, then the row update), so it never deadlocks with a payment, a withdrawal or a credit note on the same invoice. It then computes the **balance due net of payments and trust**: total − credit notes − payments applied to the invoice − trust withdrawals to it net of corrections returning money from them (refunds do not make it due again, AG-6-23 rule 7). The route must compute this itself: FO-7a's trigger (`money_write_off_write`) caps a write-off only at total − credit notes, so on a part-paid invoice it would let the whole unpaid-and-paid amount be written off. The trigger still refuses a second write-off, a draft and a reason outside 1-200 characters.
* **"A later payment counts as extra money"** (AG-6-76 rule 4, AG-6-21 rules 2-3: trust Yes → the extra to trust; trust No → a person chooses "Refund · Keep as credit") **conflicts with FO-7b's row**, which refuses "a payment over the balance … with the balance". **Ruled** (comment 6900938081): the requirement stands; money over the balance is never refused (AG-6-21 rules 2-3, §13 "No refusal of money already received"), FO-7b's refusal is wrong and Lead B corrects that brief, and here a payment after a write-off is extra money (AG-6-76 rule 4): with trust Yes it goes to the case's trust balance (FO-7t's deposit path); with trust No a person chooses "Refund · Keep as credit". "Keep as credit" has no store on `main` (see Contract gap). Not a founder question.
* **Two summary figures have no facts on `main`:** "credit" (AG-6-21's `CreditEntry`; no table, no row) and "unbilled" (AG-6-28 rule 1; nothing links a retainer's fee rows to invoice lines, and AG-6-64's Ready to bill has no row). The summary answers both as `null` with `reason: "NOT_TRACKED"`, never 0, until a row adds them. **Question for the tech lead:** accept, or sequence the rows that add them.
* **"Reminders stop"** (AG-6-76 rule 2, AG-6-12 rule 3): no row builds AG-6-12's payment reminders. Part 1 proves what exists: the derived status is `Written off`, FO-7b's `invoice.overdue` job emits nothing for it, and one exported `reminderEligible(facts)` (AG-6-12 rule 1: issued, balance above zero, not disputed, not written off, not cancelled) answers false, for the reminder row to call.
* **The statement wording's home.** The row says "until the case side's template library (map row 45) holds it"; P-584 (1) moved templates to the platform (PF-19c). The fixed "Your statement" wording stays a committed text source here until PF-19c registers a template kind for it.
