  * **AG-17-35@a0585379** says four things:
    * sign-off is the case's internal review approval (`agent/case:review-approve`, default holders Owner and Case Manager);
    * it records the time, the signer and the waived items with their reasons;
    * an edit afterwards shows "edited since sign-off" and **never withdraws** the sign-off;
    * a file **MAY be submitted without sign-off** after one confirmation.
  * The founder's decisions on `main` differ on three points:
    * **P-155 (1)**: "Before a filing is recorded the representative reviews it, **enforced by the server**".
    * **P-412 F9 (b)**: "only the representative of record approves".
    * **P-412 F9 (a)**: a fact change after approval **withdraws** the approval. `FilingReview.factsHash` (`review.prisma:61-62`: "a later change reopens the review") already does this for forms' facts.
  * A conflict between the requirement and the founder's decision goes to the founder (AGENTS rule 2), so **this row builds the founder's current decisions**:
    * the representative of record signs;
    * a change to the file after sign-off **withdraws** it;
    * the recording rule stays as `main` enforces it.
  * The alternative waits on the founder's answer: AG-17-35's any holder of `agent/case:review-approve`, "edited since sign-off" with the sign-off kept, and submission without sign-off after one confirmation.
  * **Question for the tech lead:** take the three points (who signs, withdrawal on change, submission without sign-off) to the founder. CS-98d's one-confirmation submit has the same P-155 (1) conflict and is raised with it.
* **Where the sign-off lives.**
  * `FilingReview` (`review.prisma:52-70`) requires `licence_number` (so the signer is the representative, P-155) and has **no waived-items column**. Its `factsHash` fingerprints the forms' facts only, and an LMIA file's job, positions, channels and candidates are not form facts.
  * So CS-98s3b **reuses `FilingReview`** for the representative's approval (P-155) and adds **`corp_sign_off`** in `case-corporate.prisma` with one migration:
    * the `filing_review` id;
    * the waived items at that moment (code, reason, who, when; from `ReadinessPass`);
    * `file_hash` (sha256 of the job, positions, channels, candidates and consistency captures);
    * `withdrawn_at` and `withdrawn_reason`;
    * the firm column and composite key as CS-98a.
  * A change that alters `file_hash` sets `withdrawn_at` in the same transaction (P-412 F9 (a)), and the representative re-approves.
* **Set-up checks read the file's pinned tables.** AG-17-33@76d7a279 says "set-up checks re-run against current tables". AG-17-5@b7a5e093 and BR-5 say a file in progress keeps its version, and staff adopt a newer one by hand. This row reads "current" as **the tables of the file's current (pinned) release**, re-run at the quality check. A newer release reaches the file only by adopting it. **Reading (ruled, Lead 62, comment 6901538263 on #1886):** this reading stands; the other reading (re-run against the newest release whatever the pin) is not built.
* **"Ready for sign-off" notifies the file owner, as AG-17-35 says**, and only when someone else caused it (AG17-D46). The file owner is `case_client.file_owner` (contract §5; CS-K1, null until FO-8 sets it). With no file owner, no notification is sent and the PR says so. It is one `NotificationPort.notify` kind, `CORPORATE_READY_FOR_SIGN_OFF` (PF-18np; each consumer adds its kind, Lead B rev 16.31). **Reading (ruled, Lead 62, comment 6901538263 on #1886):** the notice goes to `case_client.file_owner`; Lead 61's "case officer" ruling (comment 6901269470 on #1883) is for the board card's owner only.
