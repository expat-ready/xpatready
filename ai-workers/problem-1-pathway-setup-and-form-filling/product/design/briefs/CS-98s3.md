* **Onboarding action lines do not exist on `main`.** AG-17 §3.3 fills step 2 with "Action lines — e.g. job bank account, authorised representative, signatory; firm-added actions" from "Pack action lines tagged to this step" (SA-01B Extension 2).   * released in `corporate_rules` and listed in the G1 register (Lead 61, comment 6901269297 on #1882);
  * labelled **Sample** (the action names are configuration and are not cited).
  Firm-added actions (AG-17 §3.3) are firm overrides of published content, which X-20 holds ("firm overrides of published pathway content"), so they are not built.
* **Employer documents and opening questions use the release's existing lines.**
  * AG-17 §3.3 step 3 is "Pack document lines, filtered by 'only if'". The release already carries document requirements with predicates, and `caseChecklist` (`engine/runtime/case-checklist.ts:141`) resolves them per case. CS-98s3a adds **Sample** document lines and opening questions to the LMIA combinations' setup data. The employer documents step reads the checklist, with no second document list.
  * **AG-17-20@8133a2d4**: opening questions are asked in Set-up, and a changed answer re-resolves the dependent lines and records on the timeline which items were added or removed. The questionnaire already re-resolves the lines. CS-98s3a adds the timeline line.
* **The portal switch is not built.** **AG-17-21@f3e96745**'s portal part is X-20's ("employer portal views (AG-17-21's portal part)"). Release 1 keeps its staff half: staff complete any item, and the actual doer is recorded.
* **The quality check joins the case's existing readiness view; it is not a second one.**
  * **AG-17-31@76e680e5**: AG-17 contributes LMIA checks to the case's readiness view. **AG-17-33@76d7a279** lists them:
    * advertising rules met;
    * employer documents complete and current;
    * previous refusal addressed where the history answer requires it;
    * set-up checks re-run against the current tables;
    * the processing fee, for information only.
    Employer onboarding is **not** a separate warning.
  * `main`'s readiness is per filing (`engine/runtime/readiness.ts` `filingReadiness`; `api/src/readiness-inputs.ts`). CS-98s3b adds the LMIA checks as inputs for the file's filing, using CS-98b's corporate codes and `ReadinessPass`, so one view shows them all.
  * Job-offer timing is not checked: AG-17-33 moves it "to the expert-check list (§13)".
* **Sign-off and the representative's review differ.** **AG-17-35@a0585379**:
  * sign-off is the case's internal review approval (`agent/case:review-approve`), whose default holders are Owner and Case Manager;
  * it records the time, the signer and the waived items with their reasons;
  * an edit afterwards shows "edited since sign-off" and never withdraws the sign-off;
  * a file may be submitted without sign-off after one confirmation (CS-98d).
  On `main`, the review is the **representative of record's own** (P-155, kept by P-412 F9 (b): "only the representative of record approves"), and P-412 F9 (a) makes a fact change after approval **withdraw** it. The requirement and the founder's decisions conflict on who signs and on withdrawal, which is the founder's to settle (AGENTS rule 2). **Question for the tech lead:** take it to the founder. Recommended: for LMIA files follow AG-17-35 (any holder of `agent/case:review-approve`; a note, not a withdrawal), as the requirement's own rule for corporate files. Until it is answered, CS-98s3b builds the sign-off record and the "edited since sign-off" note, and does not change who may approve.
* **"Ready for sign-off" notifies only the file owner, and only when someone else caused it** (AG-17-35; AG17-D46). That is one `NotificationPort.notify` kind, `CORPORATE_READY_FOR_SIGN_OFF` (PF-18np; each consumer adds its kind, Lead B rev 16.31), sent to the case officer (the card's owner, Lead 61, comment 6901269470 on #1883).
* **No recurring review task** (**AG-17-25@99fcc7a5**). None is built.
