**Ledger balanced (definition, for approval):** per invoice `total = credited + written off + paid + due − extra`, every term ≥ 0, and
`written off + credited ≤ total`; per holder and currency, the trust and credit ledgers' last `balance_after_minor` equals the sum of
their entries and is ≥ 0 (trust may go below zero only by a reversal, P-634). Two currencies are never summed.
