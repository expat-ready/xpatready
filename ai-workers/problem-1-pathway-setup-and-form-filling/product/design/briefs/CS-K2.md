**Where the requirement corrects or narrows the row (the release Lead confirms each in the approval):**
* **The key is `agent/prospect:merge`.** The row and contract §3.3 say `client:merge`, which is not in PF-4's catalogue on `main`. **AG-2-16@ebc21c47** §8 names `agent/prospect:merge` (Owner, Admin, Case Manager), and it is already seeded on `main` for exactly those three roles (`prisma/migrations/20261114015002_pf3_platform_tables/migration.sql:963-965`). * **Which pairs are refused.** The row refuses "two differently named companies". **AG-2-16** rule 8 refuses a **person with a company** (either way) and says nothing against two companies with different names. Rule 4 refuses a self-merge. This brief refuses all three: person with company and self-merge (requirement), and two companies whose display names differ after trimming and case-folding (contract §3.3, P-588 "also"). **Question for the tech lead:** keep the contract's company-name refusal, or downgrade it to a line in `mergeImpact` (the requirement's reading)?
* **Two client numbers.** **AG-2-16** rules 6-7 let staff pick which client number survives; the other goes to the survivor's previous numbers, and the file owner is chosen too. The contract's `mergeClients` takes only `{ survivor, loser, reason }`. This brief adds an optional `keep_number: "survivor" | "loser"` (default `survivor`) and `file_owner: "survivor" | "loser"` (default the survivor's, else the loser's when the survivor has none). The losing number is kept in `case_client.previous_client_numbers` and is never reused.
* **Locks and races.** **AG-2-16** rule 5: two ordered advisory locks on both clients before any step, and a second merge of the same pair finds a record already merged and stops. The row does not say so; this brief does.
* **Undo.** **AG-2-16** §13 specifies no unmerge screen. The undo rests on contract §3.3 alone (P-574 (2)'s "logged and undoable" covers only automatic merges of leads and prospects with no case, so it does not apply here). It keeps §3.3's condition exactly: allowed while no case was opened or closed on the survivor since the merge.
* **What moves, from the code on `main`.** Only `Case.client_id` names a client after CS-K1. If CS-NT1 part 2 has added `case_note.case_client_id`, or PF-29a tasks exist with `host_type = case_client`, those rows also name the loser. Notes are case-side and move here when the column exists.
