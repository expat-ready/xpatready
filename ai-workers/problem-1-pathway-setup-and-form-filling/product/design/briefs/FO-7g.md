* **"Not registered for tax" is its own fact.** **Ruled** (Lead 61, comment 6900938081 on #1878; Lead B agrees, comment 6900912668): "no tax number" is not "not registered". AG-6-4 rule 5 and §7 name `taxRegistered` apart from `taxNumber`, and §7 adds `qstNumber` (Quebec prints both numbers, rule 1). FO-7ts adds `tax_registered` and `qst_number` to `money_payment_settings`; editing them, and the tax number, needs `agent/billing-config:tax` (§8), audited with old and new.
* **Who owns the rates: the requirement stands.** AG-6-4 §3: "Rate table: ExpatReady data (provincial rates), not firm-editable"; §13: "No rate editing by firms". **Ruled** (comment 6900938081): one code path, no setting.
