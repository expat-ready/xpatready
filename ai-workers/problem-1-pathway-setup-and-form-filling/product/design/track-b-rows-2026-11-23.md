**Reach, said plainly:** by default `platform.everyone_sees_all` is true and every starter role holds `agent/case:view.all`, so a firm on defaults leaks nothing. It opens when a firm turns that setting off or edits a role. Today every invoice is drafted without a case, so it is the party half (prospects) that decides. Worst first: a Case Officer or Case Manager (both hold `agent/invoice:show`) reads another colleague's client's invoice, receipt and credit note PDFs by id; Billing / Finance refunds, credits or records money on a client they cannot see. The firm wall is not affected.

**Fail-first test:** in a firm with `platform.everyone_sees_all` off, staff X (Billing / Finance) owns no prospect of client P; P's invoice has one cleared operating payment, part of it an extra. As X: `GET :id/detail` → 404 (passes today); then `GET :id`, the three PDFs, `POST payments/:id/refunds`, `POST payments/:id/extra`, record, issue, send, credit-notes, lines, bill-to, tax-default with that invoiceId.
