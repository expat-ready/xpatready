## 1. Journey order and why

- **J0 Platform foundations** (15 items; 4 W / 10 P / 1 N): Nothing else can run without it; 4 of 15 are Works already, hosting (R1.1) waits on the founder's AWS account.
- **J1 Pathway Configurator set-up** (4 items; 3 W / 1 P / 0 N): Founder's point: a client case can only open on a released pathway. Already largely Works, so it is cheap to finish and then protect.
- **J2 Firm set-up and subscription** (12 items; 0 W / 7 P / 5 N): A firm must exist (owner, plan, staff, roles) before any client work happens in it; also the revenue side.
- **J3 Firm messaging** (7 items; 0 W / 3 P / 4 N): Every later journey sends mail (confirmations, reminders, signing links); consent gate and layout must exist first.
- **J4 Enquiry and lead handling** (16 items; 0 W / 11 P / 5 N): The client's first touch; creates the person record every later step points at.
- **J5 Booking and consultation** (8 items; 0 W / 5 P / 3 N): Booking needs a person (J4) and sends invites (J3).
- **J6 Retainer and signature** (5 items; 0 W / 4 P / 1 N): A retainer needs a person and usually a consultation; signature is the event that opens the case and the deposit.
- **J7 Case opened on a release and run** (11 items; 3 W / 4 P / 4 N): The case needs a release (J1), a person (J4), a signed retainer (J6); it is the product's centre of gravity.
- **J8 Documents** (8 items; 1 W / 1 P / 6 N): Documents hang off a case's checklist (J7) and need storage from J0.
- **J9 Client money** (7 items; 0 W / 3 P / 4 N): Invoices follow signature (J6) and attach to a case (J7); trust rules need audit (J0).
- **J10 Trust, privacy and compliance** (8 items; 0 W / 3 P / 5 N): Cross-cutting duties that read from every other journey; build last, but audit parts (R1.8) sit in J0.

Order is Lead to confirm where it differs from the founder's sketch (J0, J1, firm set-up, client case, money, other): messaging (J3) and the client case are split into smaller journeys so each fits the 6-items-in-flight limit (P-724).
