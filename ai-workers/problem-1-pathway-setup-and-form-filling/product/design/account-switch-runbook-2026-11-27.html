Four causes, largest first

  • 39% gone before any work started (unexplained by our records). There are two possible causes. (a) The account was not fresh: its week runs from 21 Nov 20:29 to 28 Nov 20:29, so any use earlier that week counts. (b) Hypothesis: a session on this Mac kept running on the new sign-in. A Mac session's timers and event watch keep waking it, and every wake re-sends its whole conversation. Nothing measured the meter at the moment of the switch, so we cannot tell which.
  • The restart wave: 13% in 90 minutes. 20 sessions started at once, against the standing rule of at most 12 ranked by nearness to Works (A404). Each new session's first read (instructions, STATE, rules, PR history) is its most expensive moment. The 22 Nov lesson was the same: about 20 sessions in an hour burned 12%.
  • Lead churn. 7 Leads in 24 hours. Each new Lead's start read is about 100–120k tokens, and each check re-sends the Lead's growing conversation.
  • Noise wakes. Tonight the watch woke the Lead 6 times on false "dequeued" alarms (Mergify editing its own status comment). This is fixed as of 05:5x.
