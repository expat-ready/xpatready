# Challenge: consultation gate and the Code (note of 24 Nov 2026)

Independent challenger, 24 Nov 2026. Read against: the note; `gate.ts`; `booking/service.ts` 440-470; `consult/service.ts` 120-160; AG-04 v2.1 AG-4-15 (l. 733-760) and AG-4-44 (l. 1409-1445); the 13 Nov Code reading (`t8-regulator.md` on `origin/lead-b-front-office`); DECISIONS P-564; `platform/identity/routes.ts` and `service.ts` (licence records). No external site reached. Every legal point: **Unconfirmed: no counsel**.

## A. Would change the recommendation

**A1. "A licence record" is self-declared, so the licensee-only reason is not a control.** `platform/identity/routes.ts:73-75`: "A licence record: a person's own without a key"; `identity/service.ts:356-364` upserts free text (`representativeType`, `licensingBody`, `licenceNumber`) with no check, no expiry, no approval. Any receptionist can type a licence record and then give the reason. The `CAN_SIGN` SQL (`gate.ts:55`) only tests that a row exists.
*Fix:* the reason may be given only by (a) the booking's host with a licence record whose `licensingBody` is the College, or (b) a representative of record (the `DUTY_KEYS.represent` rule already in `CAN_SIGN`). A licence record saved by its own holder in the last N days, or never confirmed by an Owner, does not count. Put the self-declaration gap in the note as a known risk.

**A2. Two of the four reasons are not "a signed agreement", and one records a breach.** The Code wants a *written* agreement *before* the initial consultation and keeps "a copy of the signed consultation agreement" (s. 23(1), 23(3), t8 l. 15, 21); the Guide wants it "dated and signed by the client and the licensee" (t8 l. 22).
* "Client signed on paper, copy to upload" is not an exception: it is the `SIGNED_OFFLINE` status. As a *reason* it lets the meeting go ahead with no copy ever uploaded. *Fix:* make it the paper route itself (upload now, or the booking shows "Copy owed" on the firm list until uploaded).
* "Client agreed in writing by email" is neither dated-and-signed by both nor proof that the s. 23(2) contents (fee, College role, scope) were in front of the client. The note's own Q2 cites the Ontario ECA only for electronic *signatures*, not for an email "OK". *Fix:* drop it, or require the email to be attached and the agreement text to be the firm's template; call it "Client accepted by email (copy attached)", never "signed".
* "Urgent, agreement to follow" is, on the note's own facts ("Nothing found in the Code allows urgency", l. 18), a pre-written admission that s. 23(1) was not met. Offering it as a menu choice is the product suggesting the breach. *Fix:* remove it; leave "Other: <text>" (mandatory, at least 20 characters).
* The firm list "Consultations without a signed agreement" is then a breach register the College can ask for (s. 24(3)(w) in t8 l. 49; records under s. 37, t8 l. 100). That may be right (truthful records), but the note sells the audit only as risk reduction. *Fix:* say both sides plainly in the DECISIONS row; the founder decides.

**A3. Who rules is wrong.** AGENTS.md rule 2: a conflict between the team's requirement (AG-4-15 r4 "never blocks anything", Rule 7 "Warn, never block", AG-04 l. 153) and a founder decision (R1.104 "Consultation agreement signed before the first consult", `release1-end-to-end-review-2026-11-18.md:91`) goes to the founder. The note asks Lead 26. And the existing override was never recorded: `gate.ts:11` says "DECISIONS row owed"; `grep QA-772 DECISIONS.md` finds nothing. *Fix:* address Q1 to the founder through the Lead (grilling, one question); write the owed row for Lead 22's ruling first, then the new row that changes it.

**A4. Money at Attended is treated only as a harm of the block, never as a risk of the override.** AG-4-44 r8: the consult invoice is made at Attended; r1 example: "Attended (bill CAD 150 from trust)". s. 23(2)(c) puts the fee *in the consultation agreement* (t8 l. 18), so an unsigned consult means a fee the client never agreed in writing; billing it from trust touches s. 32(f)/33(1) (t8 l. 94-95). The note's (b) "blocks billing" (l. 51) argues for billing it. *Fix:* on the override path Attended records attendance but holds the invoice: "Bill now · Bill when signed" (default: when signed), and the firm list shows unbilled held consults. One more test per payment timing (When booking, After the consult) plus "Undo Attended" (AG-4-39 r10).

## B. Weakens the argument

**B1. "The software cannot stop a meeting" is overstated.** The product hands the client the meeting: `booking/service.ts:930` returns `joinLink` on the client's booking page and `:937` puts `type.meetingLink` in the calendar invite. It cannot stop a phone call, but it can (a) warn the host before the meeting ("agreement unsigned, meeting in 2 h: send / sign on paper / reschedule"), and (b) show the client "sign first" beside the join link. The real benefit of a block is *before* the meeting (deterrence, the firm visibly following its regulator, the product not seen to wave breaches through); a block *after* the meeting has only the costs the note lists. *Fix:* keep the "no hard 409 at Attended" conclusion but add the pre-meeting warning and the client-side "sign first" line as part of Q1, so the pressure moves earlier instead of disappearing.

**B2. Misattributed or over-read sources.**
* l. 22 "Rule 7: 'Warn, never block'" reads as AG-4-15 rule 7; AG-4-15 r7 is about template edits (l. 751). Rule 7 is the AG-04 decision table row (l. 153). Cite it so.
* The same Rule 7 says "no regulator or statute names in product text". The proposed setting wording "our consultants are not College licensees" (l. 56) breaks it. *Fix:* "Our consultants are not bound by a consultation-agreement rule: warn only", or ask for a DECISIONS row.
* l. 7 "Lead 25 read the official text again on 24 Nov (the brief for this lane)": no file in the repository holds it, and Q3 (l. 117) then asks for s. 23 to be read again. Either cite the brief by path or downgrade every "Lead 25" fact to unverified (AGENTS.md rule 10).
* l. 14 "This applies even when the consultation is free" is an inference from the pro bono clause, not a quote; label it.
* l. 31 Microsoft Bookings' quote is about required *questions*, not agreements; l. 42 "No product documents a hard block" is absence of documentation in 9 tools, none of which is bound by a pre-consultation written-agreement rule. The market shows usual practice, not what a regulated licensee may do. Say so.
* t8 l. 22 notes "digital signature allowed (guide only)" for the consultation agreement; the note says no e-signature rule was found (l. 7). Reconcile.

**B3. The requirement asks for acceptance, the code asks for two signatures; the note does not say so.** AG-4-15 l. 735 "accepts it", Non-goals l. 757 "No e-signature of the agreement"; the gate holds `CLIENT_SIGNED` until the firm signs (`gate.ts:24`). Part of today's friction is the firm's counter-signature, not the client. *Fix:* state the deviation; consider whether the licensee's countersign may follow the meeting (client signed before = s. 23(1) met in substance) and say whether that clears most cases without any reason at all.

**B4. "Warn only" firm setting is open to abuse.** Off by default, but any Owner turns it on and the gate disappears for everyone, including College licensees. The data to detect it exists (`licensingBody`), which the note dismisses as "per-person detection" (l. 56). *Fix:* not offered while any active staff has a College licence record; switching it on is audited and shown on the firm list; or decide per booking from the host's `licensingBody`.

**B5. Non-licensee host and a different person answering.** `recordAttendance` lets the host or anyone with `agent/booking:edit` answer (`booking/service.ts:448`). The note does not say whose reason it is when an unlicensed host holds the consult (possible unauthorised advice, a matter outside s. 23) or when an Admin answers for the host. *Fix:* the reason names the responsible licensee (host, else a representative of record), the person who clicked is audited separately; an unlicensed host on a type that needs the agreement shows a warning at booking time.

## Verdicts

* **Q1: agree with changes.** Removing the hard 409 at Attended and on the consult record is sound. Not as written: (A1) a real licensee check, not a self-typed record; (A2) cut "Urgent" and "agreed by email", make paper a status with an owed copy; (A4) hold the invoice by default; (B1) add the pre-meeting warning; (B4) guard "Warn only"; (A3) the founder rules and the owed DECISIONS row is written first.
* **Q2: agree with changes.** Keep P-564, Owner-only switch-off, audit, record per signature. Fix the warning wording (C3), check the migration claim, and drop the market-strictness argument.
* **Question 3 (read s. 23 and s. 26 on the Mac first): agree**, and add: the Guide's current status, and whether the College's 2026 regulations changed s. 23.
