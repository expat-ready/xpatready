3. **Reviews skip silently** when the US$10 balance runs out ([C7]); a rollout that "runs" may review nothing. Top-up first, and read the average cost after 20 Manual reviews.
4. **The private split can break required checks**: fork and Dependabot PRs get no secrets ([C19]), so a required check that reads the private repository fails them; and evidence has two destinations in two pages (CI artifacts vs private repo). Records first, tools last, evidence after the destination is ruled.
