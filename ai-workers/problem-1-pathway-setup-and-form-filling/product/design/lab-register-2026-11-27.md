## Gates

* **Proven:** the Done-when check was run on main after the merge and passed; the evidence (command and output, run link, or screenshot path) is in Status.
* A lane marks its own rows Built; Proven needs the evidence line. DONE rows already merged before this register say Built or Proven as checked on 27 Nov.

Records first, tools last, evidence after the destination is ruled.
