# Founder's board: end-to-end audit (A115, 16 Nov)

Ask (founder, ~11:00 IST 16 Nov, through Lead 65):
- Colourful stages, the same everywhere.
- One number and one word for one thing.
- Fewer, human tooltips.
- No reds he cannot act on.
- His steps as a status he can change.
- One cohesive board.

Base: board v12 (P-636 first screen, PR #1937). Rules: FORMULAS.md is the single source for every number; P-636 words; board data only from scripts (P-591).

## The rule set this audit applies
1. **Two views.**
   - **Yours:** the six cards and four tabs.
   - **For the tech lead:** one collapsed section with the machine room.
   - A red or a number lands in your view only if you can act on it or it tells you where Release 1 stands.
2. **One stage per capability, four colours, used everywhere:**

   | Stage | Colour | Meaning |
   |---|---|---|
   | Planned | grey-blue | not started |
   | In progress | amber | someone is on it, or qa found part of it working |
   | Built | violet | all its code merged |
   | Proven | green | qa tested it like a person and it passed |

   Stage per capability, first rule that applies:
   - **Proven:** qa's verdict Works in qa/CAPABILITIES.md on main.
   - **Built:** every BACKLOG part merged, or qa passed it.
   - **In progress:** an open pull request or a started lane names it, or qa reads it Partial.
   - **Planned:** everything else.

   One script function computes it (`first.release.stage`), so every card, bar, list and team row shows the same stage and the same colour.
3. **One number, one word.**
   - Your view reads every count from `first` (one function in the script; FORMULAS.md › First screen).
   - The checklist's hand-kept status column appears only in the tech lead's section. It is labelled "checklist mark (hand-kept, can lag qa)" and is never called Proven there.
4. **Tooltips.**
   - **In your view:** one per card (its source and time) plus Health checks. Nothing else gets an ⓘ.
   - **Panels:** one plain sentence, what to do, and what is behind it. The method (window, sample, class) sits under a closed "How it is counted".
   - **The tech lead's section:** keeps its icons, because that is where the method is needed.
5. **Your steps.**
   - One status per step, changeable at any time: To do · Done · Not now (remind me on a date) · Talk to the Lead.
   - Each step shows one line on why it matters and what it unblocks, plus the last change with its time.
   - Saved in the page's database (`board/answers`), which the Lead's check reads.

## What the Lead's check reads (unchanged place, extended shape)
- **`board/refresh`:** {state: requested | done, at, data_at, done_at}.
- **`board/answers`:** {<key>: {status: todo | done | not_now | talk, remind_on (YYYY-MM-DD, for not_now), at, what, history: [{status, at}] (last 10)}}. The key is `risk-<id>` or `ask-<id>`.
  - done: close the step in risks.json (or STATE for an ask).
  - not_now: ask again on remind_on.
  - talk: raise it with the founder at the next check.
  - An old {answer: done | not_today} entry still reads as done or not_now.

## Independent review (Opus at high effort, 16 Nov) and what changed
The rule P-608 (2) runs reviewers on Opus at high effort; the hook refused a Fable launch. The first verdict was "Changes needed". Every Major and Minor was fixed:
- **Wrong name, wrong word.** Health check 6 said "Major bugs on Proven capabilities", but its bugs sit on proof-of-concept capabilities, not on the 4 Proven Release 1 rows.
  - Fix: the check is renamed "…on proof-of-concept capabilities qa passed".
  - Fix: the page no longer rewrites "Works" to "Proven" anywhere.
- **Raw data on the page.** The Stuck card printed raw bug records.
  - Fix: one plain line each ("QA-445 (Major) on C6.2"); the lists sit behind the tap.
- **A number from another source.** The choke showed "86 specified, not started" (backlog rows).
  - Fix: it shows the stage bar's Planned count.
  - Fix: its stages have plain names: Being built, Being reviewed, Automatic checks, Waiting to merge.
- **A count that did not follow his status.** The Needs you count stayed at 3 after a step was set to Done.
  - Fix: the count follows his statuses.
- **Steps without a status.** His later steps had no dropdown.
  - Fix: every step of his has one.
  - Fix: Launch readiness shows his status on each step he owns.
  - Fix: Not now without a date defaults to a week ahead.
- **Machine detail and unknown words in his view.**
  - Fix: Health shows the light, what to do and who; the detail is one tap away.
  - Fix: Who is doing what uses the same idle rule as Stuck, has no red pill, and folds tool-only lanes into one line.
  - Fix: the scope words are gone; the Today card carries no source note.
- **Contrast.** Light-mode chip text failed AA.
  - Fix: it is darker now.
- **The audit's own numbers.**
  - Fix: Built is counted once per stage, and the icon count is corrected (above).

Still in his view on purpose: the risk texts in Launch readiness are the hand-kept risks.json words (some technical, such as OWASP ASVS); rewriting them is the Lead's records work.
