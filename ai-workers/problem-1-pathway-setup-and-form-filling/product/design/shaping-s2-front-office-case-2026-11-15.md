## 3. Map row 43: calendar sync with Google or Microsoft 365 (new rows FO-3s1 to FO-3s5)

**What the requirement really asks (AG-04 v2.1, AG-16 v1.24, re-checked).** Each staff member connects **their own** Google or Microsoft calendar in My account → Calendar (AG-4-40, -56). It needs no permission key and works on every plan (AG-4-59, AG-16-71); the firm-level calendar is withdrawn (AG-16-13, AG16-18).
* **Read:** busy times on the calendars the person ticks block that person's slots. They are read on each slot request, cached at most 60 s, and re-read fresh when a booking is written. Only start and end are used: no titles, and nothing is stored (AG-4-41 rules 1-3, 7, 10; BR-2).
* **Write:** bookings go to the one calendar the person picks: created when scheduled, updated on reschedule or host change, deleted on cancel. This runs as a job after commit; a failed call never rolls back a booking (AG-4-42; BR-14). The event's text is set by AG04-D12: the type, the booker's name, the times, the join link or address, and an "Open in Expat Ready" link. Never answers, phone or notes.
* **Not two-way:** "No sync of edits made in the external calendar back to the booking" (AG-4-42, non-goal 13). The map's word "two-way" means read plus write.
* **Broken:** a revoked or expired token marks the connection Failed. The person gets one email and one home item "Calendar disconnected — Reconnect". While it is broken, slots use Expat Ready's hours only and nothing is written. On reconnect, future bookings are written again (AG-4-43; AG-16-16).
* **Staff booking over a busy time:** a warning the staff member may override. A public booker is hard-blocked (AG-4-26/27).
* **Meet and Teams links** are made on the same calendar event (AG-4-29; P-585 (4) "also brings Meet and Teams links to FO-3v").
